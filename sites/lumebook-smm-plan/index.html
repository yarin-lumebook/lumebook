Positioning

Our wedge: the developmental moment

הספר שעוזר לילד שלכם לעבור את הרגע הגדול – סיפור חברתי שבו הוא הגיבור.

MeBook, our closest competitor, matches us on features: a free preview, a fast digital copy, social stories and a religious line. So we don't compete on features. We own the idea that the book is a tool for a specific moment in a child's development, and we prove it in content.

  • One milestone a month across every channel. Every post that month points to the matching book.
  • A 20-second pinned explainer on why a story about the child helps: they rehearse the situation safely before it happens.
  • One professional on the record each month: an OT, speech therapist, child psychologist or gan teacher who reads the story on camera.
  • Parent proof about the moment, not the book. "What was hard before, and what changed after the story?"
  • Language limits: say "helps", "supports", "a gentle way to". Never "treats", "fixes" or "proven".
Guidelines I define

Marketing guidelines

If a post, story, caption or creator brief breaks one of these, it doesn't go out.

Voice

Talk at the child's eye level, warm and practical, in spoken Hebrew with short sentences. Talk to the parent about their child.

Sayספר שעוזר לדבר על האח החדש
Neverספר שפותר קנאת אחים
Say"Helps", "a gentle way to"
NeverGuilt, clinical promises, naming competitors

Visuals

  • Show a real child's face within the first 2 seconds.
  • Show the real book in real hands. Label any AI-generated scene.
  • Burn captions into every video.
  • Keep one signature so we aren't confused with MeBook: the same end card (לומיבוק + תצוגה מקדימה בחינם), page-flip transition and sound on every reel.

Children's safety and consent

  • Repost only with the parent's written consent in DM, and screenshot it.
  • First names only. No gan or school, location tags or uniforms.
  • Using a child in an ad needs separate consent.
  • Take content down within 24 hours of a request, no questions asked.
  • Never post bath, undressed or medical footage.

Claims and disclosure

  • A creator paid with a free book must label the post as advertising, under Israel's Consumer Protection Authority guidance (ynet).
  • Don't use "30,000+ children" or "4.9 on Google" until the founders confirm them.
  • Check prices on the live site on the day you post.
  • Every post ends with one CTA: לתצוגה מקדימה בחינם – קישור בביו or כתבו "ספר" ואשלח קישור.
