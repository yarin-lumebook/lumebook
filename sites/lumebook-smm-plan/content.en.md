## Differentiation: the developmental angle

- All content connects to a developmental moment in the child's life and the book that fits it.
- One developmental theme per month, across all channels.
- One explainer video about the method, pinned to the profile.
- One professional per month presents the story on camera.
- Parent testimonials focus on the child's moment, not the book.
- Language: "helps", "supports". Never "cures", "solves" or "proven".

## Marketing guidelines

A post, story, caption or creator brief that doesn't meet the guidelines doesn't go up.

### Voice
- At the child's eye level, warm and practical.
- Spoken Hebrew, short sentences.
- We talk with the parent about the child, not at the parent.
- No guilt, no therapeutic promises, no mentioning competitors.

### Visuals
- A real child's face in the first two seconds.
- The real book in real hands. AI scenes are labelled.
- Burned-in captions on every video.
- A fixed signature at the end of every reel: the same end card, transition and sound.
- The name "Lumebook" (לומיבוק) appears in full in all content.

### Children's safety and consent
- Share only with the parent's written consent, saved as a screenshot.
- First name only. No gan, school, location or uniforms.
- Using a child in an ad requires separate consent.
- Removal within 24 hours of any request.
- Never bath, undressed or medical moments.

### Claims and disclosure
- A creator who received a book marks the content as advertising, per the Consumer Protection Authority guidelines ([ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/SyzEMeG0w)).
- Customer-count and rating figures only after Yossi approves them.
- One age range everywhere.
- Prices are checked on the site on the day of posting.

## Content system

| Content pillar | Share | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Developmental moment → book | 40% | The developmental moment, what's hard for the child, and the book as a tool for that moment |
| Book reveal | 30% | The moment the child sees themselves in the book |
| Proof & process | 20% | How a book is made, what a printed book looks like, real reviews |
| Gifts & seasonal | 10% | Birthdays, grandparents, holidays |

### Weekly cadence
- 4 reels on Instagram, also posted to TikTok and Facebook.
- Stories 5 days a week.
- One carousel.
- 3 pinned posts and up-to-date highlights.

### Call to action
- One call to action in all content: free preview.
- Every link carries a tracking tag.
- A comment asking for the link gets the link by DM.

## Creator program (paid in books)

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Who | Parent creators in Israel, 2k–50k followers, kids in the target age range, real engagement. Second tier: child-development professionals and gan teachers |
| What they get | A free personalised book. A commission per order on a personal code, if Yossi approves |
| What we ask | One reel and 2–3 stories with a link, an advertising label, permission to share. No obligation to post |
| Volume | 40 outreaches a week. Every creator is logged in the sheet: date, code, post link, clicks, orders |
| Continue or stop | After 30 days: 2 or more orders makes the creator an ambassador. No content and no orders, we stop |

## User content
- Ask every buyer to film the first moment with the book and tag us.
- One-time outreach to past customers: videos and reviews.
- The customer highlight is updated every week.
- Every share requires consent.

## Community
- Reply to every comment and message on the same working day.
- Parent groups on Facebook: relevant answers, no unrequested links.
- A joint giveaway with a creator once a month.
- A question that repeats 3 times in messages becomes content.
