## Differentiation: the developmental angle

- The content is connected to a developmental moment in the child's life
- Explainer videos about child development
- Collaboration with professionals from the field
- Parent testimonials

## Marketing guidelines

### Voice
- At eye level, warm and meaningful
- Talking about the child, not at the parent
- No therapeutic promises

### Visuals
- Real faces of children
- A physical book
- Captions are mandatory (they raise retention)
- A sound for the brand (strengthens people's association with the brand)

### Child safety and consent
- Share only with the parent's written consent, saved as a screenshot
- Use first names only
- No bath or undressed footage

## Creator program (paid in books)

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Who | Creators: parents in Israel, 2k–50k followers, kids aged 1–10. Secondary options: professionals from the field and gan teachers |
| What they get | A free personalised book. A commission per order on a personal code (with Yossi's approval) |
| What we ask | One reel and 2–3 stories with a link, plus permission to share. No obligation to post them back |
| Anecdote | Need to create a sign-up form that becomes an organised Excel sheet |
| Continuation | Option to become an "ambassador" |
