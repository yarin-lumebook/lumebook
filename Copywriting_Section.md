Copywriting Reproduce great copy by hand: find one Hebrew Reel/carousel
in this ICP's world that stopped your own scroll, and transcribe its
script by hand, then annotate --- where's the stop, the turn, the
payoff, the CTA? Log the pattern (never the content). You're building a
pattern library in your nervous system, not a copy-paste folder.
Retention rewrite Take one of her existing or draft scripts. Rewrite it
to hit the gate structure and the Hebrew word count (30s = 70--80
words). Cut it to one idea. Put both versions side by side. This is
where you learn the single hardest discipline in the format:
subtraction. Language mining Read the last week of DMs, comments, and
story-question replies. Copy the exact sentences women used about their
pain --- verbatim, in their Hebrew, not yours. These become hooks. The
sentence a woman types at 1am beats anything you'll invent. 4. Self

1.  The cringe test (before publishing). Read the script aloud to her.
    If she won't say it in her own mouth, it's dead.
2.  The Story A/B. Before committing a hook to a full Reel, post two
    hook variants and check skip rate.
3.  The retention graph - After posting, the curve tells you exactly
    where the hook or the turn failed.
4.  Psychology: Hook lever The mechanism (why attention moves)
    Self-relevance ("את אם 3-ב מתעוררת ---") The self-reference effect:
    information encoded against the self is attended to and remembered
    better than any other kind --- robust across 129 studies. Curiosity
    gap ("שכולן הסימן מפספסות") Information-gap theory: a salient gap
    between what I know and want to know creates a felt deprivation that
    seeking resolves. Open loop / "watch to the end" The Zeigarnik
    effect --- but be careful: the classic "unfinished tasks are
    remembered \~90% better" is contested; a 2025 meta-analysis found no
    reliable memory advantage, only a tendency to resume. Use open loops
    to hold attention within a piece, don't overclaim. Name the feeling
    first ("לא ,מותשת את מפונקת") Affect labeling: putting a feeling
    into words down-regulates the amygdala and produces relief --- so
    naming her exact feeling is the value, delivered in the first line.
    Contrarian / permission ("לא לך מותר להתגעגע") High-arousal emotion
    (awe, anger, anxiety, moral indignation) drives sharing; low-arousal
    sadness does not. A permission line that sparks
    indignation-on-her-behalf gets sent; a merely sad one doesn't.
