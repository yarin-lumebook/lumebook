# Who can see the tagged orders today, and who can't

## Do this now
Understand why the tagged orders box says "Not available yet", and who is the only person who can change that.

## The lesson
On the Numbers tab, box 4 is waiting. Tagged orders decide your pay, and you can't see them. Where are they?

**In the store's database.** When a book is created, the code sends the visitor's tags with it (`research/creator-content-conversion/report.md`, line 514). The measurement plan says this is the source of truth for where a visitor came from (`research/cro-crm/measurement.md`, section 2). So the data probably exists. Only someone who can pull from the database can count it, and in the store Yossi built, that is Yossi.

**In the store admin: not by link.** The marketing tab says the site does not record which link brought an order, and the reliability panel warns that the share of orders with a tag is low (`docs/lumebook-sales-data-decisions-2026-10-12.md`, section 7 and line 93). So the store admin knows that some orders carry tags, but does not show which ones.

**In GA4: not this.** GA4 only sees visitors who accepted cookies, and it files many ad clicks under "Organic Social" (`research/cro-crm/numbers-2026-10-25.md`, section 5). Lesson L23 covers this. Your pay is not read from there.

**In Meta: a different count.** Meta counts purchases it credits to itself. The measurement plan says to keep three counts separate and never add them up: orders in the store, orders credited to creators, and purchases the platforms report (`measurement.md`, section 2).

**On your side: nothing, by link.** You see the comments and the DMs you sent. Not the orders.

**The cookie gate.** The code saves tags only for visitors who accepted analytics cookies (`report.md`, line 515). Today consent is on by default, and the banner has only an accept button (`research/cro-crm/audit.md`, A19), so almost everyone gets through. If the store switches to opt-in consent after a legal review, tagged orders can drop all at once, with no change at all in your work.

**The conclusion:** the number exists in one place, and only Yossi can get it out. Until then, "Not available yet". The next lesson writes the request.

## Example
On Sunday, box 4: "Not available yet". The sentence at the bottom of the tab: "Tagged orders are not available to me. They are stored in the store's database, and I asked Yossi for a weekly export." There is no guess here, and the gap is named plainly.

## Common mistake
"The store doesn't record where a buyer came from at all", because that is what the store admin says. The code does send the tag with every book created. What is missing is a view. So the request to Yossi is "show me the orders with my tag", not "start recording".

## What the numbers can't prove
Even when Yossi sends the tagged orders, the number will be an "at least": someone who closed the tab or declined cookies may have come from you and not been counted.

## Terms
- **attribution**: assigning an order to the source that brought it. Here, by the tags saved with the order.
- **store admin**: the store's internal screens, where Yossi sees sales, ads and messages.
- **cookies**: the visitor's consent to tracking on the site. Without it, the code saves no tags.
