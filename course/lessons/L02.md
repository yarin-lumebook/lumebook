# No tag, no pay: choose the values, then make a tagged link

## Do this now
Set your tag values once in the link maker, and from now on make every link you send in a DM with it.

## The lesson
The first time you open the link maker, it asks about values. This isn't a technical question. It's your signature.

**The pay rule:** only orders that carry your tag count toward your 25%. A sale through a creator's personal link doesn't count for you, and neither does a sale that came with no tag at all. So Yossi needs to know exactly which values mean "Yarin".

**The five tags** (`research/cro-crm/measurement.md`, section 4):

- `utm_source`, which platform: `instagram` or `facebook`.
- `utm_medium`, what kind of traffic: `social`.
- `utm_campaign`, which topic: `car-` followed by the topic, for example `car-pacifier-weaning`.
- `utm_content`, where and when: `dm-20261102`, `story-20261102` or `bio`.
- `utm_term`, only for a test: empty when there is no test.

**Why `social`.** The Facebook post already sends a link with `utm_medium=social` (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/story-format.md`, section 8), and the link Instagram adds in the bio also comes with `social` (`research/cro-crm/audit.md`, step 0). That way everything that leaves your account speaks the same language.

**The important rule: keep them separate.** The same table has values that aren't yours: `creator` for creators, `paid-social` for ads, `community` for parent groups, `crm` for the store's own messages. If one of your DMs goes out with `creator`, or an ad goes out with `social`, Yossi can't tell them apart, and your sales get mixed up with other people's. Yossi tags the ads, so in your message to Yossi (lesson L06), ask that ads don't get `social`.

Three more rules from the same section: lowercase letters, a hyphen instead of a space, and no personal details in the link.

**How to use the link maker:**
1. Choose the post's topic. The link maker knows which book page it leads to (`research/carousel-audit/data/books.json`). For a post with no book, choose the store home page.
2. Choose where the link goes out: DM, story or bio. The date goes in by itself.
3. Press "Copy and log", then paste.

**How to check a link:** open it on your phone. Does the right page open? Does the address bar still show `?utm_` at the end? If Instagram added something else, like `fbclid`, that's fine.

And note: links from the old link maker, before November, put the place (a DM or a story) in `utm_medium`. From November that moves to `utm_content` (`measurement.md`, section 4). An old link that looks different isn't a mistake.

## Example
A parent wrote `מוצץ` ("pacifier") under the pacifier carousel on 2 Nov 2026. In the link maker: topic `pacifier-weaning`, place "DM". The result:

`https://lumebook.com/IL/he/books/41?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=car-pacifier-weaning&utm_content=dm-20261102`

Opened on the phone: "Bye Bye Pacifier" (`ביי ביי מוצץ`) opens, and the end of the address has `utm_`. You can send it.

## Common mistake
"If the sale happened, it's credited to you." No. A parent who saw the carousel, typed lumebook.com themselves and bought brought in a real order that doesn't count for you, because it has no tag. What counts is only what came through your link.

## What the numbers can't prove
An order with your tag shows that the parent came through your link, not that the post is what convinced them.

## Terms
- **Source (utm_source)**: the platform the visitor came from, for example `instagram`.
- **Medium (utm_medium)**: the kind of traffic. `social` is yours, `creator` is for creators, `paid-social` is for ads.
- **Campaign (utm_campaign)**: the topic or the plan, for example `car-pacifier-weaning`.

## Quiz

- [x] `utm_medium`
- [ ] `utm_source`
- [ ] `utm_content`
> `utm_source` only says which platform. The difference between `social`, `creator` and `paid-social` sits in `utm_medium`.

### Question 2

- [x] No, because it has no tag
- [ ] Yes, because they came from Instagram
- [ ] Yes, if they also wrote a comment word
> Only orders with your tag count. Without a tagged link the store doesn't know they came from you.
