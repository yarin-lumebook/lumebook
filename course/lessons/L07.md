# The map: every step on the way, whose it is, and on which screen

## Do this now
Before a Carousel task, know which step of the chain you are at, what is yours and what is Claude's, and whether you need to be at the Mac.

## The lesson
A Carousel task looks like one task. In practice it is one link in a chain of eleven steps. Next to each step: whose it is, and where it happens.

**Before posting** (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/README.md`):
1. **The monthly batch: topics, research and options for every posting day.** Claude's. Runs in the cloud on the morning of the last day of the month. The first run, 31 Oct 2026, covers November.
2. **Read, edit, grade 1 to 5 with a reason, and choose `+ להפקה` ("+ To production").** Yours. In Carousel Machine, on the Mac only.
3. **`שליחת הדירוגים לענן` ("Send the grades to the cloud"),** so the next batch reads them. Yours. In Carousel Machine.
4. **The pictures.** Claude's: the slide pictures are made for free in ChatGPT in your Chrome, and the book pictures for the last slide in fal (`story-format.md`, section 7).
5. **Approve the picture, or ask for a fix with a note** (up to twice). Yours. In Carousel Machine.
6. **Slides, a text-direction check, ZIP and a copy to Drive.** Automatic.
7. **The preview screen (`איך זה ייראה`, "How it will look"), and the Postiz block: date, time, `לתזמן` ("Schedule") or `טיוטה בלבד` ("Draft only").** Yours. In Carousel Machine. The time starts at 06:00 (`shared/facts.json`, `default_hour`).

**After posting:**
8. **The posting itself.** Postiz, automatic at the set time.
9. **Answer the comment words with a DM that has a tagged link.** Yours. Instagram on your phone, and the link maker here. Nothing sends these DMs on its own (`story-format.md`, section 7).
10. **The numbers, on Sunday.** Yours. Here, and in Instagram.
11. **Requests to Yossi.** Yours. On WhatsApp.

**What is yours:** steps 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10 and 11. Everything else is Claude's or automatic. The code that runs 1, 4, 6 and 8 stays Claude's: when something breaks there, you ask Claude (lesson L27).

**Why "on the Mac only".** Carousel Machine runs at `localhost:8777`, and the creator map at `localhost:8780`. "localhost" means the server runs on the Mac itself, so your phone can't reach it. Hence the rule: Carousel and Creators tasks are desk tasks. Messages and Numbers tasks work from your phone.

**Why the grade comes with a reason.** The next batch reads the grades (`README.md`, line 61). A one-sentence reason tells it what to repeat and what to drop. But a grade is taste, not a result: only the results of published posts can change a hook formula (same line).

## Example
Tuesday evening, at the Mac. In Carousel Machine, two carousels are waiting under `לאישור` ("For approval"): step 5. You approve one picture, and for the other you write "the child's face isn't looking at the camera". Claude makes a new picture, the slides build on their own (step 6), and then on the preview screen you set the posting day at 06:00 (step 7). On the morning of the posting day, from your phone: the first comments, and for each one a DM with a link from the link maker (step 9).

## Common mistake
"The system runs on its own: it posts, replies and sells." It posts on its own. It replies to no one. If step 9 is dropped, the post asked for a word, parents wrote it, and nobody got a link.

## What the numbers can't prove
A published carousel proves that step 8 ran, not that any step after it happened.

## Terms
- **batch**: all the options for a whole month, written in one run before the month starts.
- **production**: the path from a chosen option to finished slides: pictures, approval, export.
- **localhost**: the address of a server that runs on the computer itself. It opens only from that computer.
