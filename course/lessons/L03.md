# After the click: from the comment word to a paid order

## Do this now
Reply to whoever wrote the comment word, and know which of the five stages that parent is at.

## The lesson
In the Messages task you see comments like `מוצץ` ("pacifier") or `עוגה` ("cake") under the carousel. That's the word the last slide asked for: `תכתבו "מוצץ" ואשלח לכם קישור להכנת הספר` ("Write 'pacifier' and I'll send you a link to make the book") (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/story-format.md`, section 7). From that moment to a paid order there are five stages, and at each one parents drop off.

**1. Comment word.** The parent wrote the word. That's interest, not a purchase yet.

**2. DM with a tagged link.** You send it. Nothing sends these messages automatically (`story-format.md`, section 7). A comment that didn't get a DM stops here.

**3. Click.** The parent opens the link and lands on the book page. The tag is saved in that browser tab.

**4. Book started.** The parent goes through the book builder (`research/cro-crm/audit.md`, steps 3 to 7):
- the child's name, age, boy or girl, the language of the book
- how to create the book, and choosing a book
- a face photo of the child
- phone and email, and a one-time code by WhatsApp or SMS

At the end of the book builder the store creates the book record and sends the tag with it (`audit.md`, step 7a). Then comes the preview: a cover with the child, and the pages inside stay locked until purchase (`audit.md`, step 8).

**5. Paid order.** Payment on Cardcom's page. Shipping is added only there (`audit.md`, step 9). After paying, the parent edits, approves, and the book goes to print.

**Where it leaks, in terms of your pay:**
- Between 1 and 2: no DM was sent, so there's no link.
- Between 3 and 4: the parent closed the tab before the end of the book builder, and the tag was lost (lesson L06).
- Between 4 and 5: the book was started and not paid for. The WhatsApp reminders belong to the store, meaning to Yossi. You only read their results.

**What you see today:** stage 1 (the comments) and stage 2 (the DMs you sent, in the link log). Stages 3 to 5 sit with the store. So your first calculation is: out of all the comment words, how many got a DM with a link.

## Example
A parent wrote `מוצץ` ("pacifier") under the pacifier carousel. The DM:

`היי, הנה הקישור להכנת הספר 📖` ("Hi, here's the link to make the book 📖")
`התצוגה המקדימה בחינם, ומשלמים רק אם אהבתם.` ("The preview is free, and you only pay if you liked it.")

And under it, the tagged link from the link maker to the page of "Bye Bye Pacifier" (`ביי ביי מוצץ`). The line about the preview is taken from the wording that was tested on the site (`story-format.md`, section 8).

Now the parent is at stage 2. If they open the link, they move to stage 3. If they finish the book builder in the same tab, their order will carry your tag.

## Common mistake
"A comment word is a sale." It's stage 1 of 5. Between a comment and a paid order there is a DM, a click, a whole book builder with a photo and a code, and a payment. Twenty comments without DMs are zero tagged links.

## What the numbers can't prove
The number of comments proves that the post sparked interest, not that anyone started a book.

## Terms
- **Comment word**: the word the last slide asks people to write in the comments, one word per topic, for example `מוצץ` ("pacifier").
- **Book builder (wizard)**: the step-by-step form where the parent enters the child's details, a photo and a phone number before seeing the book.
- **Preview**: the cover with the child that appears at the end of the book builder. The pages inside open only after payment.
