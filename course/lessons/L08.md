# The check before a carousel goes out

## Do this now
On the preview screen (`איך זה ייראה`, "How it will look"), go through six checks before you press `לתזמן` ("Schedule"), and check whether the carousel is already scheduled in Postiz.

## The lesson
The last two steps on a Carousel task's checklist are "check the preview screen" and "schedule it in the Postiz block at 06:00". This is the last point where you can catch a mistake before it goes out. Six checks:

1. **Up to 10 slides.** Instagram through Postiz accepts at most 10 images (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/story-format.md`, section 2). An eleventh slide and the post fails.
2. **The comment word on the last slide, and in the last two lines of the caption.** The same word in both places. One word from the topic, never `ספר` ("book") or `לומי` ("Lumi") (`story-format.md`, section 7).
3. **Nothing from the call to action before the last slide.** No brand name, no book name and no website address, not on the slides and not in the caption. The link comes only in the DM (same section).
4. **Facebook gets its own post.** A separate text that ends with the tagged link `https://lumebook.com/IL/he?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social`, with no comment word, above slide 1 with its text (`story-format.md`, section 8).
5. **The medical disclaimer.** At the bottom of every slide from slide 2 on, and after the caption. It is added automatically, but make sure it is there (same section).
6. **The time: 06:00.** In November, posts go out on Monday, Wednesday and Friday (the dates are in each carousel's `postiz.json` in `carousel/carousel-pipeline/approved/`).

**The trap: a post already in the queue.** If the carousel was already scheduled in Postiz and then edited, the preview screen shows the new version, but Postiz still holds the old one. A post already sent to Postiz keeps the old slides until it is sent again (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/README.md`, line 71). Re-sending replaces the post in the queue, and Claude does it only with your approval (`story-format.md`, section 8).

So after any edit to a carousel that is already scheduled: ask Claude to replace it in Postiz.

## Example
A carousel is scheduled for Wednesday. On Monday evening you change its last slide, and going through the checks on the preview screen, everything looks perfect: 10 slides, the comment word `עוגה` ("cake") on the last slide and in the caption, a Facebook post with the link. But the carousel was sent to Postiz before the edit. Without re-sending, the old version goes out on Wednesday at 06:00. Write to Claude: "Replace Wednesday's carousel in Postiz with the new version."

## Common mistake
"What you see in Carousel Machine is what goes out." Only if the carousel was sent to Postiz after the last edit. What actually goes out is what Postiz holds.

## What the numbers can't prove
A carousel that passed every check can bring comments and DMs. That does not prove it will.

## Terms
- **posting queue**: the posts already sent to Postiz and waiting for their time, each with the slides it had at the moment it was sent.
- **re-sending**: replacing a post in the queue with the new version, at the same date and time. Only with your approval.

## Quiz
### Question 1
You edited the call to action of a carousel that is already scheduled in Postiz. What goes out at the set time?
- [x] The old version, until the post is sent again
- [ ] The new version, automatically
- [ ] Nothing, the post is cancelled
> Postiz keeps the slides it had at the moment it was sent. Only re-sending replaces them.

### Question 2 · recall L02
Which tag separates your links from creator links and ad links?
- [x] `utm_medium`
- [ ] `utm_content`
- [ ] `utm_term`
> `social` is yours, `creator` is for creators, `paid-social` is for ads. All of them are in `utm_medium`.

### Question 3 · open
Why is a comment word missing from the last slide a pay problem, and not just a design problem?
> Without the word there are no comments, without comments there are no DMs, and without DMs there are no tagged links.
> Without tagged links there are no tagged orders, so nothing is counted toward your pay.
