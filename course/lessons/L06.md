# Write Yossi a request he can act on: a per-link view, and A21

## Do this now
Write Yossi one message with two requests, following the five steps of the checklist, and mark the task "Done".

## The lesson
A Yossi task always has the same checklist. It exists because "Can you check this?" goes unanswered, and a precise request gets an answer:

1. **What you need, in one sentence.**
2. **Why:** what it changes for sales or for your pay.
3. **The exact change** you are asking for.
4. **How you will know it worked:** the before number.
5. **Send it and mark it "Done".** The task is finished even without a reply.

This time there are two requests.

**Request 1: show the orders with your tag.** The code already sends the tag with every book created (`research/creator-content-conversion/report.md`, line 514). All that is missing is a screen or a weekly export that shows which orders carry `utm_medium=social`.

**Request 2: the A21 fix.** Here you need to understand one leak. When a parent arrives through a tagged link, the tag is saved in the tab's memory. When the tab closes, the tag is deleted (`research/cro-crm/audit.md`, A21). The audit says this happens a lot in the browser that opens inside Instagram, and with people buying a gift who come back a few days later in a new tab. That parent can buy through your link and still end up as an order with no tag.

The fix the audit suggests: keep the tag for 30 days, in the browser's permanent storage or in a site cookie, with consent (`audit.md`, A21). You don't need to know how to code it. You need to ask for it, and know how to check it.

**The before number.** The audit recommends measuring the fix by the share of orders that carry a tag, before and after (`audit.md`, A21, and also `research/cro-crm/measurement.md`, section 9). You don't have that number. So the request includes: record it before the change. After the change, it can't be recovered.

**A message, not a presentation.** Yossi needs to read it, understand it and decide in a minute. No introduction, no persuading him how important it is.

## Example
The message, on WhatsApp:

"Yossi, two requests about my pay:
1. See the orders that carry utm_medium=social once a week. Those are my tags. The code already sends the tag with every book created, only an export or a screen is missing.
2. The tag is saved only in the tab, so anyone who closes it and comes back later loses it. Keep it for 30 days, as A21 in the audit says.
How we'll know: before the change, record what percent of orders come with a tag, and check again two weeks after.
One more small thing: ads shouldn't get utm_medium=social, so they don't get mixed up with mine."

Send it, mark it "Done", and the task is finished.

## Common mistake
"A tagged link stays tagged until the parent pays." No. The tag lives in the tab where the link opened. A parent who tapped, closed, and came back tomorrow through Google ends up without your tag, even if it all started with your DM.

## What the numbers can't prove
A rise in the share of tagged orders after the fix proves the leak got smaller, not that your posts sold more.

## Terms
- **tab memory (sessionStorage)**: where the store saves the tag today. It is wiped when the tab closes.
- **before number (baseline)**: the measurement taken before a change. Without it, you can't show that the change did anything.
