What every carousel must do (Yarin's brief), 7 slides:
- Each option is about one named Israeli girl or boy (a different name from names.md per option) and talks to mom (feminine singular) or to both parents (plural). Never to dad alone: no אבא on its own, no אתה. Talking to both parents may say "אמא ואבא" (allowed only when addressee is parents). The image anchor shows the mother or both parents.
- Before any slide: a private hook slate of 6 kid and 6 expert candidates in pack.json (`hook_slate`, schema_version 3), built and scored as in nightly.md step 5b and hook-bank.md "Nightly hook slate". Each option is built from one passing candidate; never use a failing candidate or an expired trend formula, and never rank the options.
- 1 hook built from a hook-bank formula (a different formula per option, scored 9+ of 12 with no zero), 2 a second hook that works alone, 3 what is behind the feeling or problem, 4 and 5 two concrete solutions with real value, 6 the resolution that closes the story, 7 one short line suggesting to help the child with a personalised story (סיפור אישי) where they are the hero, then the comment CTA in the addressee's form (כתבי ... וקבלי ... שלך for mom, כתבו ... וקבלו ... שלכם for both parents).
- SLIDE 1 IS THE THUMBNAIL (Yarin, 10 Oct 2026: "clickbaity and always have the child's face facing the viewer with an exaggerated expression"). Words: the biggest honest gap the topic allows. Tension first and never the answer, with a number, a real quoted sentence, a contradiction, a confession in the child's voice or a precise moment; no soft words (קצת, לפעמים, אולי); the carousel must still answer it, and the banned openers stay banned. An option may only use a slate candidate that scores 2 on stopping_power, honest_curiosity and swipe_promise. Picture: the slide 1 image brief says the child faces the viewer and looks straight into the camera, names one big exaggerated expression that fits the hook (huge wide eyes and raised eyebrows, dramatic pout with puffed cheeks, open-mouthed gasp, giant grin), and uses a close head-and-shoulders or waist-up shot. Never from behind, in profile, from the side or face down; a parent only small in the background. Read hook-bank.md "Slide 1 is the thumbnail" and image-briefs.md "Slide 1 is the cover". The validator rejects a pack that misses any of this. This applies to the six kid and expert options, not to the games and books carousels.
- Yarin's own hook formula `child-numbered-ask` is in the hook bank (example: אמא ואבא / 2 דברים שיעזרו לי / לפני שאתם שמים אותי בגן). Use it for at most one kid option that talks to both parents, with slides 4 and 5 opening דבר ראשון / דבר שני.
- The CTA code word ALWAYS sits inside straight quotation marks right after the verb, on slide 7 and in the caption's last line: כתבו "גן" בתגובות וקבלו קישור ליצירת ספר שבו הילד שלכם הוא הגיבור (Yarin, 8 Oct 2026).
- Never say or hint that anything is made with AI: not on slides, in captions, alt text or anywhere else (Yarin, 8 Oct 2026: "it ruins trust and engagement"). The validator rejects AI, בינה מלאכותית and "נוצר בעזרת".
- Hook the reader again on every slide: slides 1 to 6 each end with a reason to swipe (record it as `pull`, with the note in short Hebrew).
- Nothing before slide 7 prepares a sale (no book, story offer, link or comments talk on slides 1 to 6).
- In the six kid and expert options, never name LumeBook / לומבוק or any book title anywhere a parent reads (slides, caption, CTA). (Only the separate games and books carousels below name LumeBook, once, on their LumeBook suggestion slide.)

Every brief keeps a plain wall or sky in its text zone, because the slide text sits straight on the picture with no box. If the copy says the parents are gone, the brief must say "the parents are not in this scene". Name each prop once and say who holds it.
