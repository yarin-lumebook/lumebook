The challenge

The therapist’s brand had a real voice and an existing audience, but it was scattered across three social profiles with different names, photos and value propositions, and an inconsistent visual style. Her bio described techniques rather than what she does for the women who come to her, and she wasn’t reaching new people on Instagram. In a service built on trust, every contradiction like that creates doubt.

The insight

The starting assumption was that she needed more content and more reach. The research showed the real constraint was trust. I looked at how women describe their pain in forums and advice columns, and none of them used the word “pattern” that the profession uses. The data said the same: half of the breakout reels I analysed were completely silent, with on-screen text only, and their median length was 25.5 seconds against 69 for ordinary reels.

What I did

I led the project end to end. I repositioned her as a guide for midlife women who want to understand and break recurring patterns, and built the brand framework, messaging and a Hebrew visual identity. I also mapped 21 Israeli creators and analysed 44 reels with local transcription, then turned the findings into four content pillars, 80 ideas, a role for each channel, and a measurement framework focused on saves, private shares and conversations rather than followers.

The results

Over 90 days (Jul 1 – Sep 28, 2026), views grew 462% and content interactions grew 340%. The lift followed the new positioning, unified messaging and research-led content system. This is a comparison with the prior period, not a controlled experiment, so it shows a strong temporal link rather than proving full causality.

What I’d do next

What isn’t measured yet: bookings and conversations that came from the content, and saves and private shares, which can’t be seen from the outside. Eight experiments are ready to run with their results left blank on purpose. The next step is to run them and find out which content mechanisms bring conversations, not just views.
