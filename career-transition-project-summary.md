# Career Transition Project — Summary

## Goal
Land a first professional role in performance marketing / marketing ops at a seed–Series A Israeli B2B SaaS company, as a generalist growth hire. Proof-of-work over credentials.

## Background
- Israel-based, dual citizenship (Hungarian passport, EU eligible)
- Background in face-to-face sales — framed as differentiator (pipeline accountability most marketers lack)
- Relocation (Netherlands, Germany, Ireland, Australia) deprioritized; Israel is the primary base, EU kept as future optionality
- Frameworks used throughout: *Atomic Habits*, *How to Win Friends and Influence People*, *48 Laws of Power*

## Current State — Week 1 of 90-Day Plan
Specialization spike: **marketing operations** (chosen over paid acquisition/SEO via concentration-of-forces principle).

Active work:
- LinkedIn overhaul (headline: Role | Stack | Value; tight 1-paragraph About)
- HubSpot certification track (Marketing Software cert → lifecycle stages → lead scoring → nurture → dashboards)
- GA4 certification (Google Skillshop)
- Daily habit system + community engagement protocol
- HubSpot portal set up for hands-on practice
- Prepped 10 structured questions (broad → specific) for an informational interview with a working performance marketer

## Roadmap
**Month 2 (proof of work):** full demand-gen engine in HubSpot; real-spend paid campaign; public funnel teardowns of Israeli B2B SaaS companies on LinkedIn; job search run as a HubSpot pipeline.

**Month 3 (search as campaign):** defined ICP, channel mix weighted to warm outreach/referrals, weekly funnel-style scoreboard.

**Longer-term:** EU relocation and/or Open Universiteit Nederland degree — contingent on where traction actually develops, not a default.

## Key Principles Established
- Job search = trust/proof problem, not a knowledge problem
- Spike before spreading — depth in one lane beats shallow breadth
- Tool stack (HubSpot + GA4 + LinkedIn Ads) chosen because it maps to how B2B buyers move and where Israeli SaaS spend concentrates
- Ops = infrastructure identity (pipes, lifecycle, attribution); performance = campaign execution identity — spike is ops, performance fluency builds alongside
- Israel is the right base: relationship-driven, B2B SaaS-dense hiring market
- Carnegie's "give before asking" → two-week rule before asking community for anything
- Client market drives location decisions, not the reverse

## Working Style Notes
- Wants short, direct, reasoned answers — no bullet-summary or tutorial tone by default
- Pushes back on unverified/generic/irrelevant recommendations — expects specifics in defense
- Moves fast once decided ("les go ops") — values momentum
- Dislikes AI-sounding phrasing; asks for compression when responses run long
- Prefers broad-to-specific ordering
- Engages mental models/frameworks seriously — wants them applied, not name-dropped
