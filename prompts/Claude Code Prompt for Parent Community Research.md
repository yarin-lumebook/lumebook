# Claude Code Prompt for Parent Community Research

You are conducting an exhaustive community-discovery project for LumeBook, an Israeli company that creates personalised children’s books.

## Objective

Find as many relevant Facebook groups, Facebook communities, WhatsApp groups, and WhatsApp communities as possible where LumeBook could recruit Israeli parents for a product-for-content collaboration.

The offer:

- The parent receives one free personalised children’s book
- In return, the parent creates authentic content showing both the book and their child’s face
- Suitable formats include unboxing, the child’s first reaction, bedtime reading, a short review, a Reel, or a Story
- The child’s face must be clearly visible in the content
- The book is the full compensation
- Parents do not need a large following
- The participating adult must be the parent or legal guardian of every child appearing
- LumeBook may later offer selected participants a personal link that gives their audience a discount
- Do not describe this as a discount code or personal code

LumeBook serves Israeli families. Prioritise Hebrew-speaking communities and parents of children roughly ages 1 to 10.

## Important distinction

We are not merely looking for large parenting groups. We need communities where a respectful recruitment post for a free-book content collaboration has a realistic chance of being relevant and permitted.

For every result, investigate:

1. Is the community still active?
2. Is it relevant to Israeli parents?
3. Are promotional posts, collaborations, recommendations, product-testing opportunities, or recruitment posts allowed?
4. Is administrator approval required?
5. Is there a named administrator or public contact route?
6. Is there evidence that similar brand or collaboration posts have appeared?
7. Would a direct recruitment post fit, or should LumeBook contact the administrator first?
8. Does the community appear legitimate and safe?
9. For WhatsApp, is the invitation link public and still working?
10. Is the same community duplicated under another name or link?

Never invent a group, URL, member count, administrator, rule, or activity level. Mark anything that cannot be verified as “Unknown.”

Do not join private groups, bypass access controls, scrape personal member information, message anyone, or publish anything. Use only publicly visible information. Do not collect children’s names, photographs, or personal information during the research.

Create one Markdown file only:

Do not create a CSV or modify any other project file.

Begin with an executive summary containing:

Use these priority levels:

- A: Excellent fit, active, and posting appears allowed
- B: Strong audience fit, but administrator approval or further verification is needed
- C: Relevant but broad, inactive, highly restrictive, or uncertain
- Reject: Spam, unsafe, irrelevant, expired, duplicated, or clearly prohibits this activity

For “Recommended approach,” use exactly one of:

- Post directly
- Ask admin first
- Paid post or formal partnership
- Monitor
- Do not use

## Verification requirements

- Open and inspect every result before including it
- Prefer direct community URLs over search-result URLs
- Record evidence supporting claims about rules and activity
- Check WhatsApp links during the research and flag expired or unverified invitations
- Deduplicate communities by URL, Facebook group ID, invite code, administrator, and substantially identical names
- Keep rejected results in a separate appendix
- Include the date every result was checked
- Preserve Hebrew community names exactly as written
- Sort the final list by priority, audience fit, activity, and size
- Aim for broad coverage, not an arbitrary fixed number
- Continue searching until additional query variations stop producing meaningful new communities

Select the 20 communities most likely to produce suitable parent-generated content in which the child’s face is clearly visible.

Explain briefly why each community is promising and whether LumeBook should post directly or approach its administrator.

### Search gaps

List audiences, cities, or platforms where reliable communities could not be found. Suggest relevant organisations LumeBook could contact to gain access, without inventing contact details.

### Outreach guidance

Create three short Hebrew outreach drafts:

1. A warm post for a general parenting community
2. A concise post for a UGC or creator community
3. A private message asking a group administrator for permission

Requirements for all drafts:

- Use natural Israeli Hebrew with RTL-safe formatting
- Write לומבוק, never לומיבוק
- Do not mention AI
- Do not use em dashes
- Clearly state that the free personalised book is the full compensation
- Clearly state that the child’s face must be visible in the content
- Clearly state that only a parent or legal guardian may participate
- Explain the expected content without sounding legalistic
- Do not claim that everyone who responds will be accepted
- Do not request children’s information in the public comments
- Direct interested parents to a private or secure application process
