5. SCIENCE → MARKETING BRIDGE
==================================================

This is one of the MOST IMPORTANT requirements.

Do NOT create an academic psychology curriculum with some marketing examples added afterward.

Every important theoretical concept must eventually pass through:

UNDERLYING HUMAN MECHANISM
→ BEHAVIORAL CONSEQUENCE
→ CUSTOMER INSIGHT
→ MARKETING IMPLICATION
→ STRATEGIC APPLICATION
→ CREATIVE APPLICATION
→ SOCIAL/DIGITAL APPLICATION
→ METRIC
→ EXPERIMENT
→ BUSINESS RESULT

For example:

CONCEPT:
Social proof

MECHANISM:
People sometimes use other people's behavior as information when making judgments under uncertainty.

BEHAVIORAL CONSEQUENCE:
Visible adoption may reduce uncertainty.

CUSTOMER INSIGHT:
A prospective customer may hesitate because they are uncertain whether a product is trustworthy or effective.

MARKETING IMPLICATION:
Credible evidence of customer adoption/outcomes may reduce perceived risk.

EXECUTION:
Testimonials, reviews, demonstrations, customer stories, usage evidence.

EXPERIMENT:
Compare social-proof-led creative against another persuasive approach.

METRICS:
CTR, qualified leads, conversion rate, CAC.

BUSINESS RESULT:
Determine whether credible social proof improves acquisition efficiency.

This is how theory should become practical knowledge.
