      Gaps

      Where reliable communities were not found, and who could open the door

      Arabic-speaking families

      Facebook search for Nazareth and "أمهات الداخل" gave city buy-sell groups and pan-Arab pregnancy groups that describe themselves as "from all over the world". None is verifiably Israeli. Route: early-childhood departments of Arab local authorities and Arab-society parenting professionals with public pages.

      Russian and English speakers

      A few groups each (Русскоговорящие мамы Израиля, МамыАшкелона, Мамочки Нетании, Our Modi'in, English Speakers Raanana), all with ad-day or admin-approval rules. Confirm which language editions of the book exist before posting in them.

      Ashkelon on WhatsApp

      No Ashkelon parent WhatsApp group was found: the WhatsApp search found no WhatsApp links on the Ashkelon municipality site. Route: Ashkelon community centres and the city's early-childhood department.

      WhatsApp

      Public invites live mostly on municipal and community-centre pages, and most are one-way update groups or channels. 21 invites could not be checked because WhatsApp rate-limited the checks. Route: ask the early-childhood centres to share the call.

      Books and reading

      Parent-facing children's book groups are small (about 1.3K each). The big book groups are adult readers or second-hand sellers. Route: public libraries' children's programmes and the book-recommendation pages under Other routes.

      Fathers

      The אבא פגום network is the only large one (136K group plus a 30.7K marketplace). The rest are small support groups.

      Not searched

      Haredi, Druze and Bedouin family communities were not specifically searched. Jerusalem mothers groups are private with low visible activity.

      Method and checks

      How it was found

      • Facebook group search in a logged-in browser, 50+ queries in Hebrew, English, Russian and Arabic by city and topic. About 620 raw hits, about 320 relevant. Best queries: "אמהות <city>" and "הורים <city>". Saturation showed when "אמהות טריות" gave 1 new group out of 16.
      • Each candidate's About page: privacy, members, activity box, admins, About text and rules from the admins (287 opened).
      • Re-ranked on 14 Oct 2026 for the social-media shooting-day offer: local groups over 80 km from Ashkelon and creator groups moved to C. Each record keeps its 9 Oct grade.
      • Municipal and community-centre sites crawled for WhatsApp invites; creator hubs, WhatsApp directories, Linktrees and ynet and Kipa group lists.

      What was checked

      • Every Facebook URL rendered on 9 Oct 2026. WhatsApp invites checked until WhatsApp returned HTTP 429; unchecked ones say so.
      • Facts: members, activity, privacy, rules, invite status. Judgement: priority, approach, post angle.
      • No member lists opened, no private phone numbers or emails kept, no child data collected. Nothing joined, posted or messaged.
      • Every A row has an activity figure and a quoted rule or About line that allows posting.
