Which carousels actually sell in the kids and parenting niche?

I swept the carousels of __IL_ACC__ Israeli and __GL_ACC__ English-language parenting, kids' book, toy and personalised-book accounts, ranked each one against its own account's median, and read the slides of the winners.

Correction to v1. The first version covered six personalised-book brands only and called that "the niche". Those brands barely use carousels to sell. The real winners sit with parenting creators, and they are below.

How this was measured

__IL_ACC__Israeli accounts swept
__IL_CAR__Israeli carousels ranked
__GL_ACC__global accounts swept
__GL_CAR__global carousels ranked

For each account I read its last 48 posts since April 2024 and took the median likes and comments. A carousel's score is its likes or comments divided by that median, so a 300-follower creator and a 300K brand compete fairly. Instagram never shows another account's sales, so I used the closest visible signal: carousels that end in "comment a word and I'll DM you". Every comment on those is a person asking for a link, a guide or a price. Two Israeli posts go further and state sales or a purchase link outright.

What the evidence says

The selling mechanism is the comment-word DM

__IL_FUN__ Israeli carousels end with "comment X and I'll send it". Those posts pull comments at many times the account's normal rate: up to 295x on a small account. Globally the same move reaches 16,571 comments on one post.

Reach and leads are different posts

Letters, life stories and reversal hooks get the reach (14K to 136K likes). Lists and how-tos that end in a comment word get the leads, often with modest likes. The best ones do both.

Books sell through carousels in Hebrew

A book list for the move to municipal gan closes with "write the word ספר" and a coupon. A digital parenting book's carousel reports 230 buyers in 48 hours and 530 at close.

AI illustration is not a barrier

One of the top Israeli lead carousels (1,751 likes, 1,771 comments) is drawn entirely with AI-style illustrated characters. Carousels also beat the same account's other posts in __IL_WIN__ Israeli accounts.

__GL_EXTRA__

Three carousels to make for Lumebook

These are my recommendations from the patterns above, not tested results. All three end in a comment word and a DM, which needs DM automation set up on the account. MeBook's creator reels already use "comment ספר for my discount code" (example), so each template uses a milestone word instead.

A. Milestone book list (tal_strul model)
  1. Cover: a real child with the hook "5 ספרים שמכינים לאח או אחות חדשים".
  2. Four published books, one per slide, with what each does for the child.
  3. Slide 6: the book where your child is the big sibling (Lumebook).
  4. Last slide: comment "אח" for links, a free preview and a code.
B. Letter to a mom (natashagolan and danit model)
  1. One photo on every slide, letter in text boxes: "היי אמא, עוד רגע הבכור שלך הופך לאח גדול".
  2. Five to eight slides of the honest feelings.
  3. One slide on what helped: telling the firstborn a story where they are the hero.
  4. Ask readers to send it to an expecting mom, and comment a word for the free preview.
C. "Instead of X, say Y", drawn (avital model)
  1. Illustrated scenes in Lumebook's own style of a child at gan drop-off.
  2. Each slide: what parents usually say, what to say instead, what the child learns.
  3. Last slide: comment "גן" for a short free story starring your child, sent as a preview link.

What this cannot tell you

  • No order data. Comments on a funnel post are requests, not purchases. Only the yaffa_michaela_zukerman carousel states a sales figure, and that is her own claim.
  • The pool is wide, not complete. Accounts came from our 204-creator list, Hebrew and English Instagram search, and Instagram's similar-accounts lists. A big account outside those paths would be missing.
  • 48 posts per account, since April 2024. Older viral carousels are not in the ranking.
  • Some numbers are shared. Collab posts appear under both accounts (I counted each post once). A few accounts hide likes, and those were skipped.
  • Personalised-book brands barely place. Wonderbly's best carousel had 260 likes and Hooray Heroes' 79; MeBook posted none. The lesson for Lumebook comes from parenting creators, not from competitors.

Data: research/carousel-research/ in the Lumebook project folder (finalist CSVs, captions, slide images).
