# Parent community outreach map

LumeBook recruitment of Israeli families for a social-media shooting day at home in exchange for a free personalised book. Communities were researched and checked on 2026-10-09 and re-ranked for this offer on 2026-10-14. Every count, rule and activity figure was read on the community's own page on 2026-10-09 unless a row says otherwise.

## The offer

**יום צילום לרשתות החברתיות של לומבוק, אצלכם בבית, תמורת ספר מותאם אישית במתנה**

- LumeBook comes to the family's home for a short shooting day for LumeBook's social media
- The child (face visible) and a parent are filmed with the book
- The videos go on LumeBook's socials; the parent does not need to post or have followers
- The free personalised book is the full compensation
- Only a parent or legal guardian of every child on camera can take part
- Closest families to Ashkelon first (Yarin travels by public transport)

Distance: Straight-line km from Ashkelon city centre to the group's city (approximate city-centre coordinates). Public transport time was not checked.

## Executive summary

- **Total unique communities found: 361**, plus 64 pages, sites and organisations listed separately as other routes (not counted).
- **Facebook: 295** groups, each opened on its About page in a logged-in browser (read-only: nothing joined, liked, posted or messaged).
- **WhatsApp: 59** groups, communities and channels. Invite status: VALID 31, ERROR 21, EXPIRED 7. ERROR means WhatsApp rate-limited the check, so the invite is unverified.
- **Other platforms: 7** (Telegram groups and public forums).
- **Priority:** A 20, B 104, C 155, Reject 82. For the shooting-day offer, 26 rows moved down: local groups more than 80 km from Ashkelon, and creator groups (parents no longer need to post).
- **Recommended for direct posting: 54**; administrator outreach first: 143; paid post or formal partnership: 18; monitor only: 64.
- **Rejected: 82**; questionable (priority C): 155.
- **Usable groups by distance from Ashkelon:** Ashkelon 10, Under 30 km 17, 30 to 55 km 57, 55 to 80 km 63, Over 80 km 24, National (members anywhere) 108.
- **Most promising segment:** the Ashkelon mothers groups, several of which allow free posting, then Sderot, Ashdod and Netivot. National groups come after the local ones, with the family's city asked in the form.
- **Major gaps:** no Ashkelon parent WhatsApp group was found; no Israeli Arabic-language parent group was verified; the parent sign-up form does not exist yet. Details in Search gaps.

### How to read this

- **Geographic focus** shows the city and the straight-line distance from Ashkelon. National groups have members anywhere.
- **Activity evidence** for Facebook is the Activity box on each group's About page, copied as shown. Use it to compare groups, not as an absolute.
- **Promotional-post policy** quotes or paraphrases the group's About text and rules from the admins.
- **Confidence:** High = written rules were read; Medium = only About text or a valid invite page; Low = name and numbers only, or an invite that could not be checked.
- Verified facts are members, activity, privacy, rules and invite status. **Assumptions** are the priority, the approach and the post angle, which are judgement from those facts.

## Outreach guidance

- Ask the admin first whenever the rules mention approval, a business day or a thread, and post only where and when the rules allow.
- Never collect children's details in comments. Every draft points to a private form. The ambassadors form is built for creators with a follower band, so families need a short sign-up form: parent name and phone, city or neighbourhood, the child's first name and age for the book, days that suit a visit, guardian confirmation, and consent that the child's face and the parent will appear on LumeBook's socials. That form does not exist yet.
- Before each visit, get written consent from the parent that names where the videos will appear. Book visits by city so one trip covers several families (a suggestion, not tested).
- The Hebrew drafts use the fallback voice (2 of 15 approved samples), pass the project's Hebrew lint except the slide-only word limit, write לומבוק, mention no AI and use no em dashes.

- **URLs:** every Facebook group URL rendered on 2026-10-09. WhatsApp invites were checked one by one until WhatsApp returned HTTP 429; rows marked unverified need a re-check. Other-route links show their HTTP status.

- **Privacy:** no member lists opened, no private phone numbers or personal emails recorded, no child data collected. Nothing was joined, posted or messaged.
- **Honest outreach:** every draft states the deal, the visible-face requirement, guardian-only participation, where the videos will appear, and that not everyone is accepted.
