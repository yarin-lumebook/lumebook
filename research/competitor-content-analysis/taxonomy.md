# Coding taxonomy (use these exact values)

Pick ONE primary value unless the field says "list". If nothing fits, use `other` and explain in `notes`.

## pillar — WHAT the piece is about (the job it does), not how it's shot
- `product_demo` — shows how the product works / what you get (upload photo → preview → book)
- `reveal_reaction` — child/recipient sees the product for the first time; the reaction is the payload
- `customer_story` — a specific family's situation and how the product fit into it (testimonial with narrative)
- `social_proof` — reviews, numbers, press, "X families", star ratings, montage of customers
- `problem_awareness` — names a child/parent problem (fears, new sibling, bedtime, screens) before/without product
- `parenting_education` — tips/advice valuable without buying
- `gift_occasion` — framed around an occasion (birthday, holiday, grandparent gift, back-to-school)
- `product_range` — catalogue/new title/new character/collection showcase
- `offer_promo` — discount, sale, deadline, bundle is the main message
- `behind_scenes` — how it's made, team, printing, founder
- `entertainment` — trend, humour, meme, music moment; product secondary
- `community_ugc_repost` — reposting customers' content / shout-outs
- `comparison_objection` — vs alternatives, answers an objection (price, quality, AI, privacy, shipping)
- `brand_mission` — values, why we exist
- `other`

## funnel_stage
- `awareness` — a cold viewer would not need to know the brand; leads with a human/child/problem/entertainment
- `consideration` — explains how it works, what you get, proof, objections
- `conversion` — explicit offer, price, deadline, "order now", promo code, limited time
- `retention` — speaks to existing customers (second book, sibling, share your book, refer, community)
Ambiguity rule: classify by what the LAST 30% of the piece + caption asks the viewer to do. If it asks to buy with an incentive/deadline → conversion; asks to buy without incentive but shows product mechanics → consideration; no ask → awareness. Paid ads are not automatically conversion.
