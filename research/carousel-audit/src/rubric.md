## Scoring rubric (version 1)

Every carousel gets a score from 1 to 10 on ten dimensions. The scores are a reviewer's judgement from the posted PNGs viewed at phone width (360 px tiles), not a measurement. Use them to compare carousels with each other and to spot what to fix, never as evidence that something performs: performance comes from the exports.

The rubric follows the project's own rules where they exist: Yarin's carousel rules (no brand or book title on the slides, the code word לומי, slide 1 shows the child's face with an exaggerated expression) and the playbook (`docs/lumebook-instagram-carousel-playbook-he.md`, sections 5, 9 and 12). "Product relevance" is judged inside the no-brand rule: how naturally the post leads to a book LumeBook sells, not whether the brand appears.

| Dimension | 1 to 3 | 4 to 6 | 7 to 8 | 9 to 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** (slide 1 text) | A category label ("4 games") with no tension | Clear topic, mild interest | A specific moment or sentence, with a promise the post keeps | Specific, surprising and honest; a parent in that moment cannot pass it |
| **Visual stopping power** | Small objects, no face, low contrast | A clear picture with no strong focal point | A big expressive face or an object that tells the topic | Face and topic read at a glance, unlike anything else in the feed |
| **Audience relevance** | Outside the 2 to 8 core, or a rare situation | Relevant to a narrow age or a niche | A common situation for parents of 2 to 8 year olds | Common, timely, and matched to a book that sells |
| **Emotional resonance** | Informational only | Some feeling, generic | A recognisable feeling from the inside | A feeling a parent says "that's us" to |
| **Curiosity and progression** | No reason to swipe; items unlabelled | Numbered, but slides are interchangeable | Numbering or open loops on most slides, a payoff | Every slide earns the next; recap and close land |
| **Mobile readability** | Low contrast or small body text | Readable with effort | Clear at phone size | Instant at phone size, including body lines |
| **Information density** | Cramped (3+ lines on many slides) or empty | Uneven | One idea per slide, headline plus one or two lines | One idea per slide everywhere, nothing wasted |
| **Value to parents** | Nothing to take away | Reassurance or ideas without actions | Two or more actions a parent can do tomorrow | Specific, sourced actions useful even if they stop before the CTA |
| **Product relevance** | No link to anything LumeBook sells | A loose link | The topic matches a catalogue book and the story idea follows from the advice | The book is shown or the story idea is the natural next step |
| **CTA clarity** | No action, or two different actions | One action but unclear where or what for | One action, where to do it and what they get | Same single action on the slide and the caption, low effort, clear reward |
