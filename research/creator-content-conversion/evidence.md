# Evidence behind each conclusion

Companion to `report.md`. Every number comes from `posts.csv`, `creators.csv` or the comment classification described in `methodology.md`, collected on 14 Oct 2026, unless another file is named.

- **Observed** = seen in a post, a comment, a profile or a file. **Interpretation** = my reading. **Hypothesis** = to be tested.
- Quotes are audience comments or on-post text as published, at most 12 words, children's names replaced by [שם], no usernames. Every quoted comment below was re-read by me in the raw comment data, not only in the coders' summaries.
- `rel` = the post's plays (reels) or likes (other formats) divided by the same creator's median for that format in their recent sample. `per 100` = per 100 sampled comments.

## E1. Buying-occasion topics do not win attention inside a creator's own feed

**Observed (posts.csv, recent sample, 469 posts, 41 creators):**

| Coded topic group | Reels | Creators | Median `rel` plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| LumeBook buying occasion (birthday, new sibling, pregnancy, pacifier, potty, gan start, first grade, holiday) | 82 | 33 | 0.93 |
| Development and behaviour | 50 | 26 | 1.00 |
| Everyday parenting | 40 | 26 | 1.00 |
| Humour and entertainment | 49 | 18 | 1.25 |
| Books and reading (incl. bedtime) | 11 | 10 | 0.90 |

- Within creators who have at least two high-relevance and two low-relevance reels or posts, the high-relevance posts had the higher median ratio in 4 of 12 creators.
- Formats with the highest median ratio: before/after demonstrations 3.08 (7 reels, 6 creators), collab (co-authored) reels 1.61 (12, 11), day vlogs 1.54 (12, 10). Product reviews 0.76 (19, 10).

**Examples of what does stand out (observed):**
- may_rot1, toilet-wiping demo with a balloon, 381,775 plays, 39x her median: [DdUFGI3oO8T](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdUFGI3oO8T/). A potty-skill demonstration, not a buying post.
- savta_ziona_official, grandmother-character comedy on a boat, 622,766 plays, 69x: [DdMP8MYIva7](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdMP8MYIva7/).
- ima.shir, a night-time ice-cream outing with her kids, 858,323 plays, 29x: [DeCZjK0NZpm](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeCZjK0NZpm/).
- yuli.brosh, 90s nostalgia, 103,567 plays, 24x: [DaPzBaVNElA](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaPzBaVNElA/).

**Interpretation:** what spreads in these feeds is humour, family moments, nostalgia and hands-on demonstrations. Milestone topics hold the creator's normal level; they do not lift it. **Limit:** observational, and post age varies within the 7 to 90 day window.

## E2. Professionals' how-to and reassurance posts produce the practical questions; parents' humour produces identification and laughter

**Observed (184 posts, 2,982 classified comments):**

| Creator stratum | Posts | Comments | Help requests | Purchase-intent signals per 100 | Identification + experience + help per 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Parents | 60 | 1,064 | 15 | 1.1 | 8.6 |

- Humour-angle posts: 425 comments, 0 help requests, 0.9 intent per 100.

**Examples (observed, quoted comments):**

- ima.shir, a child "knew" about the pregnancy [DdtDs1WNRDp](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdtDs1WNRDp/): many commenters tell the same story about their own child, for example "גם הבת שלי ידעה" (my daughter knew too).

**Interpretation:** professional content creates the moment where a parent asks "what should I do / what should I get". Parent humour creates closeness, not questions.

## E3. The main consideration mechanic is "comment a word and I will DM you"

**Observed:**
- 72 of 469 recent posts (15%) by 21 of 41 creators ask for a comment word in exchange for a link, file, list, guide, coupon or workshop.
- Within the 20 creators with such posts and a comparison baseline, the comment ratio was higher on keyword posts in 18.
- Reels with a keyword CTA: median 5.19 comments per 1,000 plays, against 1.08 for reels with no CTA.
- In the comment sample, 1,035 of 2,982 comments (35%) are keyword requests. On keyword posts, identification, experience and help requests fall to 2.8 per 100.

**Book lists use exactly this mechanic (observed):**
- tal_strul, five books for the move to a municipal gan, with a coupon: [DZphMo0CAUb](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZphMo0CAUb/), June 2026 (older breakout), 1,389 comments, 84x her comment median; all 30 sampled comments are the word "ספר".
- hilla_gover_slp, books by age from birth to 3: [Da0n_qzACqS](https://www.instagram.com/p/Da0n_qzACqS/), 882 comments; sampled comments are "רשימה" or "לינק".
- topaz_meir_slp, reading aloud plus a file of book picks by age: [Dc6VXOdMWOv](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc6VXOdMWOv/), 112,928 plays (5.0x her median), 1,055 comments; sampled comments are "ספרים".
- hen.kalimian.kids, a confidence book before first grade, "write 'book' for the discounted link with free shipping": [DdegHrWjvH9](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdegHrWjvH9/); 12 of 13 sampled comments are "ספר".
- hen.kalimian.kids, first-grade readiness reels with a free test or guide: [DaNo5CQIOll](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaNo5CQIOll/) 1,401 comments and [DbF8NhWO-Hk](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbF8NhWO-Hk/) 1,379 comments, about 31x her comment median (older breakouts).

**Interpretation:** a keyword comment is a request for an offer. It shows that the topic and the offer are wanted, and it is the closest public proxy to a link click. It says nothing about purchase. Note that "ספר" is used by MeBook's creator funnel (`research/carousel-research`, memory note of 6 Oct) and by these creators for other books; LumeBook's carousel code word is לומי.

## E4. Spontaneous purchase intent is rare, and it appears where a useful object is shown working

**Observed:** across 2,982 comments: 7 explicit buying intent, 6 self-reported purchases, 70 product or price questions (2.8% together). Per 100 comments: product demonstrated 7.1, product featured 4.9, no product shown 0.6. Within the 40 creators with a strong and an ordinary post compared, the strong post had the higher share of purchase-intent comments in only 7.

**Examples (observed):**
- lital_magnezi, a push light on a gan toilet door, no CTA at all: [Dd5sq3TI1I5](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd5sq3TI1I5/). 16 of 30 sampled comments ask for the link or where to buy, for example "וואוו רעיון מעולה איפה קונים?" (great idea, where do you buy?). One says she found it cheaper elsewhere.
- _shirley_unger_kids_activities, autumn picture books from her gan shelf: [DeKFbzRAmIy](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeKFbzRAmIy/). 9 of 13 sampled comments are product questions or "we have it".
- knafaim.ot_by_noy, board games recommended by an OT: [DdWglWIKur-](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdWglWIKur-/): "איפה קונים?" (where do you buy?).
- tal_strul, bedtime books (co-authored with a publisher): [DdwREvSCJg2](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdwREvSCJg2/): "קופון הנחה" (discount coupon), "אשמח להמלצות למתוקי בן 3" (I'd love picks for my sweetie aged 3).
- books_are_friends_slp, book gifts for age 3: [DbtoJF_iDU1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbtoJF_iDU1/): "אני חייבת את קווין הקואלה ל[שם]" (I must get [title] for [name]), the only explicit want for a named children's book in the sample.
- hen.kalimian.kids, letter-card game: [DdomwlZuYTj](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdomwlZuYTj/): one commenter asks to buy the cards although only a free guide was offered.

**Interpretation:** buying talk follows a concrete, useful thing shown in use, more than a feeling or a joke. **Limit:** counts are small; this is a direction, not a rate to plan on.

## E5. Children's books are already part of what these creators recommend

**Observed:** 18 recent posts by 9 creators feature or recommend children's books (posts.csv `product_category = children_book`): books_are_friends_slp (6), daria_mansour1 (3), hilla_gover_slp (2), tal_strul (2), and one each by _shirley_unger_kids_activities, hen.kalimian.kids, lital_magnezi, topaz_meir_slp, yeladim_ktanim. Comments ask for picks by age and for where to buy (E2, E4). Two book posts were co-authored with or tagged a publisher (tal_strul with @agambooks; hilla_gover_slp's list drew a joking comment from the same publisher's account).

**Interpretation:** a personalised book can enter as one item in a list professionals already make, in the same keyword-DM flow, instead of as a one-off ad.

## E7. Birthdays, the top-selling book, show up in creator content as personal tributes

**Observed:** 7 recent posts coded as birthday (5 analysed for comments, 98 comments): 86 are greetings, 0 identification or help requests, 1 purchase-intent comment. Examples: aba.maor's tribute to his son [Dd_6ZOfDjxd](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd_6ZOfDjxd/) (5,387 likes), sapir_ivan_mugatz's son turning 5 [Dd8FX7wt7Fj](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dd8FX7wt7Fj/). nofar_aizik's 4th-birthday party post [Dc0QYruIchn](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc0QYruIchn/) drew requests for the party-table product links (keyword), not for gifts. The birthday-party producer (natallyaharon) posts humour, not party content, in the recent sample.

**Interpretation:** the birthday is LumeBook's biggest occasion in sales (internal note of 12 Oct) but there is no visible conversation about birthday gifts in this content. A birthday post that shows the book as a gift would be new content, not a copy of something proven.

## E9. Sponsorship is common, undisclosed by Instagram's label, and comes with personal codes

**Observed:** 0 of 469 recent posts carry Instagram's paid-partnership label. Coded from captions and tags: 30 likely sponsored, 26 collaborations, 48 promotions of the creator's own product or service, 1 giveaway. Personal discount codes are common, for example "Lotan" 10% ([Dcjq3TmoN7n](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcjq3TmoN7n/)), "BARKAS" 20% ([DcjCHEkMWTR](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcjCHEkMWTR/)), "VANISH26" 25% ([DdCFaTJKIAG](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdCFaTJKIAG/)). Sponsored and own-product reels have fewer likes per 1,000 plays (5.2 and 6.0) than organic reels (11.8). One sponsored breakout (baryona, [DcjCHEkMWTR](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcjCHEkMWTR/)) has 277,026 plays and 125 likes, 0.45 likes per 1,000 plays.

**Interpretation:** LumeBook's personal link competes with codes these audiences already know. A like rate that low next to high plays is consistent with paid promotion (inference, unverified).

## E10. Price and real-world proof are almost never shown

**Observed:** price is visible in 7 of 469 recent posts. A child's reaction is the proof in 17 posts; a demonstration result in 52; an expert credential in 40. In MeBook's creator-collab reels (comments read on 3 Oct, `research/competitor-content-analysis/comments_mebook_hebrew.md`) the open questions were price, delivery time and how to order.

**Interpretation:** the questions buyers ask are not answered in the content they see. The 12 Oct note's guess that people drop at payment because they are unsure of what they get or of the total fits this, but is still a guess.

## E11. Popular performance does not predict buying talk

**Observed:** for the 40 creators with a strong and an ordinary post compared, the strong post had a higher share of purchase-intent comments in 7 and a higher share of substantive comments in 20. Popular low-relevance topics in the sample: couple humour (rivki_shashar [DdEhOlyqJ5P](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdEhOlyqJ5P/), 731,961 plays), comedy characters, travel (hatravelers_, median 86,155 plays), recipes, a pregnancy prank (hen.kalimian.kids [DdGLBpXOU6U](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdGLBpXOU6U/), 314,611 plays).

**Interpretation:** reach and buying signals come from different posts. A creator's biggest hit is not the best template for a LumeBook post.

**Observed:**

- yaffa_michaela_zukerman, caption of [DdoeylHDeQQ](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdoeylHDeQQ/): thanks "530 המשפחות" (530 families) who brought her book home, and closes sales. - A creator in the map wrote privately (email, 13 Oct, `creators/correspondence/`) that over 100 copies of the book she wrote for a competitor sold, mostly through her own Instagram community. Private and unverified.

**Interpretation:** creators with a trusted voice can sell books to their own audiences. How many LumeBook books a creator post sells is unknown.
