Back when I had three kids, three and under,
I started looking into preschool options for my son.
I was stunned at the cost
and started doing some quick math,
realizing that sending each of my kids
to a few years of preschool, even just part time,
was going to cost my family a staggering amount of money.
This wasn't going to work for us.
So I decided to look online
for some at-home preschool options.
I found worksheets, workbooks, computer programs,
and other loosely written lessons,
but nothing was as thorough or hands-on
as I knew preschool at home could be.
So I sat down, used all my professional skills
as a former kindergarten and first grade teacher,
and started to write my own program.
This eventually became Playing Preschool.
Today, Playing Preschool
is one of the most used preschool programs available.
It's been used by countless families around the world
for the last seven years,
and I can't wait to introduce it to you.
Playing Preschool is a comprehensive preschool program
that spans 190 days of learning over 19 thematic units.
The program begins with a startup guide
where I'll walk you through how to teach Playing Preschool
so you can be successful with your child from day one.
Each unit starts with a supply list,
suggested book list, and the unit's song and poem.
There's also a weekly overview
so you can glance at the upcoming lessons
and see how the week will progress for your family.
Playing Preschool lessons are all built
on a predictable framework,
providing you and your child
with a routine formula for success.
I'm so proud of Playing Preschool
and what it gave my family
and what it's given to countless others,
an affordable way to do preschool at home
that the entire family can enjoy.
