Once upon a time, in a little village, there lived two friends named Tom and Tim.
They were inseparable and did everything together. One day, they found a big chocolate bar. Tom said,
"Let's share it equally." But Tim replied, "No, I want it all for myself."
They argued and decided to break the chocolate in half. Tim took a big piece and ate it greedily,
while Tom took a small piece and smiled. Suddenly, Tim felt guilty and realized his mistake.
He apologized to Tom and they became friends again.
They learned that sharing brings happiness and friendship.
