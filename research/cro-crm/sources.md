Evidence types used: **LumeBook data** (internal), **observed** (seen on a live site this session), **code** (read in a site's public code), **usability research**, **controlled experiment** (randomised, field or lab), **observational study**, **survey**, **official documentation**, **law or regulator**, **legal commentary**, **vendor benchmark** (published by a company that sells the tool; commercial bias), **opinion**.
