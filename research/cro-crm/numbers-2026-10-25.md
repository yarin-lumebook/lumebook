## 3. The funnel and the products

- **Seven-day conversion, all mature cohorts (25 Oct):** 15,833 orders created, 1,945 paid within 7 days (12.3%); 6,606 contacts (a normalised phone number) created an order, 1,392 paid within 7 days (21.1%). Each person creates about 2.4 previews. The 12 Oct readout had 21.7% per person.
- **Reached payment:** no new number. Mixpanel is not connected to the admin ("mixpanel_export_too_large"), so 527 previews, 202 at payment and 85 paid (30 days to 12 Oct) remain the only stage counts.
- **What sells (2,092 paid orders, all time):** ready-made catalogue books 84.9%, AI-written 13.1%, written from scratch 2.0%. Top books: birthday 275, AI-written 274, "הלב של יובל" 177, "חברים צבעוניים" 124, "ביי ביי מוצץ" 87, "אח חדש" 75, the diaper book 68, "אחות חדשה" 61, "שלום כיתה א׳" 61.
- **Children:** 51.0% girls, 48.9% boys. Book language: Hebrew 91.7%, Arabic 4.5% (93 orders), English 1.6%, Russian 1.0%. Age 5 on 75.3% of orders, which the admin itself flags as probably a default value.
- **Older admin tiles (period not stated, totals match all time):** 1,495 buyers, 1.41 purchases per buyer, ₪198 revenue per buyer. Its "conversion" tiles (gallery 45%, AI 58%, scratch 64%) use a different base, orders that reached the editor, and are self-selected; they are no reason to push the AI path.

| Second book offer after purchase | 261 (234) | 62, 22.2% (all returning buyers) | not shown | $9.37, $0.15 |
| Back to school broadcast | 813 (813) | 1, 0.1% | not shown | $29.18 |

- The average message cost $0.036 in August (Messaging $0.0047 plus Channels $0.0312, from a partial usage snapshot), about $106 for these 2,956 messages in 90 days.
- **What it supports:** the plain reminder with no coupon is the strongest recovery message on both readings; the first coupon after it still adds something; the third adds little (12 Oct: +5.4 and +1.6). The ₪79 second-book offer is by far the best linked message. The one list-wide broadcast sold nothing measurable and is the kind of message the consent problem makes riskiest (`report.md` priority 3).
- **What it does not support:** causation. The analytics page says there is no random control group now; the marketing page compares with "the small group we deliberately left alone", and because extra purchases almost equal all purchases, that group bought close to nothing and is probably tiny. Sales from links and the book QR cannot be matched at all, because the site does not record which link brought an order.

## 6. What this changes in the plan

1. **Ads are close to target, so the funnel is now the cheaper lever.** At ₪550 a day, every point added between preview and payment lowers cost per order without new budget. Priorities 1 and 2 (`report.md`) stand. Keep the budget flat until the admin's one Control-versus-Challenger test has run.
2. **Measurement moves from "important" to "first this week" for the ads owner:** one URL parameter line on every Meta ad (`utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid-social&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}&utm_term={{placement}}`; confirm the parameter names in Ads Manager's builder), CardCom and the test hosts as unwanted referrals, and one purchase event (`measurement.md` sections 3 and 4).
3. **Recovery order confirmed:** reminder first with no coupon, one coupon at most, no third coupon (`crm-flows.md` section 6, X5).
4. **No list-wide broadcasts** until marketing consent is separate; after that, only messages with a reason (book left, birthday, purchase).
5. **Holdouts are now a fix, not a nice-to-have:** the admin itself says there is no random control group (`measurement.md` section 8).
6. **"למי הספר?" moves to do now** as one optional question on the thank-you page for four weeks (X8), because Meta credits 43 of 102 purchases in its age table to women 45 and over.
7. **The demo's birthday-book path is the right example:** it is the best seller, and 85% of paid orders are catalogue books, which supports opening the wizard on the book already chosen.

Still unknown: reached-payment counts since 12 Oct (Mixpanel), contribution per order, where the ads land, and what the "chatgpt / paid" traffic is.
