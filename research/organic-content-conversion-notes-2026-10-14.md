# Organic content conversion notes

Research date: 14 October 2026. Scope: practical measurement and Instagram experimentation. Primary sources read in this session; recommendations below are proposed working methods, not proven performance guarantees.

## Verified platform facts

- Instagram's Best Practices section is in the professional dashboard and covers creation, engagement, reach, monetization and guidelines; it includes personalized account tips. Its creation guidance addresses posting frequency, capturing attention and Reel length. [Meta, Introducing Best Practices, 1 October 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/)
- Trial Reels show content to non-followers first, and Meta says metrics including views, likes, comments and shares become available approximately 24 hours after sharing. Automatic sharing with followers, when enabled, is based on views in the first 72 hours. These are engagement/distribution measures, not purchase measures. [Meta, Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/)
- Google Analytics accepts UTM campaign parameters on referral links, shows campaign traffic in its Traffic acquisition report, and supports `utm_content` to distinguish creative versions. Parameter values are case sensitive; Google recommends including source, medium and campaign when tagging URLs. [Google Analytics, URL builders](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en)

## Proposed working method

These are recommendations to test, not claims that a format converts for LumeBook:

1. Define one intended action for each content item, such as viewing a sample, visiting the product page, or completing a purchase.
2. Start from an actual customer question or purchase objection, show relevant product evidence, then ask for the intended next action.
3. Build separate creative tests for discovery, demonstration, reassurance and a direct purchase invitation.
4. Use platform insights to diagnose attention and interaction. Use website events and order data to judge business results.
5. Give each link placement a consistent UTM name. A shared bio link identifies traffic through that link; it does not identify which previous post persuaded the visitor.
6. Log the topic, hook, evidence, CTA, link placement, publishing date, measured action and next experiment. Change one major creative variable when practical, and compare repeated tests rather than declaring a single post a universal winner.

## Measurement limits

UTM tags identify tagged referral visits, not every influence that contributed to a sale. Treat post-level attribution through a shared bio link as an assumption unless there is additional evidence. The source documents do not establish universal conversion benchmarks, an ideal posting frequency, or a guaranteed content mix.
