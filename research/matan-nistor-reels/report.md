# What makes @matan.nistor's Reels work

Read 09 / 11 / 2026. Internal study for LumeBook. Every number below is a public count or a coded tag with its denominator; "interpretation" and "hypothesis" mark everything that is not.

## Overview

**Sample.** His Reels tab holds 2,050 items: 1,553 Reels (25 / 02 / 2021 to 08 / 11 / 2026) and 497 older feed and IGTV videos with a different view counter, kept apart. The top 100 are the 100 Reels with the most views on the grid (8,233,826 down to 122,531), so this is his full discoverable Reels history, not a first-screen sample. 20 lower performers from the same months (median 0.62 times their month's median, against 4.7 times for the top 100) were picked as comparisons. All 120 were downloaded and coded from frames, captions, covers and sampled comments; transcripts are covered in "Coverage" at the end. Metadata for every Reel since 01 / 01 / 2024 (764) let some patterns be checked across 753 Reels.

**What stands out (observed):**

1. **Breakouts are built around something that happens, not something he says.** A story engine (escalating encounters, a reveal, a twist, an argument) runs through 54 of the top 100 and 1 of the 20 comparisons; 16 of the 20 comparisons are a claim followed by reasons.
2. **His strongest repeatable format outside cash stunts is the narrated montage:** his voice making one argument while the picture changes every second or two to act it out. 6 of the top 100, a median 13.5 times their month's median views; none among the comparisons.
3. **Comment-keyword CTAs collect leads, not reach.** Across 753 Reels since 2024, the 124 with a "comment X and I'll send it" caption drew 3.4 times the comments per view and reached his top tenth about one eighth as often (1.6% against 12.0%). None of his top 25 Reels has one.

Everything else in this report is a lead to test, not a cause: the top 100 also differ in topic, period, collaborations, pinning and possibly paid reach.

## Major findings

Each finding: what was observed and how often, examples with times, what speaks against it, a possible explanation (hypothesis), how sure, and a test. Examples link to the Reel; times are from the frames.

### 1. Something happens: a story engine, not a lecture

**Observed.** A story engine (a setup that escalates to a payoff, a story arc, a twist, a delayed reveal, an argument, repeated episodes with a rising stake, or a social experiment) appears in 54 of the top 100 (16 of ranks 1 to 25, 14 of ranks 76 to 100) and 1 of the 20 comparisons. "A claim, then reasons, then a close" is the whole structure of 37 top Reels and 16 comparisons. The coders judged the promised payoff delivered in 47 of 100 top Reels and 4 of 20 comparisons; in the 20 pairs, the top Reel delivered and its comparison didn't 6 times, the reverse twice. Filmed situations (street, skit or POV, vlog, tour) are 31 of the top 100 and 2 of the comparisons.

**Examples.**
- [DYJdtg5ovNb](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYJdtg5ovNb/) (#1, 8.2M): encounter after encounter, each with a small risk ("אתה עוקב?" at 0:04.25), and a running counter top right from 0:08 ("סך כסף שחולק", ₪200 rising to ₪2,460 by 1:18).
- [DZw0OnMu-rQ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZw0OnMu-rQ/) (#13, 650K, 224 s): a hotel-room argument with his partner; the dispute surfaces at 0:20, the upgrade is argued at the reception desk (about 1:47 to 2:53), paid for and revealed 3:08 to 3:42.
- [DYzLsUgoosB](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYzLsUgoosB/) (#21, 434K, the highest like rate in the top 100 at 35.8 per 1,000): "Pov: פתחת את הבית קפה הכי גבוה במדינה ☕" at 0:00, window cleaners on a hanging platform at 0:00.75, coffee made in the kitchen 0:18 to 0:41, the hand-off and the workers' reactions 1:02 to 1:18.
- [DaQeQSlIS8b](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaQeQSlIS8b/) (#25, 395K): a one-hour taxi in Switzerland; the passenger covers her mouth at 0:11, the fare "CHF 426.80" lands at 0:12.
- [DaNwAg0KME0](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaNwAg0KME0/) (#94, 133K, 140 s): a packing argument that escalates from "יש לך פה שני ג'ינסים" at 0:20 to its emotional peak at 1:48 to 2:00, where she lists what the move took from her (machine transcript).

**Against it.** Lectures still make up a big share of the top: 29 podcast clips and 11 stage clips, including #5 [C_tCtPvoT3x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_tCtPvoT3x/) (1.5M) and #8 [CsoiU5Ro_Bj](https://www.instagram.com/reel/CsoiU5Ro_Bj/) (972K, one static shot). Inside the top 100, the period-adjusted advantage of filmed situations comes mostly from the four cash stunts: without them, vlogs (4.4 times their month) and skits (4.0) sit level with podcast clips (4.7). Story-engine Reels sit at 4.8 times their month against 3.9 for the rest, a modest gap. And the comparisons were matched on caption topic, which pulled them toward his most common genre, the advice clip, so the 54 against 1 partly reflects how they were chosen.

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** A situation creates its own open question (will he pay, who wins the argument, what does the suite look like), so the viewer stays for the answer; a lecture has to carry the same pull with the claim alone, and most of his lectures don't.

**How sure.** Moderate: the clearest separator between top and comparison Reels, but the comparison set is small and was picked from advice-heavy months and topics.

**Test.** The same message produced twice: once spoken to camera, once as a short filmed situation with a stake and a payoff on screen (experiment 1 in `experiments.md`).

### 2. The narrated montage: one voice, a new picture every line

**Observed.** Six top Reels are a single voiceover over footage that changes every 1 to 2 seconds to act out each line: [DCgV2ujI0cw](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DCgV2ujI0cw/) (#3, 2.0M, 32.8 times its month), [DNWIV26oabB](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DNWIV26oabB/) (#7, 1.2M, 15.9 times), [C5DTQqpom4f](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C5DTQqpom4f/) (#10, 760K, 29.0 times), [DKwNYG7IRlN](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DKwNYG7IRlN/) (#11, 703K, 9.8 times), [DYgu19To8RP](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYgu19To8RP/) (#29, 366K, 11.1 times), [DEaOcTNoGLV](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DEaOcTNoGLV/) (#89, 137K, 2.5 times). Median 13.5 times their month, against 4.7 for podcast clips and 3.7 for talking heads. None of the 20 comparisons uses it. Of the five posted after share counts became comparable (Nov 2024), four have 2.0 to 9.4 shares per 1,000 views against a top-100 median of 1.6; DCgV2ujI0cw, posted 18 / 11 / 2024 as the counter starts, shows 0.02.

**Examples.**
- DCgV2ujI0cw: his podcast shot at 0:00 ("תאתגר את") and again at 0:02 ("תפסיק לעשן!" in red), a film clip for every other line: a gangster scene under "אתה מעשן כל" at 0:01, a man in bed under "מוקדם בבוקר?" at 0:03, a boxer at 0:05, ending on "מגיע לכם." in lime from 0:19.
- DNWIV26oabB: a phone filming a real queue at sunset, "כל אלה עומדים בתור" at 0:00, "מה באמת עוקף את כולם עכשיו?" at 0:02.5, then the walk along the fast-track lane from 0:07 and "תיירים שכירים" at 0:25. The argument (time beats money) is shown, not just said.
- C5DTQqpom4f: "אני חי בערך בסגנון חיים של 80,000 ₪ בחודש" on the first frame, then one clip of his own life per line from 0:07, and a question to the viewer at 0:27 ("לפני 4-5 שנים זה מה שדמיינת?").
- DKwNYG7IRlN: "ואז -" over a motorcycle POV, a pickup crashes into frame at 0:01, then a passage from his own book read over film clips ("אתה שוכב" at 0:28, "תיקח את הסיכון" at 0:49).

**Against it.** Six Reels is a small group, two of the six rely on third-party film clips (rights LumeBook can't use), and DEaOcTNoGLV shows the same device at only 2.5 times its month.

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** The picture changes as often as in his busiest edits, but every change is the next line's illustration, so the eye gets novelty and the ear gets one continuous argument; the viewer never waits for a talking head to make the point.

**How sure.** Low to moderate: strong numbers on a small group.

**Test.** A LumeBook narrated montage built only from footage LumeBook owns (playbook structure 2, brief 2).

### 3. The first line stands on its own, or it is a deliberate cliffhanger

**Observed.** The first spoken or written sentence makes sense with no context in 72 of the top 100 and 8 of the 20 comparisons (pairs: top only 6, comparison only 2). The comparisons more often open on a generic call-out to a group with nothing concrete beside it ("שכירים", "אם אתם...": 9 of 20 against 12 of 100) or mid-thought out of a longer podcast. The topic was clear by a median 1.5 s in the top 100 and 2.0 s in the comparisons.

**Examples.**
- [C_tCtPvoT3x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_tCtPvoT3x/) (#5, 1.5M) against its pair [C-VS6pZxd9p](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C-VS6pZxd9p/) (51K), the same claim about parents' money advice. The first opens "אני חי בסגנון חיים" and cuts at 0:00.75 to him laughing with a backpack under "של מעל 100,000₪ בחודש", then a helicopter at 0:01.25. The second opens "גם אני" then "אני מניח בדיוק כמוכם" over his podcast set, and later uses stock footage (a woman at a window, 0:16).
- [C3vlzLeImMa](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C3vlzLeImMa/) (comparison, 20K): starts mid-sentence at a flip chart, "ברגע שאתם תהיו אנשים מספיק חזקים" at 0:00.
- [DOqIOjNiISW](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DOqIOjNiISW/) (#22, 430K, 4,165 shares): "אל תלכו לבית הספר!" in red on the first frame, the host's "ממש ככה?" at 0:01.
- [C8CDQJvI18j](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8CDQJvI18j/) (#6, 1.2M): the whole list ("תחליף את זה בעד זה") is on screen from the first frame.

**Against it.** Inside the top 100, the 28 Reels without a self-contained first line sit higher against their month (7.3 times) than the 72 with one (4.1 times). Several are deliberate open loops: DKwNYG7IRlN opens on "ואז -" with a crash; DYJdtg5ovNb opens on a single word, "עם", with ₪20,000 in hand. Mid-thought openings also appear in top Reels ([DW_dpe4lGvB](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW_dpe4lGvB/), #83, "כשהייתי בשנות ה-20 הייתי בפאנג'ויה"). The difference is between an opening that is unfinished on purpose and one that was cut out of the middle of a conversation.

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** A viewer decides in the first second or two whether they understand what this is; a sentence that depends on a missing question fails that, while a cliffhanger with a picture that explains itself passes.

**How sure.** Moderate for the contrast with comparisons; low as a rule for every Reel.

**Test.** Re-cut the opening only (experiment 2).

### 4. Money, a number or a spectacle in the first second marks the very top

**Observed.** A money amount or number as the hook: 8 of ranks 1 to 25, 2 of ranks 76 to 100, 2 of 20 comparisons. A spectacle or striking place as the hook: 10, 4 and 3. A number visible or spoken in the first 3 seconds: 9, 6 and 5.

**Examples.**
- [DYJdtg5ovNb](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYJdtg5ovNb/): at 0:00.25 a close-up of ₪200 notes under "20,000₪".
- [C8j_5daiAdv](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8j_5daiAdv/) (#2, 3.3M): a crop to the cash at 0:00.57; "400" with "X2" at 0:15 when the prize doubles.
- [C_tCtPvoT3x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_tCtPvoT3x/): "100,000₪ בחודש" with a helicopter by 0:01.25.
- [C9cogjXo7ow](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C9cogjXo7ow/) (#49, 227K): "POV: הזמנת מסוק פרטי ב-23,000₪" at 0:00 over a backpacker; the booking receipt inset at 0:09 shows 5,900, not 23,000 (the number on screen does not match its own proof).
- [DW6J5jPEee2](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW6J5jPEee2/) (#71): ₪43,642.06 paid to the airline at 0:03.3.

**Against it.** Three of the eight money hooks in the top tier are cash handouts, a format that has little to do with craft; [C9cogjXo7ow](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C9cogjXo7ow/)'s comments are mostly criticism of showing off. Money hooks draw low like rates (cash giveaways: 4.1 likes per 1,000 views).

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** A concrete amount is understood instantly, without sound, and makes a stake obvious.

**How sure.** Moderate for "concrete beats abstract"; low for money specifically, which is his niche.

**Test.** For LumeBook the concrete thing is not money (reels never mention price): a name, a date or a number of pages on the first frame (experiment 2).

### 5. Provocation is his constant, not what separates hits

**Observed.** Coders saw disagreement bait in 73 of the top 100 and 13 of the 20 comparisons, an identity appeal (employees, graduates, parents, men, women) in 79 and 15, status aspiration in 73 and 15. In the comments, though, the two groups split: in 3,851 sampled comments on the top 100, disagreement and criticism is the largest category (824, 21%), ahead of praise (787, 20%), jokes or emoji (545), agreement (386), keyword entries (323), questions (260) and personal stories (195); buying intent is 21 (0.5%). In 331 sampled comments on the comparisons, half are keyword entries (164) and disagreement is 24 (7%).

**Examples.**
- [DeB4C26RjFU](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeB4C26RjFU/) (#74, 2,776 shares, 16.6 per 1,000 views): a selfie answer to a story question about a flydubai flight ("מה אתה אומר על הטיסה של פליי דובאי?" from 0:00), claiming business owners, not employees, saved the plane; the two most-liked sampled comments correct him.
- [DNS9eKGojP8](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DNS9eKGojP8/) (#23, 3,932 shares): "אני חושב שלנשים" at 0:00, "יותר מתאים להיות" over a TV clip at 0:00.75; the claim that women suit self-employment because of their hormone cycle.
- [DFsVHkjohR4](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DFsVHkjohR4/) (#87): a TV slap scene under "מתן ניסטור מנסה לחנך את עם ישראל"; each slap is labelled with a choice he mocks ("*נרשמה לתואר במנהל עסקים*" at 0:13).
- [C9cogjXo7ow](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C9cogjXo7ow/) and [C0ZSOdsth3G](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C0ZSOdsth3G/) (#9, home tour, "22,500₪" rent at 0:05): comments mostly mock or criticise.

**Against it.** Provocation is as common in the comparisons, so it cannot by itself explain which Reels break out.

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** Provocation is the house style that keeps his followers reacting; the Reels that leave his base are the ones that also carry a situation or a concrete hook (findings 1 to 4), and once outside the base they collect argument rather than requests.

**How sure.** Moderate for the comment split (the samples are first pages in Instagram's own order, not representative); high that provocation is common to both groups.

**For LumeBook.** Don't copy the provocation: parents and grandparents buying a gift are not an audience to insult. What transfers is specificity: a statement concrete enough that someone could disagree with it.

### 6. Selling Reels and reach Reels are different Reels

**Observed (account-wide, 753 Reels posted 01 / 01 / 2024 to 26 / 10 / 2026, views divided by the median of their month).** 124 captions offer something in return for a comment word ("תגיבו את המילה X ואשלח לכם", "וזה אצלכם"). They draw 2.0 comments per 1,000 views against 0.58, a median 0.94 times their month against 1.03, and 1.6% of them reach his top tenth against 12.0%. The same holds within each year: 2025, 25 Reels, none in the top tenth against 9%; 2026, 94 Reels, 2.1% against 16.4%. In the top 100: 10 Reels with a keyword, none in ranks 1 to 25, 7 in ranks 76 to 100, at 2.4 times their month against 5.9. Six of the 20 comparisons carry one. The 45 top Reels with no next step at all include 13 of the top 25. His own replies in the sample are short and repeated: "כנס להודעות 📲" (7 times), "זה אצלך ⚡️" (6), "צפייה מהנה 💻" (6), "שלחתי לך 📲" (3): a visible DM funnel entry, whether automated or not.

**Examples.**
- [DUvqlKuCKTl](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DUvqlKuCKTl/) (#85): he closes his club and announces a new series; a hand-drawn arrow points down to a huge "חדש" at 0:55. 4,119 comments on 141,736 views (31 per 1,000), the highest comment rate in the top 100.
- [DW6J5jPEee2](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW6J5jPEee2/) (#71, 172K) against [DXwPDBDI5yp](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXwPDBDI5yp/) (comparison, 26K): both Japan trailers end on "תגיבו "X" בתגובות לולוג המלא" (0:36 and 0:13.5). The first has a story (a cancelled flight at 0:01, ₪43,642 at 0:03.3, the detour through a border crossing); the second is a food montage with no stake.
- [DW_dpe4lGvB](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW_dpe4lGvB/) (#83): keyword "אומץ" for the full podcast; about 29 of 45 sampled comments are the keyword.
- [DXqklEJCOg4](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXqklEJCOg4/) (comparison, 30K): keyword "עשיר"; 38 of 44 sampled comments are the keyword.

**Against it.** The keyword may sit on Reels that were made to sell (episodes, guides, courses) and would reach less anyway; the comparison of one Reel with and without a keyword was never run.

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** The reach Reels ask for nothing or for a follow; the selling Reels ask people who already trust him to raise a hand. Instagram's comment counts reward the second, but the comment is an entry form, not interest from new people.

**How sure.** Moderate to high for the association (large account-wide sample, consistent by year); low on cause.

**Test.** Keyword on screen against keyword in the caption only, same Reel, judged by DMs and link visits, with views as the guardrail (experiment 3).

### 7. More cuts and effects don't separate the hits; edits that carry information do

**Observed.** The comparisons cut faster (median 4.0 scene changes per 10 seconds against 3.4 for the top 100; average shot 2.4 s against 2.7 s) and use overlay effects (confetti, stickers, money graphics) more often (16 of 20 against 45 of 100; pairs: top only 4, comparison only 6). Stock footage: 11 of 20 comparisons, 24 of 100 top Reels, 3 of ranks 1 to 25 (pairs: top only 3, comparison only 7). Several top Reels are a single take: [DUsT5o3CAey](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DUsT5o3CAey/) (#75, 104 s), [C2zZVCrIr7H](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C2zZVCrIr7H/) (#61, 23.5 s), [DNQ4rYNoEy-](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DNQ4rYNoEy-/) (#57, 15 s), [C6-zVrOII1Y](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6-zVrOII1Y/) (#15, 47 s whiteboard), [Cx0p6FeopEJ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Cx0p6FeopEJ/) (#72, 89 s with almost no cuts).

**Examples of edits that carry information.**
- DYJdtg5ovNb's counter (from 0:08) turns the street stunt into a score.
- [C0ZSOdsth3G](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C0ZSOdsth3G/): the only overlay is the green "22,500₪" at 0:05, answering the opening question.
- [DaQeQSlIS8b](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaQeQSlIS8b/): the fare overlay "CHF 426.80" at 0:12, then the meter close-up at 0:18.
- [C_tCtPvoT3x](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_tCtPvoT3x/): his own YouTube thumbnail inset at 0:30 as he names the analogy.

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** An edit earns attention when it answers a question or adds proof; cutting for its own sake and stock illustration add movement without information.

**How sure.** Moderate: the scene detector misses punch-in zooms and soft cuts, and pace was measured, not judged.

### 8. Very short text cards punch above their length

**Observed.** Account-wide, the 51 Reels under 15 seconds sit at a median 1.74 times their month (15.7% in his top tenth), against about 1.0 for every longer band. The top 100 hold six text-only cards: [C8CDQJvI18j](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8CDQJvI18j/) (6.5 s, 1.2M, 27.2 times its month), [DC9aQQIo1xq](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DC9aQQIo1xq/) (3 s, his matriculation certificate under a Q&A sticker), [C_xChcHIs9-](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C_xChcHIs9-/) (3 s, a list styled like a post), [DANj05nITho](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DANj05nITho/) (3 s, "useless degrees", the caption asks viewers to add more), [DNQ4rYNoEy-](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DNQ4rYNoEy-/) (15 s, three dated predictions over pull-ups), [DGddYCxIzMT](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DGddYCxIzMT/) (63 s screen recording, 14.9 shares per 1,000 views).

**Against it.** Five of the six sit at 2.2 to 3.6 times their month, ordinary for the top 100; one Reel (C8CDQJvI18j) carries the effect. Views on a 3-second Reel can include automatic replays (Instagram's view counting is not published in detail).

**Possible explanation (hypothesis).** The whole message is readable in one look and invites a reply (add to the list, argue with one item); short length also means many replays.

**How sure.** Low.

**Test.** A 5 to 7 second LumeBook text card against a 25-second version of the same list (experiment 4).

## Attention against a commercial next step

What gets attention and what asks for a next step are mostly separate Reels in this account:

- **Attention Reels** (ranks 1 to 25): 13 ask for nothing, 5 ask for a follow, none ask for a comment keyword, 1 sells his own offer. Their comments argue.
- **Next-step Reels:** a keyword funnel (comment a word, get a DM), an episode, a ticket or the club. They sit lower in views (keyword Reels 0.94 times their month across 124 Reels since 2024) and higher in comments; their comments are entry forms.
- **The bridge** in the strongest next-step Reels is a story that earns the ask: [DW6J5jPEee2](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW6J5jPEee2/) earns "יפן" with a cancelled flight and a ₪43,642 payment; [DUvqlKuCKTl](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DUvqlKuCKTl/) earns "חדש" with the news that the club is closing.
- **Not visible:** whether keyword comments became sales, how many DMs were answered automatically, or how many followers came from the cash Reels. Public views and comments do not show leads or revenue.

For LumeBook this means two kinds of Reel with two different measures: reach Reels judged by views from non-followers and shares, and לומי Reels judged by DMs and books started from the link, not by comment count.

## Method and limits

The full method is in `report_method.md`, `src/PILOT.md` and `src/CODING_BRIEF.md`. In short: views are the grid number (`play_count`, Instagram plus Facebook plays for cross-posted Reels); likes and comments are divided by Instagram-only plays; shares are comparable only from Nov 2024; saves, watch time, retention, reach and sales are not public. The top 100 is his full Reels history, ranked; the 20 comparisons were matched on caption topic and month before watching, and only 5 of the 20 pairs turned out to match on topic, format and length together (`src/pairs.md`). Everything here is correlation. Commenter usernames were not stored and @mentions inside comment text were masked.

## The three most useful findings

1. **Give every Reel a situation with a stake and a payoff on screen** (finding 1): the widest gap between his hits and his misses.
2. **Narrate one argument over footage that acts it out line by line** (finding 2): his strongest repeatable format, and buildable from footage LumeBook owns.
3. **Keep reach Reels and keyword Reels apart, and judge them differently** (finding 6): the keyword fills comments but rarely travels.

**Biggest unresolved uncertainty:** how much of his reach is paid. Nothing public shows promotion, yet several top Reels have like rates a quarter of his normal; if the head of this list was bought, its creative lessons count for less.
