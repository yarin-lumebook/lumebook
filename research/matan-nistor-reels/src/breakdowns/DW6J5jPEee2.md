**Interpretation**
- The cancellation and the large number in the first seconds probably gave a reason to watch and a story to share (276 shares); the food montage gave only a promise, which may explain the low reach even though the keyword worked as well.
- The keyword comment seems to work regardless of story; the story decides how many people see the Reel. The shorter food Reel put the ask on a larger share of the runtime, which may be why almost every comment is the keyword.
- The joke comment and the questions in the Japan Reel suggest viewers engaged with the narrative as well as the offer.

**What carries it (interpretation):**
- Conflict, proof screenshot and amount in the first 5 s (rows 1 to 4).
- A visible route (pins, flags, logos) lets a viewer follow the trip without sound.
- The one-word CTA is the same in both, so the lead mechanics are repeatable.

- The celebrity reaction clip at 5 s is unrelated to the route.
