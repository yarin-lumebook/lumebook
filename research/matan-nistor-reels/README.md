# @matan.nistor Reels teardown (09 Nov 2026)

What makes the Reels of Matan Nistor (Israeli entrepreneur and business coach, Hebrew, 205K followers and 1,984 posts on the profile read 09 Nov 2026) get views, comments and a next step, and what LumeBook can test from it. Internal study only: his videos, transcripts and the sampled comments stay on this Mac (`media/projects/2026-11-09-matan-nistor-reels/`, not in git, never re-post).

## How it was collected

1. Inventory: the Reels tab's own query (`PolarisProfileReelsTabContentQuery_connection`) read page by page from the signed-in built-in browser, 171 pages, 2,050 items. Post dates decoded from the media id.
2. Metadata: `/api/v1/media/<id>/info/` from the same signed-in tab, 4 requests at a time, no rate-limit responses.
3. Comments: `/api/v1/media/<id>/comments/`, first 3 pages per Reel plus replies under up to 3 threads; commenter handles were never stored.
4. Videos: `yt-dlp` (public, signed out) for 116 Reels; 4 that Instagram restricts for signed-out viewers (C8j_5daiAdv, DfZH_YXxZHr, C9xPfanv9eJ, DVBtI-yCEAk) came from the video link the signed-in page itself plays.
5. Evidence per Reel (`src/process.py`): 4 fps frames for 0 to 5 s, 1 fps contact sheets for the whole Reel, ffmpeg scene cuts, loudness per half second, Whisper turbo Hebrew transcript (local, greedy decoding, first 120 s).
6. Coding: rubric v1, six Sonnet coders, 20 Reels each, every coder given a mix of top and comparison Reels.
