# Five LumeBook Reel briefs from the @matan.nistor study

Each brief adapts one mechanism from his Reels to LumeBook, without his wording, persona, money stunts, provocation or third-party footage.

**Read first:**
- `CLAUDE.md`
- `lumebook.json`
- `shared/facts.json` (offer, top books, buyers, Hanukkah dates)
- `research/israeli-product-reels/src/product-facts-2026-10-15.md`
- the November reel system (`reels.json` defaults and lint)
- the Hebrew voice skill and its reel-script rules (`references/reel-scripts.md`, 09 / 11 / 2026)

**Rules every brief follows (from those files):**
- 10 to 30 seconds.
- No price, discount or delivery cost.
- No language options or digital copy.
- Yarin never speaks as a parent.
- No AI wording.
- No child on camera.
- LumeBook / לומבוק.
- The default לומי CTA, unless a test says otherwise.
- Hebrew without dashes, semicolons or hyphens.

Three briefs rebuild reels already in `reels.json` (1.4 serves as test 1's control, 4.1, 3.3, 8.3) because Yarin's copy for them was rewritten today; those keep his lines and change only the structure.

Lines marked **only if true** depend on something that must have really happened; drop them otherwise. No line here is a testimonial or a customer quote.

Product facts used:
- The child's picture on the cover and inside.
- A blank page for a handwritten dedication.
- A free preview after leaving a phone number.
- Nothing is printed or sent until approved.
- Home delivery within up to 7 business days.
- Last order day for Hanukkah 05 / 12 / 2026.

Re-check them on lumebook.com before posting.

## Brief 1: "סבתא פותחת" (a situation with a payoff on screen)

**Audience and objective.** Grandmothers who buy for a grandchild: in Meta's attributed purchases women 55 to 64 are the largest group (`shared/facts.json` buyers, internal). Objective: reach outside the followers before Hanukkah. This is version B of test 1; version A is reel 1.4 (Yarin naming the same facts).

**Who is on camera.** A real grandmother who really ordered a book: a barter creator or the family-shoot track. Her written consent is required. The grandchild's face on the cover may be shown only with the parents' written consent; otherwise her hand covers the face and only the name shows.

**Yarin's rule (05 / 11 / 2026): every reel includes him.** Open with his 2 s hook clip to camera ("סבתא אחת פותחת את הספר של הנכד"), then cut to her, or ask him for a one-off exception. For that reason this brief is not in the November calendar yet; it needs a real grandmother first.

**Opening visual (0:00).** Top-down on a kitchen table: a sealed LumeBook box, her hands with scissors already cutting the tape.

**Hook on screen:** "סבתא פותחת את הספר של הנכד"

**Scenes and timing (about 28 s):**

| Time | Picture | Spoken (her, in her own words) | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | Hands open the box | "הזמנתי לנכד ספר, ועכשיו נראה אם זה באמת הוא." (only if true) | Hook, top third |
| 0:03-0:07 | She lifts the book to the lens, cover full frame | Her first real reaction, unscripted, kept even if it is one word | Her words as captions |
| 0:07-0:15 | Three page turns, each held 2 s; her finger points | "תראו, הוא על הכריכה." / "והנה הוא שוב, בתוך הסיפור." (said while pointing) | Captions word by word |
| 0:15-0:23 | Blank dedication page, close-up of her pen writing | "פה אני כותבת לו משהו משלי." | Captions |
| 0:23-0:25 | She closes the book and holds it against her chest | Her last line, unscripted | Captions |
| 0:25-0:28 | Same shot, held | none | Default purple CTA: "תכתבו לי "לומי" / ואשלח לכם קישור ליצירת הספר" |

**Edit and sound.**
- One cut per action, no stock, no effects, no zoom tricks.
- Her own sound; music at 30% only where she isn't speaking.
- Captions in the safe zone (below the top 14%, above the bottom 35%), her words in white.
- The payoff (her face with the book) stays on screen; nothing new after it.

**CTA.** The default לומי card for the last 3 s and the default caption CTA.

**Caption.** "סבתא פותחת את הספר של הנכד 📚", then the default CTA line and the usual hashtags.

**Primary measurement.** Skip rate against reel 1.4 (test 1). Secondary: non-follower views, shares per 100 reach.

**Adapted from.**
- [DYzLsUgoosB](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYzLsUgoosB/): an act and the reaction held, 1:02 to 1:18.
- [DZw0OnMu-rQ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZw0OnMu-rQ/): the payoff shown on screen, 3:08 to 3:42.
- [DaQeQSlIS8b](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaQeQSlIS8b/): the reaction at 0:11, then the reveal at 0:12.
- Principle: something happens, with a stake ("is it really him?") paid off on screen (finding 1, playbook 4).

## Brief 4: reel 3.3 with the keyword earned by a deadline

**Hook on screen:** "עד מתי מזמינים / ספר לחנוכה?" (3.3's hook)

**Scenes and timing (about 20 s):**

| Time | Picture | Voiceover (3.3, unchanged) | On screen |

| 0:04-0:08 | Close on the circle; a small chip in the top right counts the days left | "עד 5 / 12. שבוע לפני סוף החג." | "5 / 12" large; chip, e.g. "עוד 16 ימים" if posted on 19 / 11 |

**CTA.** Version A: default לומי card and caption. Version B: caption only, which needs Yarin's one-off exception to the defaults.

**Primary measurement.**

## Brief 5: "סבתא, לחנוכה הזה" (a one-look card)

**Audience and objective.** Grandmothers choosing a Hanukkah gift. Objective: cheap reach. This is test 5.

**Format.** One still card for 10 s (the reel rule's minimum). Yarin's rule (05 / 11 / 2026) is that every reel includes him, so this card needs his voice reading it or his hook clip first, or his OK for an exception; it is not in the November calendar. The same text is on screen from the first frame. Background: the open book on a table, softly blurred, so the text is the subject.

**Card text (all on the first frame, inside the safe zone):**

> סבתא, לחנוכה הזה:
>
> במקום עוד צעצוע, ספר שהוא הגיבור שלו
>
> במקום כרטיס ברכה, הקדשה בכתב היד שלך
>
> במקום עוד תמונה בטלפון, הפנים שלו על הכריכה

**Spoken.** None; light music at 30%.

**Edit and sound.**
- No cuts, no animation; the text never moves.
- One line in the brand violet for emphasis (the first swap).
- Caption carries the CTA.

**CTA.**
- Caption: "סבתא, לחנוכה הזה 🎁", then the default לומי caption line and the hashtags.
- Optional first caption line, which adds a second ask; Yarin decides: "מה עוד הייתם מוסיפים לרשימה?"
- No on-screen CTA: the card stays readable in one look. This needs the same one-off exception as brief 4B.

**Primary measurement.**
- Views against LumeBook's Reel median for the month.
- Shares per 100 reach.
- Version B is the same three lines over three shots, 25 s (test 5).

**Adapted from.**
- [C8CDQJvI18j](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C8CDQJvI18j/): "תחליף את זה בעד זה", 6.5 s, the whole list on the first frame.
