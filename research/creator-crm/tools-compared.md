# Tools and approaches compared

One short entry per tool or approach examined for LumeBook's creator programme: one person, barter only (a personalised book for one agreed video, plus a personal link with one 25% allocation per sale that the creator splits between earnings and an automatic audience discount), Hebrew-speaking creators, sending stays manual and nothing may read Yarin's DMs or inbox.

All pages were read on 19 Oct 2026. Prices are copied from the vendor's own pricing page. Many pages show "$" without naming a currency; those are marked "$ (code not named)". Labels follow `sources.md`: **verified** (the page says it), **vendor claim** (the seller says it about itself), **inference** (my reading). Full URLs are in `sources.md`.

"Hebrew" below means what the vendor documents. "Not stated" means the pages read did not mention it, which is not proof that it is missing.

---

## A. Influencer and creator platforms

### CreatorIQ (enterprise)
- **For:** large brands and agencies: discovery, recruiting, payments, measurement, brand safety (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** scale and reporting for big teams (vendor claim).
- **What a solo barter programme would use:** nothing; built for teams. (inference)
- **Price:** not public, "Request pricing" only (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Traackr (enterprise)
- **For:** global brands: plan, activate, measure (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** deciding terms and content type per creator before activation, in its "Plan" step (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** the habit of writing the terms down before contact goes further. (inference)
- **Price:** not public (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Later Influence, formerly Mavrck (enterprise and managed service)
- **For:** influencer campaigns sold mostly with a managed service (vendor claim). The name history is on Later's About page (verified).
- **Does well:** content tracking and draft review (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** little. (inference)
- **Price:** not public for Later Influence; demo only (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Its help centre returned an error, so its stage names were not read.

### GRIN (mid, with a free plan)
- **For:** ecommerce creator programmes: gifting, affiliate, paid (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** a gifting-only programme type, and deal terms that "lock" once the creator accepts (vendor claim). One creator record with history and a commissions ledger (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** the locked-terms idea: the agreed deal is saved as it was on the day of the yes. Its reply automation needs inbox access, which is ruled out. (inference)
- **Price:** Free $0 a month with a 20% wallet fee and 10 gifts in total; Starter $200 a month (100 gifts a month); Growth $500; Scale $1,500; Enterprise custom. $ (code not named). (verified, grin.co/pricing, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Aspire (mid)
- **For:** creator programmes for ecommerce brands (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** the clearest documented gifting flow: welcome email, brief, creator fills in the shipping address, link or code, product fulfilment, content, payment; optional contract and post tracking. Stages can be added, removed and reordered (verified, Aspire help centre).
- **Would use:** the stage order, and the step where the creator types their own address into a form instead of a chat. (inference)
- **Price:** not public; demo form only (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Upfluence (mid)
- **For:** search, outreach, CRM, store integration, affiliate, payments (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** a mix of automatic and custom statuses (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** the split between statuses the tool sets and statuses the person sets; its automatic ones depend on email activity, so they do not apply here. (inference)
- **Price:** not public; custom quote, 12-month minimum on annual plans (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### HypeAuditor (mid, discovery first)
- **For:** discovery, audience and fraud checks, campaigns, payouts (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** custom statuses per creator plus separate contract and payment statuses (verified, help article). Content auto-tracking by hashtag and mention, but it says Instagram Stories are not tracked that way (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** separate small status fields (deal, content) instead of one long status list. (inference)
- **Price:** no amounts on the pricing page as read; a free tier covers basic campaign management (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Modash (mid)
- **For:** discovery, relationships, Shopify gifting, content tracking, payments (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** a lean creator list: five default statuses (Shortlisted, Contacted, Active, Ended, Blocked), labels, notes, a few custom columns and an activity timeline of status changes (verified, Modash help centre).
- **Would use:** the timeline, and the honesty that "Status updates are currently manual" even in a paid tool (verified). (inference on the lesson)
- **Price:** Essentials $199 a month (100 tracked creators); Performance $499 a month; Enterprise from $14,700 a year; 14-day trial without a card. $ (code not named). The page's yearly figures equal 12 months while also claiming a discount. (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Influencity (mid)
- **For:** discovery, relationship hub, campaigns, seeding, payments (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** nothing specific. (inference)
- **Price:** no amounts shown; 7-day trial (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Site in English and Spanish only (verified).

### Heepsy (light, discovery first)
- **For:** creator search with a small CRM and outreach email (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** little; its value is discovery, and LumeBook already has 343 vetted creators. (inference)
- **Price:** Free $0 (1 project); Starter $69 a month billed yearly or $89 monthly; Plus $149 / $199; Advanced $229 / $299. $ (code not named). (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Insense (mid, paid UGC marketplace)
- **For:** briefs, creator applications, shipping, content delivery with revisions, licensing for ads (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** the written brief, the revision limit and the usage-rights step. The paid marketplace does not fit a zero-cash programme. (inference)
- **Price:** Brand from $500 a month quarterly or $400 annual, plus a 7% to 20% marketplace fee; trial $650. $ (code not named). (verified, 19 Oct 2026)

### Archive (mid, content capture)
- **For:** finding tagged and untagged brand videos and Stories on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts (vendor claim).
- **Would use:** the idea that "did they post?" is answered by watching public posts, not by asking. LumeBook's weekly refresh already does this for Reels. (inference)
- **Price:** not public (verified, 19 Oct 2026).

## B. Affiliate and link apps

### Shopify Collabs
- **For:** recruiting creators, gifts, affiliate links or codes, sales tracking, payouts (verified, Shopify help).
- **Does well:** gift statuses sent, redeemed, fulfilled, canceled: a ready-made shipping sub-pipeline (verified).
- **Would use:** the gift statuses as a model. Only usable on Shopify; whether LumeBook's store is on Shopify, and whether Collabs accepts creators in Israel, were not checked. (inference)
- **Price:** no subscription price on the pages read; 2.9% fee on automatic commission payments, in USD (verified, 19 Oct 2026).

### UpPromote
- **For:** Shopify affiliate programmes: links, coupons, gifts, payouts (vendor claim).
- **Price:** Free $0; Growth $29.99 a month (2% of referral sales); Professional $89.99 (1.5%); Enterprise $199.99 (1%). $ (code not named). (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: only through Google Translate (verified).

### GoAffPro
- **For:** affiliate links, coupons, commissions, payouts (vendor claim).
- **Does well:** documents the exact mechanism LumeBook's offer uses: a referral link that applies the affiliate's discount at checkout with no code to type (verified, GoAffPro docs).
- **Price:** Hobby free (under 100 daily orders); Premium $49 a month; Business from $99. $ (code not named). (verified, 19 Oct 2026)

### LumeBook's own ambassador programme (already live)
- **What it is:** the store's own pages `/IL/he/ambassadors` and `/IL/he/recommend`, read from the store's public code on 14 Oct 2026: a ₪30 budget per printed book split between the follower's discount and the creator's commission, a personal link that remembers the visitor for 7 days, commission held 14 days, a dashboard per ambassador, payouts by bit, PayBox or site credit (`research/creator-content-conversion/report.md:517`).
- **Would use:** this is the results engine. It already attributes printed-book sales per creator. Nobody has read its data for creators yet (`report.md:519`).
- **Price:** none; it is LumeBook's own.
- **Open:** how ₪30 relates to the 25% in `shared/facts.json` (₪30 is 25.2% of ₪119), and whether any map creator is enrolled.

## C. General trackers and CRMs

### Google Sheets
- **For:** a row per creator. (inference)
- **Does well:** per-sheet right-to-left setting, with cell direction switching automatically for Hebrew (verified, Google help); phone apps; offline editing once switched on (verified).
- **Would use:** as a backup and export target, not as the daily screen. (inference)
- **Price:** no price on the product page; storage counts against the 15 GB in every Google Account (verified). Free use by one person is an inference from that.

### Airtable
- **Does well:** views, forms and automations (vendor claim).
- **Against it here:** Hebrew text shows left to right, the layout does not mirror, Hebrew is not an interface language (verified, Airtable help), and there is no offline access (verified).
- **Price:** Free: 1,000 records per base, 2 weeks of history; Team $20 per user a month billed yearly or $24 monthly. $ (code not named). (verified, 19 Oct 2026)

### Notion
- **Does well:** Hebrew interface with a mirrored layout and per-block text direction (verified, Notion help); phone apps; offline pages once marked "Available offline", but only the first 50 rows of a database download (verified). About ten third-party influencer CRM templates exist in its marketplace; their fields were not shown (verified listing only).
- **Price:** Free for one person with 7 days of page history; Plus $10 per member a month (verified, 19 Oct 2026).
- **Note:** a Notion connector is attached to Claude in this setup, so Claude could read and write a Notion database. Not tested.

### Trello
- **Does well:** a list per stage and a card per creator; phone apps can edit offline and sync later (vendor claim, 2017 post).
- **Against it here:** Hebrew is not one of its 21 interface languages (verified).
- **Price:** Free up to 10 boards; Standard $5 per user a month billed yearly (USD) (verified, 19 Oct 2026).

### monday.com
- **Does well:** boards, CRM product with offline activity logging (vendor claim). An Israeli company (not checked in this research).
- **Against it here:** paid plans have a 3-seat minimum, so one person pays for three (verified minimum, inference on the effect). Right-to-left documented for forms only (verified, 2023 post); whether Hebrew is an interface language could not be read (help page error).
- **Price:** Free up to 2 seats and 3 boards; Basic $9 and Standard $12 per seat a month billed yearly; CRM Basic $12, Standard $17, Pro $28. $ (code not named; set by billing country). (verified, 19 Oct 2026)

### HubSpot free CRM
- **Against it here:** Hebrew is not one of its 17 interface languages (verified, HubSpot knowledge base, updated 4 Sep 2026).
- **Price:** free for up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts; Starter from $7 per seat a month (verified, 19 Oct 2026).

### folk

- **Price:** Standard $24 per member a month billed yearly or $30 monthly (verified, 19 Oct 2026).

### Attio
- **Price:** Free up to 3 seats and 50,000 records; Plus $35 per user a month billed yearly. $ (code not named). (verified, 19 Oct 2026). Hebrew: not stated.

### Pipedrive
- **Price:** the pricing page returned errors. A Pipedrive blog post dated 22 Sep 2026 lists Lite $14, Growth $39, Ultimate $79 per seat a month billed yearly (vendor claim, not the pricing page). Hebrew: not checked.

## D. The approach LumeBook uses now

### The creator map (published artifact with its database)
- **For:** finding, messaging and tracking creators, built for this programme (`creators/creator-research/src/template.html`).
- **Does well:** Hebrew messages that know each creator's kids and moment, a check on grammar and on the offer wording, learning Yarin's voice from edits while locking the offer facts, matched-pair message tests, follow-up timing, saved views. No other tool reviewed documents Hebrew messages, offer locking or voice learning.
- **Weak:** the stages stop at "Content created"; nothing about the deal, shipping, posting or sales; no history; edits outside claude.ai stay in one browser; failed saves are not retried; phone cards are long. Details in `report.md` section 2.
- **Price:** no extra cost beyond the Claude plan Yarin already has (inference).

## What the comparison says (inference)

1. **No influencer platform fits a ₪0, Hebrew, no-inbox programme.** The ones with published prices start at about $199 a month for paid tiers; none documents Hebrew; their main automations (reply-driven statuses, unified inboxes) need the inbox access LumeBook ruled out.
2. **The parts worth copying are small:** Aspire's stage order and creator-filled address form, GRIN's locked terms, Modash's timeline and five statuses, Shopify Collabs' gift statuses, Archive's "watch public posts" idea.
3. **The results engine already exists in LumeBook's own store** (the ambassador programme). The missing piece is reading its data per creator, not buying a tool.
4. **Of the general tools, only Google Sheets and Notion document Hebrew right to left.** Neither offers what the current map already does with messages.
