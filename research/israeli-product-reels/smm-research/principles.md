# SMM research principles behind the playbook

Research of 18 Oct 2026. Four research passes read platform documentation (Instagram, Meta, TikTok, YouTube), peer-reviewed studies, large industry datasets and regulators, and traced popular statistics back to their origin. Every source listed was opened during the research; for most paywalled journal papers only the abstract was read, and the full notes say which. The full evidence, with dates, sample sizes and what each source shows, is in `smm-research/notes/`.

How to read a principle: the **rule** is what a Reel can be checked against; **strength** says how much the evidence carries (strong, moderate, weak or contested) and why; **sources** are the studies and platform pages it rests on; **for LumeBook** turns it into a filming choice. The table's whys cite these IDs. Most platform numbers come from paid ads, not organic Reels, and nothing found is specific to Hebrew or Israel unless the principle says so.

## 1. The hook (first 3 seconds) (H)

### H1. Pass the 3-second skip test

- **Rule:** Within the first 3 seconds the Reel gives a concrete reason to keep watching (a spoken line, an on-screen headline or a striking visual of the product or a reaction), with no intro, greeting, logo card or slow setup before it.
- **Strength:** strong. Instagram officially measures the under-3-second skip and uses it as a ranking prediction; the exact weight is unpublished.
- **Sources:** [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); [Instagram Adds Retention Metrics for Reels](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/) (platform); [Improving how reels are ranked and recommended](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through) (platform); [Instagram for Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US) (platform); [Instagram gives creators more insight into their reels' performance](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/instagram-gives-creators-more-insight-into-their-reels-performance/) (platform); [Your guide to getting started with YouTube Shorts](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/your-guide-to-getting-started-with-youtube-shorts/) (platform); [Capturing Attention in Feed: The Science Behind Effective Video Creative](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/capturing-attention-feed-video-creative) (platform); [Understanding attention on TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/mediascience-study-brands-memorable-tiktok) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Open on the finished book or the moment it lands (a child's face reacting, the name on the cover), not on "hi, today I want to show you". Read skip rate in Insights per Reel.
- **Overlaps with:** E1, T3
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H2. Make the value clear early (result or promise first)

- **Rule:** By about 3 to 5 seconds a viewer can tell what the product is and what it gives the child or parent (the result shown, or the promise stated).
- **Strength:** moderate. Consistent across TikTok, Meta and Google, but all from paid ads, all correlational, and none publishes full methods.
- **Sources:** [9 creative tips to drive auction ad performance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance) (platform); [Deconstructing the Power of Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/reels-creative-strategies) (platform); [The Science of the Hook](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/the-science-of-the-hook-how-to-supercharge-your-reels-performance) (platform); [Capturing Attention in Feed](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/capturing-attention-feed-video-creative) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Show the result first: the personalised cover with the child's name, or the child seeing themself in the book, then explain.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H3. Show the product early, but keep the brand light and repeated (pulse, do not plaster)

- **Rule:** The product appears early and more than once inside the action (in hands, on the page, in the child's lap); the logo or brand name is brief and recurring, not a static central card, and on screen for a small part of the Reel.
- **Strength:** contested. Recall and brand-link research favours early or late branding depending on the measure; avoidance research says prominent central branding drives skipping. The reconciliation all sources allow: product early and woven into the story, brand marks brief and repeated.
- **Sources:** [Moment-to-moment optimal branding in TV commercials: preventing avoidance by pulsing](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/moment-to-moment-optimal-branding-in-tv-commercials-preventing-av/) (peer-reviewed); [Deconstructing the Power of Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/reels-creative-strategies) (platform); [The First Five Seconds: Creating YouTube Ads That Break Through](https://business.google.com/aunz/think/marketing-strategies/creating-youtube-ads-that-break-through-in-a-skippable-world/) (platform); [About the ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en) (platform); [On the Development and Strength of Category-Brand Associations in Memory: The Case of Mystery Ads](https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=development+strength+category-brand+associations+memory+case+of+mystery+ads+Fazio) (peer-reviewed); [Understanding Creative Fatigue, as summarised in Creativity pays dividends](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-effectiveness) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** LumeBook is an unfamiliar brand, so the product (the personalised book) should be seen early and often, while the LumeBook logo or name stays small and occasional (on the cover, in the caption, one end card at most).
- **Overlaps with:** S1, K5, C7
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H4. Open with a person, ideally a face and a voice

- **Rule:** A person appears and speaks (to camera or in voiceover) within the first 3 seconds.
- **Strength:** moderate. Several independent sources agree on early retention; the Emplifi data shows the person effect fades by 30 s and reach was lower, so it is not a free win.
- **Sources:** [What drives Facebook Reels performance](https://emplifi.io/resources/facebook-reels-data/) (industry study); [9 creative tips to drive auction ad performance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance) (platform); [The First Five Seconds](https://business.google.com/aunz/think/marketing-strategies/creating-youtube-ads-that-break-through-in-a-skippable-world/) (platform); [Faces engage us: photos with faces attract more likes and comments on Instagram](https://www.news.gatech.edu/news/2014/03/20/face-it-instagram-pictures-faces-are-more-popular) (peer-reviewed); [Deconstructing the Power of Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/reels-creative-strategies) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Yarin's face or voice in the first 3 s (a short spoken line works even if the child is not filmed); a child's face is not required for the hook, and Bakhshi found kids' faces no more engaging than adults' in photos.
- **Overlaps with:** K8, S4, F6
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H5. Open a curiosity gap and close it

- **Rule:** The opening poses a specific question or an unresolved situation (what will the child do when they see it? how is the face made?) and the Reel visibly answers it later.
- **Strength:** moderate. Strong lab theory and official guidance; no Reels-specific causal data. The payoff must deliver, or curiosity turns to disappointment (Loewenstein).
- **Sources:** [The Psychology of Curiosity: A Review and Reinterpretation](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/PsychofCuriosity.pdf) (peer-reviewed); [The Teasing Effect: An Underappreciated Benefit of Creating and Resolving an Uncertainty](https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=The+teasing+effect+underappreciated+benefit+creating+resolving+uncertainty+Ruan+Hsee) (peer-reviewed); [Emotion-Induced Engagement in Internet Video Advertisements](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1509/jmr.10.0207) (peer-reviewed); [How to make Instagram reels](https://about.instagram.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/how-to-make-instagram-reels) (platform); [The Science of the Hook](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/the-science-of-the-hook-how-to-supercharge-your-reels-performance) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** "Watch his face when he finds his name" or "guess which page made her laugh", answered on screen within the Reel.
- **Overlaps with:** K2, R4
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H6. Deliver the hook in sound and on screen

- **Rule:** The Reel has audio (speech and or music, never muted), and the hook is both said or heard and shown (on-screen text or a clear visual), so it works with sound on and off.
- **Strength:** moderate. Official on muted Reels being less visible; the lift figures are paid-ads and unmethoded.
- **Sources:** [Deconstructing the Power of Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/reels-creative-strategies) (platform); [The Science of the Hook](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/the-science-of-the-hook-how-to-supercharge-your-reels-performance) (platform); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [What drives Facebook Reels performance](https://emplifi.io/resources/facebook-reels-data/) (industry study); [Capture attention with updated features for video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/updated-features-for-video-ads) (platform); [How to make Instagram reels](https://about.instagram.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/how-to-make-instagram-reels) (platform); [YouTube Shorts ads: asset specs and best practices](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Speak the hook and put a short Hebrew headline on screen; add burned-in Hebrew captions for speech. Keep headline text short when someone is talking (Emplifi's speech-plus-text dip).
- **Overlaps with:** E4, T1, T2
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H7. Make the first frame work as a cover: vertical, clear, native

- **Rule:** The Reel is full-screen vertical (9:16), the first frame and chosen cover show the subject large and readable, the headline sits inside the safe zone, and there are no borders, watermarks or other-app logos.
- **Strength:** moderate. Official on what is demoted; no study isolates the cover frame itself.
- **Sources:** [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [What drives Facebook Reels performance](https://emplifi.io/resources/facebook-reels-data/) (industry study); [9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance) (platform); [Unlock the potential of Reels](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Film vertically on the phone, pick a cover that shows the open book or the child's reaction, keep Hebrew headline text away from the bottom and right-side buttons, never repost TikTok downloads with watermarks.
- **Overlaps with:** E7, F1, F2, F3, T4
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### H8. Look like a creator, not an ad

- **Rule:** The Reel looks phone-made and personal (handheld or tripod at home, natural sound, real moments, direct address), not like a polished TV commercial.
- **Strength:** moderate for "original, native" (official); weak for "lo-fi beats polished" as a measured effect (case studies and platform claims only).
- **Sources:** [What is Instagram's Adam Mosseri really saying in his year-end memo](https://om.co/2026/01/01/what-is-instagrams-adam-mosseri-really-saying-in-his-year-end-memo/) (platform); [9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance) (platform); [The Science of the Hook](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/the-science-of-the-hook-how-to-supercharge-your-reels-performance) (platform); [Instagram for Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** This fits a solo SMM filming at home: real reading moments, real reactions, Yarin's own voice. Do not reuse other creators' clips.
- **Overlaps with:** E6, F7
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

## 2. Every few seconds: retention and interaction (R)

### R1. Earn watch time relative to length: cut every second that does not move the story

- **Rule:** No dead air or repeated setup in the middle; the Reel ends soon after the payoff, so most viewers reach the end.
- **Strength:** strong. Multiple official statements; weights unpublished.
- **Sources:** [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); [Instagram Shares Algorithm Insights To Inform Strategy](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) (platform); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [Instagram Adds Retention Metrics for Reels](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/) (news report)
- **For LumeBook:** Trim openings and endings hard; a 20-second Reel watched to the end is better placed than a 60-second one abandoned at 15.
- **Overlaps with:** R2, E8
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R2. Choose length by content, stay well under 3 minutes

- **Rule:** Use about 7 to 15 s for a single visual moment or loop, about 30 to 60 s for a demo or story, and never more than 3 minutes.
- **Strength:** moderate for the 3-minute ceiling (official); weak for any exact optimum (correlational, brand accounts, and better accounts may simply make longer Reels).
- **Sources:** [Instagram for Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US) (platform); [How to make Instagram reels](https://about.instagram.com/blog/tips-and-tricks/how-to-make-instagram-reels) (platform); [What Is the Ideal Instagram Reels Length?](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/) (industry study); [How Long Should TikTok Videos Be?](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/optimal-tiktok-video-length/) (industry study); [YouTube Shorts ads: asset specs and best practices](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Reaction or reveal Reels at 7 to 20 s; "how it is made" or "what is inside the book" at 30 to 60 s. Test length with Trial Reels rather than trusting a benchmark.
- **Overlaps with:** R1, E8
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R3. Keep the middle moving: a new beat every few seconds, information inside entertainment

- **Rule:** Each shot in the middle adds something new (a new page, a reaction, a step, a change of angle), and product facts are delivered inside an emotional or entertaining moment rather than as a dry list.
- **Strength:** moderate. Peer-reviewed for TV and internet ads; no published data on cut rate for Reels. Rules like "cut every 2 to 3 seconds" are untraced (see Myths).
- **Sources:** [Why Do Consumers Stop Viewing Television Commercials?](https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=Why+do+consumers+stop+viewing+television+commercials+Woltman+Elpers) (peer-reviewed); [Emotion-Induced Engagement in Internet Video Advertisements](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1509/jmr.10.0207) (peer-reviewed); [Creative Best Practices for TikTok Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads) (platform); [Unlock the potential of Reels](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Alternate book detail with reaction (page, face, page, face); say the benefit ("this page has her dog in it") while the child is enjoying it, not as a feature list over a static cover.
- **Overlaps with:** E2, F8
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R4. Put the payoff near the end, and make short Reels loop

- **Rule:** The strongest moment (the reveal, the child's reaction) comes in the last seconds, and the last frame leads back into the first; for Reels under about 10 s, edit a seamless loop.
- **Strength:** weak to moderate. One industry dataset on Facebook Reels; no official Instagram statement that replays raise ranking.
- **Sources:** [What drives Facebook Reels performance](https://emplifi.io/resources/facebook-reels-data/) (industry study); [Instagram Media Insights reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights/) (platform); [YouTube is changing how Shorts views are counted](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted) (platform); [The Teasing Effect](https://api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=The+teasing+effect+underappreciated+benefit+creating+resolving+uncertainty+Ruan+Hsee) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** End on the child's face at the reveal or on the closing of the book, cut so it flows back into the opening shot.
- **Overlaps with:** H5, K2
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R5. Give viewers someone to send it to

- **Rule:** The Reel contains a moment a parent or grandparent would send to a specific person: a gift idea, a relatable parenting moment, a strong emotion (awe, delight, surprise) or a useful tip.
- **Strength:** strong that sends matter (official); moderate on what drives them (peer-reviewed but news articles by email).
- **Sources:** [Instagram Shares Algorithm Insights To Inform Strategy](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) (platform); [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); [Improving how reels are ranked and recommended](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through) (platform); [Mosseri's year-end memo, via Om Malik](https://om.co/2026/01/01/what-is-instagrams-adam-mosseri-really-saying-in-his-year-end-memo/) (platform); [What Makes Online Content Viral?](https://jonahberger.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/ViralityB.pdf) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** Grandparents and aunts are natural recipients: build Reels around "the gift that makes a child the hero" moments and reactions that trigger awe or delight, not sadness.
- **Overlaps with:** C4, K1
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R6. Ask for comments with a real, specific question, and answer them; avoid bait

- **Rule:** The video or caption asks one concrete question viewers can answer from their own life (or offers a comment word that delivers something real), the account replies within the first days, and there is no "tag 3 friends" or "comment YES" for its own sake.
- **Strength:** weak to moderate. Comments are an official ranking input but not a top signal; the question effect is from Facebook brand posts in 2012; no rigorous Instagram study on caption questions was found.
- **Sources:** [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); [Popularity of Brand Posts on Brand Fan Pages](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.intmar.2012.01.003) (peer-reviewed); [Instagram for Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US) (platform); [Engagement Bait](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/) (platform); [Instagram Organic Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram) (industry study); [Instagram Shares Algorithm Insights](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) (news report)
- **For LumeBook:** Questions parents can answer from life ("what is the first word your child read?"), and comment-word offers that deliver something useful (sample page, price list) rather than bare "comment YES".
- **Overlaps with:** C2, C5
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R7. Earn saves with something worth returning to

- **Rule:** When the goal is saves, the Reel includes reusable information (a list, an age or occasion guide, steps to order) held on screen long enough to read.
- **Strength:** weak. Saves are an official input, but no study was found on what drives saves of Reels.
- **Sources:** [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); [Instagram Organic Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram) (industry study); [What Makes Online Content Viral?](https://jonahberger.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/ViralityB.pdf) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** "Gift ideas by age" or "what to write in the dedication" Reels; or put the list in a carousel, which collects saves better.
- **Overlaps with:** K4
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

### R8. Use Reels for reach to new people, and judge them on reach signals

- **Rule:** Treat Reels as the format for reaching non-followers; judge each Reel on skip rate, average watch time, non-follower views and sends per reach, and test new hooks with Trial Reels before showing followers.
- **Strength:** moderate. Two large datasets agree on direction (Socialinsider's engagement gap is narrow); the non-follower role of Reels is official.
- **Sources:** [Data Shows Instagram Reels are Best For Reach, But Not Engagement](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/) (industry study); [Instagram Organic Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram) (industry study); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [Instagram will let video creators make trial Reels for non-followers](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-will-let-video-creators-make-trial-reels-for-non-followers-160016725.html) (platform); [The Science of the Hook](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/the-science-of-the-hook-how-to-supercharge-your-reels-performance) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** Post the same footage with two different first 3 seconds as Trial Reels; keep the one with the lower skip rate.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/hooks-retention.md`

## 3. The call to action (C)

### C1. One CTA per Reel

- **Rule:** The Reel asks for one action. The same action may appear in voice, on screen and in the caption, but no second, different action is added.
- **Strength:** Moderate. The multiple-CTA result comes from one peer-reviewed study of brand posts (CSR topics, not Reels), and choice overload in general is contested.
- **Sources:** [Driving consumer engagement through diverse calls to action in CSR messages on social media](https://scholars.ln.edu.hk/en/publications/driving-consumer-engagement-through-diverse-calls-to-action-in-co/) (peer-reviewed); [Message content features and social media engagement](https://mural.maynoothuniversity.ie/id/eprint/13927/) (peer-reviewed); [Can there ever be too many options? A meta-analytic review of choice overload](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article-lookup/doi/10.1086/651235) (peer-reviewed); [Choice overload: a conceptual review and meta-analysis](https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1016/j.jcps.2014.08.002) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** A scrolling parent wants to spend little effort (one of Chernev's overload conditions), so give one ask: "כתבו לומי ואשלח קישור", not that plus "follow" plus "tag a friend".
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### C2. Ask for engagement only when it has a purpose

- **Rule:** Every engagement ask has a real purpose (get a link, answer a question, give a recommendation). There are no "like if", "comment yes", "tag 3 friends" or "share to win" asks whose only goal is the count.
- **Strength:** Strong that the policy exists (platform-official on both apps). Weak on how Instagram detects bait, since Instagram would not say which terms are penalised.
- **Sources:** [News Feed FYI: Fighting Engagement Bait on Facebook](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/) (platform); [Engagement bait, Content Distribution Guidelines](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/) (platform); [Helping creators find new audiences (Recommendations and originality)](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-Originality) (platform); [Instagram says using certain CTAs can impact post reach](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-says-using-certain-ctas-can-impact-post-reach/717732/) (news report)
- **For LumeBook:** Frame every ask as a service ("רוצים לראות איך זה נראה עם השם של הילד? כתבו לומי"). Never "תגיבו כן אם אתם אוהבים".
- **Overlaps with:** R6, C5
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### C3. Comment-keyword-to-DM is a legitimate conversion CTA within the API rules

- **Rule:** When a Reel's goal is a sale, it can ask for one Hebrew keyword in the comments, and an automated DM then delivers the personal link. The DM goes out within 7 days of the comment, is the only unprompted message, and any follow-up comes only after the person replies.
- **Strength:** Strong on the mechanics (API documentation), weak on the effect size (vendor correlation only), contested on reach risk (Instagram reversed its own advice within days).
- **Sources:** [Send a Private Reply to a Commenter](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/private-replies/) (platform); [Instagram clarifies advice on single-word CTAs](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/) (news report); [HypeAuditor x Manychat study](https://hypeauditor.com/manychat/) (industry study)
- **For LumeBook:** This fits the project's code word לומי and the creator personal link. The DM, not the caption, carries the link, and the DM must not call it a "קוד הנחה".
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### C4. Give a reason to send, and prioritise sends over saves

- **Rule:** When reach to non-followers is the goal, the Reel gives a specific person to send it to and a reason, tied to a real need. Save prompts are optional and secondary.
- **Strength:** Strong that sends matter for ranking (three platform-official sources). Weak that asking for a send produces sends on Reels: no direct study found, and a generic "share this" could read as share bait (C2).
- **Sources:** [Instagram shares algorithm insights to inform strategy](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) (platform); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [Instagram Reels Chaining system card](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/) (platform); Moran et al. 2020 (C1): a CTA aimed at a behaviour lifted that behaviour in Facebook posts [FB]. (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** Grandparents and gift buyers are the natural targets of a send: "שלחו לסבתא שתמיד שואלת מה לקנות".
- **Overlaps with:** R5, K1
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### C5. Ask an open, specific question when comments are the goal

- **Rule:** When comments are the goal, the Reel or caption ends with one open, specific question that a parent can answer from their own life, not a yes/no or one-word prompt.
- **Strength:** Moderate. The peer-reviewed finding is old and from Facebook, while the ranking relevance is official.
- **Sources:** [Popularity of Brand Posts on Brand Fan Pages](https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1016/j.intmar.2012.01.003) (peer-reviewed); Reels Chaining system card (C4): the chance you will comment is one of the significant ranking predictions [IG organic]. (see above); Social Media Today (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** "איזה שם הייתם שמים על הכריכה?" or "מה הילד שלכם הכי אוהב לשמוע לפני השינה?" invite real answers and give creators material for replies.
- **Overlaps with:** R6, C2
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### C6. Say the CTA and show it, after the value and inside the safe zone

- **Rule:** The CTA is both said and shown on screen, after the product or benefit has been shown, sits inside the safe zone, and is repeated in the first line of the caption.
- **Strength:** Moderate for ads (official but exploratory, purchase intent probably self-reported). Weak for organic Reels: no organic timing study was found.
- **Sources:** [Deconstructing the Power of Reels: How Creative Strategies Can Drive Success](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/reels-creative-strategies) (platform); [Unlock the Power of Reels Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/) (platform); [Instagram Reels ad specs](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** End the Reel with a short spoken line and a text card ("כתבו לומי בתגובות"), placed above the caption area, and open the caption with the same words.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### C7. Keep the sell light: little brand time, the product shown twice, a benefit named

- **Rule:** The brand name or logo is on screen for no more than about a quarter of the Reel, the product appears at least twice, and at least one concrete benefit or feature is said or shown.
- **Strength:** Weak to moderate. One platform source on ads, no sample size, and a likelihood-of-top-tier measure.
- **Sources:** Meta for Business (see above); Chae 2021 (C1): reward-based calls were less effective than invitations [FB (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** Show the child's name on the cover twice (opening and reveal) and name one benefit ("הוא מוצא את עצמו בכל עמוד"). Say לומבוק once, near the end.
- **Overlaps with:** H3, S1, K5
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

## 4. What is shown (S)

### S1. Show the product being used, within the first two seconds

- **Rule:** In the first 2 seconds the book is on screen and being handled (opened, a page turned, a finger on the child's name), not a closed box, a logo card or a still.
- **Strength:** moderate. Lab experiments and platform recall data point the same way, but none tests organic Reels or books, and ad recall is not sales.
- **Sources:** [Effect of Product Presentation Videos on Consumers' Purchase Intention](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.812579) (peer-reviewed); [The Impact of Dynamic Presentation Format on Consumer Preferences for Hedonic Products and Services](https://doi.org/10.1509/jm.13.0521) (peer-reviewed); [Brand collaborations: TikTok creators drive big results](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/brand-collaborations-tiktok-creators-drive-big-results) (platform); [Capturing Attention in Feed: The Science Behind Effective Video Creative](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/capturing-attention-feed-video-creative) (platform); [Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Creative_Codes_ENG.pdf) (platform); [Tailored campaigns](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/automation/tailored-campaigns) (platform); [Moment-to-Moment Optimal Branding in TV Commercials](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.1100.0567) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** open on hands opening the printed book at the hero page; keep the LumeBook logo small and later.
- **Overlaps with:** H3, K5, C7
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S2. Show the proof that settles the buyer's main doubt, in one steady shot

- **Rule:** The Reel shows checkable proof of the claim that worries buyers most: for a personalised book, the child's real photo next to the illustrated page, and the child's name in the printed text, held still long enough to look (about 2 seconds or more).
- **Strength:** weak to moderate. The mechanism (diagnostic information reduces uncertainty) is well supported in e-commerce; the specific format is untested.
- **Sources:** [On Product Uncertainty in Online Markets: Theory and Evidence](https://doi.org/10.2307/41703461) (peer-reviewed); Cheng (see above); No study was found that tests side-by-side (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** likeness is the doubt ("will it look like my child?"). The playbook found only 5 of 36 personalised-book Reels showed photo beside illustration (recounted 18 Oct); this is an open gap LumeBook can fill without a child's face by using a consenting family's photo or Yarin's own (concept 2).
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S3. If the price appears, show the whole price at once

- **Rule:** When a price is in the video or caption, it is the full price in shekels, with delivery stated next to it (for example ₪119 and ₪30 home delivery, or free pickup), never a teaser price with fees appearing later.
- **Strength:** strong for the legal rule (if shown, the total); contested for whether showing a price raises sales.
- **Sources:** [חוק הגנת הצרכן, התשמ"א-1981](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הגנת_הצרכן) (regulator); [Consumer Reactions to Drip Pricing](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2019.1207) (peer-reviewed); [The price does not include additional taxes, fees, and surcharges: A review of research on partitioned pricing](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcps.2015.04.006) (peer-reviewed); [Price Salience and Product Choice](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2020.1261) (peer-reviewed); [Lifting the Veil: The Benefits of Cost Transparency](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2019.1200); No peer-reviewed study was found on showing a price versus hiding it ("price in DM") in social posts or Reels. (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** open pricing is a gap in the category (5 of 173 Reels showed a price). Product facts: ₪119, home delivery ₪30, free pickup in Jerusalem. Because free pickup exists, ₪119 may count as the total, but say the delivery cost in the same breath to avoid a drip-pricing surprise; ask a lawyer before relying on "₪119" alone.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S4. Hands holding the product can carry the Reel; a face is optional

- **Rule:** A Reel where an adult's hands hold, open and point at the book (no face) is a first-choice format; add a face only when someone needs to talk.
- **Strength:** moderate. Faces lift likes in one large study and not in another; hands-holding-product images are linked to purchase intent in one large study. All on photos, none on Reels.
- **Sources:** [The Power of Brand Selfies](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437211037258) (peer-reviewed); [Faces Engage Us](https://doi.org/10.1145/2556288.2557403) (peer-reviewed); [Is a Picture Worth a Thousand Words?](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022243719881113) (peer-reviewed); [Vicariously touching products through observing others' hand actions increases purchasing intention](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23845) (peer-reviewed); [Creative best practices for performance ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** approaches B and C and concepts 1 and 5 are well grounded; judge them by buying questions in comments and DMs, not likes.
- **Overlaps with:** H4, K8, F6
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S5. No identifiable child's face in LumeBook's own Reels; consent and no identifiers when a creator's child appears

- **Rule:** LumeBook's own Reels show no identifiable child's face; a creator Reel with a child has the parent's written consent, never combines the child's face with name, gan, school or location, and shows nothing embarrassing.
- **Strength:** strong as a legal and policy risk rule (official Israeli guidance, Israeli privacy law, Meta policy). No study was found on whether child-free product Reels sell less.
- **Sources:** [חשיפת ושיתוף מידע על ילדים ברשת על-ידי הוריהם (Sharenting)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/sharenting_for_parents1/he/SharintingPost13Access.pdf) (regulator); [Calcalist report on the Authority's first guidance](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/rjufahgryx) (news report); [Expanding Teen Account Protections and Child Safety Features](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/07/expanding-teen-account-protections-child-safety-features/) (platform); [העסקת ילד או נער בהופעה או בפרסומת](https://protocol.co.il/child-work-show-commercial/) (regulator); [Sharenting, parent blogging, and the boundaries of the digital self](https://doi.org/10.1080/15405702.2016.1223300) (peer-reviewed); [#familygoals: Family Influencers, Calibrated Amateurism, and Justifying Young Digital Labor](https://doi.org/10.1177/2056305117707191) (peer-reviewed); [Adolescents' Perspectives on Sharenting](https://library.iated.org/view/PELEG2024ADO) (peer-reviewed); [Bar-Ilan University: FoMO and sharenting](https://www.biu.ac.il/en/article/584384) (news report); Bakhshi et al. 2014 (S4): photos of children were no more popular than photos of adults. (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** supports the playbook's no-child design for Yarin. For barter creators (29 of 36 personalised-book Reels in the sample show a child), add written consent, no identifiers, and the option of a hidden face; any customer's child's photo or illustration used in LumeBook marketing needs the parents' written consent (section 2(6)); ask a lawyer whether the Youth Employment permit applies to barter content with under-15s.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S6. Let real people vouch: creators, customers and visible customer proof

- **Rule:** The Reel carries a real person's first-hand use or verdict (a parent or grandparent who owns the book), or visible real-customer proof (messages, reviews, counts), rather than brand adjectives.
- **Strength:** moderate. Direction is consistent across platform data, purchase data, a meta-analysis and surveys; the platform numbers are self-reported paid-ad results, and the surveys measure intention, not buying.
- **Sources:** [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/370205/tiktok-says-creators-drive-social-ad-campaign-succ.html) (platform); [Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) (peer-reviewed); [Social Media Brand Community and Consumer Behavior](https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.1120.0469) (peer-reviewed); [The Effect of Electronic Word of Mouth on Sales: A Meta-Analytic Review](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.14.0380) (peer-reviewed); [Influencer Marketing: How Message Value and Credibility Affect Consumer Trust of Branded Content on Social Media](https://doi.org/10.1080/15252019.2018.1533501) (peer-reviewed); [Intention to Purchase through Parent Blogs: Evidence from Poland](https://doi.org/10.1080/15332861.2023.2234121) (peer-reviewed); [Mommy Influencers' Impact on the Purchase Intention of Followers in Türkiye](https://doi.org/10.30798/makuiibf.1588314) (peer-reviewed); [How Online Reviews Influence Sales](https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/how-online-reviews-influence-sales/) (industry study); [Global Trust in Advertising 2015](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2015/global-trust-in-advertising-2015/) (industry study)
- **For LumeBook:** pick creators who look like the buyer (Israeli parents of 1 to 8 year olds, grandparents) over bigger reach; in LumeBook's own Reels, show real customer messages or photos (with consent) instead of adjectives like "אמיץ, חכם, מיוחד".
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S7. Disclose the partnership in words, at the start

- **Rule:** A creator Reel says it is a partnership in words in the first caption line or on screen in the first seconds (for example "בשיתוף לומבוק"), plus Instagram's paid-partnership label when money or goods changed hands.
- **Strength:** strong as an Israeli legal requirement; contested on the trust cost (experiments find a modest cost, field data finds sponsored posts still engage).
- **Sources:** [חוק פרסום סמוי חל גם על אינסטגרם? מנדלבליט המליץ בחיוב](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3413075) (news report); Consumer Protection Law (S3 link) (see above); [פרסום סמוי באינסטגרם: תביעה ב-30 מיליון ש'](https://www.bizportal.co.il/advertismentandmarketing/news/article/742517) (news report); [A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Disclosing Sponsored Content](https://doi.org/10.1080/00913367.2020.1765909) (peer-reviewed); ["This Post is Sponsored"](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intmar.2016.12.002) (peer-reviewed); [Credible Influencers](https://doi.org/10.1027/1864-1105/a000310) (peer-reviewed); [Influencer Marketing on Instagram: Empirical Research on Social Media Engagement with Sponsored Posts](https://doi.org/10.1080/15252019.2022.2123724) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** the sample showed loose disclosure (70 of 173 with words, 4 with the label). Put the disclosure words in the creator brief as the first caption line.
- **Overlaps with:** T8
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

### S8. Show the personalising and the child as the hero

- **Rule:** The Reel shows the act of personalising (one photo, the name typed, the free preview) and its payoff (the child's own name and likeness inside the story), so a parent can picture their own child.
- **Strength:** moderate that customising raises the customer's own valuation (lab); weak that watching someone else personalise does the same (untested).
- **Sources:** [The "I Designed It Myself" Effect in Mass Customization](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.1090.1077) (peer-reviewed); [The IKEA effect: When labor leads to love](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcps.2011.08.002) (peer-reviewed); Hartmann et al. 2021 (S4): a lab experiment showed self-reference drives the response to images of an unseen person holding a product. (see above); [Personalised print books and family literacy outcomes: A literature review](https://cdn.literacytrust.org.uk/media/documents/2017-11-21_Personalised_books_and_family_literacy_outcomes_-_National_Literacy_Trust.pdf) (industry study)
- **For LumeBook:** concept 2 (the making-of) and a short shot of the free preview carry this; keep the ordering step brief and end on the printed page.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/shown-filmed.md`

## 5. Editing (E)

### E1. Open with motion and the point inside 3 seconds

- **Rule:** By second 3 the viewer has seen a change (a cut, movement or a face reacting) and knows what the Reel is about (spoken line, on-screen text or the book visible).
- **Strength:** moderate. Instagram states it plainly, and lab work explains why, but no source gives an Instagram retention curve or a tested 3-second effect size.
- **Sources:** [Instagram for Creators: Frequently Asked Questions](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US) (platform); [Emotion-Induced Engagement in Internet Video Advertisements](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.10.0207) (peer-reviewed); [Involuntary Attention and Physiological Arousal Evoked by Structural Features and Emotional Content in TV Commercials](https://doi.org/10.1177/009365090017003001) (peer-reviewed); [Capturing Attention in Feed: The Science Behind Effective Video Creative](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/capturing-attention-feed-video-creative) (platform); [What actually makes short-form video ads work? (Meta x Toluna)](https://tolunacorporate.com/what-makes-short-form-video-ads-work-meta-toluna-study/) (industry study)
- **For LumeBook:** open on the child's face as they see themselves in the book, or the book opening, not on a logo or a slow establishing shot.
- **Overlaps with:** H1, T3
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E2. Cut to remove dead air, at a moderate pace, within one scene

- **Rule:** Talking or demo sections use seamless jump cuts that remove pauses within the same scene, at roughly one cut every 2 to 5 seconds; the Reel avoids rapid unrelated scene changes, especially over emotional moments.
- **Strength:** moderate. Consistent across 30 years of lab work and one short-form experiment, but none of it is on organic Instagram behaviour and the short-form study is small.
- **Sources:** [The Effects of Edits on Arousal, Attention, and Memory for Television Messages: When an Edit Is an Edit Can an Edit Be Too Much?](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15506878jobem4401_7) (peer-reviewed); [The effects of production pacing and arousing content on the information processing of television messages](https://doi.org/10.1080/08838159909364504) (peer-reviewed); [The Effects of Related and Unrelated Cuts on Television Viewers' Attention, Processing Capacity, and Memory](https://doi.org/10.1177/009365093020001001) (peer-reviewed); [Jump Cut Editing Style and Transition Frequency Differentially Affect Interactive and Sustained Engagement in Short-Form Video](https://archives.marketing-trends-congress.com/2026/pages/PDF/paper_professor_DOST_HUANG.pdf) (conference paper)
- **For LumeBook:** for a parent talking to camera, cut out pauses and false starts; for the child's reaction, hold the shot and do not stack fast cuts on top of an already emotional moment.
- **Overlaps with:** R3, F8
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E3. Use speed changes on objects and movement, not on people "enjoying" the product

- **Rule:** Slow motion, if used, is applied to pleasant, detailed movement (pages turning, a gift being unwrapped) and not to a person visibly enjoying the product; fast motion is reserved for energetic, exciting sequences.
- **Strength:** moderate. Two multi-study papers in a top journal, and they fit together (slow motion helps objects and movement, can hurt human endorsers).
- **Sources:** [The Art of Slowness: Slow Motion Enhances Consumer Evaluations by Increasing Processing Fluency](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231179187) (peer-reviewed); [The Effect of Slow Motion Video on Consumer Inference](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437211025054) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** slow motion on the personalised page reveal or the cover in hand is supported; slow motion on a parent's or child's delighted face risks looking staged.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E4. Build for sound on, and caption the speech in short synced chunks

- **Rule:** The Reel carries its message in audio (voice or natural sound), is never silent, and shows short on-screen text or captions timed to the speech, inside the safe zone, never filling most of the frame.
- **Strength:** moderate. The sound-on share comes from Meta and TikTok without published methods; captions have strong lab support, but full duplicate text has a counter-finding.
- **Sources:** [Unlock the power of Reel ads](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/) (platform); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (platform); [Instagram's "How to build effective Reels ads" guide](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagrams-how-to-build-effective-reels-ads-leitfaden) (news report); [Kantar report: How brands are making noise and driving impact with sound on TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/kantar-report-how-brands-are-making-noise-and-driving-impact-with-sound-on-tiktok) (platform); Meta x Toluna study (see above); [Video Captions Benefit Everyone](https://doi.org/10.1177/2372732215602130) (peer-reviewed); [Cognitive constraints on multimedia learning: When presenting more material results in less understanding](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.93.1.187) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** record a clear Hebrew voice (parent or Yarin) or the child's real reaction audio; add short Hebrew captions synced to speech for the silent minority; keep text off the bottom UI area.
- **Overlaps with:** H6, T1, T2
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E5. Choose audio the account may use; treat trending audio as optional

- **Rule:** Music in a LumeBook Reel comes from Meta's Sound Collection or is original audio, and a trending sound is used only if it is available to the account, fits the moment and is still early in its trend.
- **Strength:** strong for the licensing constraint (official), weak for any reach gain from trending audio (official but unquantified), moderate for music fit.
- **Sources:** [Access to the licensed music library on Instagram](https://help.instagram.com/402084904469945) (platform); Instagram creator FAQ (see above); Instagram Ranking Explained (see above); [Finding success on reels in 2025](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram) (platform); [Evaluating Empirical Research into Music in Advertising: A Congruity Perspective](https://doi.org/10.2501/s0021849907070055) (peer-reviewed); [Sprout Social Index 2025 release](https://www.cmswire.com/the-wire/sprout-social-research-reveals-consumers-think-jumping-on-viral-trends-is-embarrassing-for-brands/) (industry study)
- **For LumeBook:** check the account type before planning a trend recreation; if the track is not offered, use original voice or Sound Collection rather than a ripped track; pick music that matches the moment (warm for a gift reveal, playful for a funny kid moment).
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E6. Look native, but technically clean

- **Rule:** The Reel looks like a phone video from a real home (direct to camera, natural light, real setting), yet is sharp, well lit, full-screen 9:16 and uploaded in high resolution, with no borders or letterboxing.
- **Strength:** contested. Instagram says technical quality matters; an ad study favours a native, lower-fidelity style; image research favours professional photos. The sources reconcile if "lo-fi" means style (phone, direct address, real home), not poor focus or light.
- **Sources:** Instagram creator FAQ (see above); Instagram Ranking Explained (see above); Meta x Toluna study (see above); [Is a Picture Worth a Thousand Words? An Empirical Study of Image Content and Social Media Engagement](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022243719881113) (peer-reviewed); Unlock the power of Reel ads (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** shoot on the phone at home in daylight, lens cleaned, export at 1080x1920, no studio look, no frames or black bars.
- **Overlaps with:** H8, F7
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E7. Every Reel is original: own footage, no foreign watermark, real added value

- **Rule:** The Reel is LumeBook's own footage (or a creator's, posted as a Collab), carries no other app's watermark, and if it recreates a trend it adds its own scene, voice or commentary.
- **Strength:** strong. Repeated, specific platform statements across 2023 to 2026.
- **Sources:** [Helping Creators Find New Audiences](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality) (platform); Instagram creator FAQ (see above); Instagram Ranking Explained (see above); [New Instagram Policies Target Reposted Content](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/) (news report); Finding success on reels in 2025 (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** export from CapCut only without its watermark, or edit in Instagram or Edits; repost creator videos through Collab or the repost button, never as a re-upload; trend recreations must use LumeBook's own child, book and Hebrew voice.
- **Overlaps with:** H7, F1, F2, F3, T4
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### E8. Stay under 3 minutes, end cleanly, and do not count on loops

- **Rule:** The Reel is under 3 minutes (ideally as short as the story allows), and ends on its payoff or call to action without a dead tail; a seamless loop is optional, not a target.
- **Strength:** strong for the 3-minute cap; weak for any best length; weak for loop endings (no direct evidence found).
- **Sources:** Instagram creator FAQ (see above); Finding success on reels in 2025 (see above); [Instagram Officially Expands Reels Length to 3 Minutes](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-officially-expands-reels-length-to-3-minutes/737766/) (platform); [What Is the Ideal Instagram Reels Length?](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/) (industry study); [What Drives Virality (Sharing) of Online Digital Content?](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022242919841034) (peer-reviewed); Instagram Ranking Explained (see above); [Instagram Media Insights reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights/) (platform); Dost and Huang 2026 (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** most product Reels should fit 15 to 60 s; a reveal-to-reaction story rarely needs more; cut the last frame right after the payoff or CTA.
- **Overlaps with:** R1, R2
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

## 6. What the content is about (K)

### K1. Aim for a high-arousal positive feeling: surprise, joy, awe, warmth, amusement

- **Rule:** The Reel builds to one clear moment of surprise, delight, awe, warmth or laughter, and does not rest on sadness or calm information.
- **Strength:** strong. Large field data and experiments in top journals point the same way, though none measured Instagram Reels.
- **Sources:** [What Makes Online Content Viral?](https://jonahberger.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/ViralityB.pdf) (peer-reviewed); What Drives Virality (Sharing) of Online Digital Content? (see above); Emotion-Induced Engagement in Internet Video Advertisements (see above); [What Holds Attention? Linguistic Drivers of Engagement](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231152880) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** the core emotional asset is the child's surprise at seeing their own name and face in a story, and a grandparent's warmth; tears are fine when they are happy tears.
- **Overlaps with:** R5, C4
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K2. Tell a mini story: a character, a want, a turn, a payoff

- **Rule:** The Reel has a recognisable character (child, parent, grandparent), a setup, a turn or reveal, and a payoff, rather than a list of product features.
- **Strength:** strong for narrative persuasion in ads; moderate for transfer to 15 to 60 s Reels, which leave little room for a long arc.
- **Sources:** [The role of transportation in the persuasiveness of public narratives](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.79.5.701) (peer-reviewed); [The Extended Transportation-Imagery Model: A Meta-Analysis of the Antecedents and Consequences of Consumers' Narrative Transportation](https://doi.org/10.1086/673383) (peer-reviewed); [Why Narrative Ads Work: An Integrated Process Explanation](https://doi.org/10.1080/00913367.2016.1268984) (peer-reviewed); [Self-Referencing and Persuasion: Narrative Transportation versus Analytical Elaboration](https://doi.org/10.1086/510216) (peer-reviewed); Tellis et al. 2019 (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** "the child does not believe the book is about them, then finds their name, then reads it to grandma" is a full arc in 20 seconds; problem-solution framing fits here (a bedtime struggle, then the book that the child asks for every night). No peer-reviewed source on problem-solution format in short video specifically was found.
- **Overlaps with:** H5, R4
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K3. Use relatable, everyday humour

- **Rule:** When the Reel is funny, the joke comes from a recognisable parenting or kid moment the viewer has lived, not from a gag unrelated to family life.
- **Strength:** strong. Platform statement plus a meta-analysis and a large field study.
- **Sources:** Instagram creator FAQ (see above); [A meta-analysis of humor in advertising](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11747-008-0096-y) (peer-reviewed); [Advertising Content and Consumer Engagement on Social Media: Evidence from Facebook](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2017.2902) (peer-reviewed); Berger and Milkman (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** funny kid reactions, sibling jealousy over who got a book, a parent's honest bedtime chaos; keep the developmental or expert line serious, since humour lowers credibility.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K4. Give practical value, but wrap it in emotion or personality

- **Rule:** Any useful tip or fact (reading at bedtime, why seeing their own name helps a child) is delivered inside a feeling, a story or a laugh, and any developmental claim is modest and checkable.
- **Strength:** contested in isolation (usefulness helps email sharing but hurts video-ad sharing), consistent when combined (information plus emotion beats either alone).
- **Sources:** Berger and Milkman (see above); Tellis et al. 2019 (see above); Lee (see above); [Valuable Virality](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmr.13.0350) (peer-reviewed); [Reading personalized books with preschool children enhances their word acquisition](https://doi.org/10.1177/0142723714534221) (peer-reviewed); Helping Creators Find New Audiences (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** a parent tip like "kids ask for the book with their name again and again" works when shown through a real child asking; avoid claims like "boosts IQ".
- **Overlaps with:** R7
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K5. Make the product part of the story; keep brand talk short

- **Rule:** The book is shown in use as part of the action (being opened, read, given), and explicit brand messaging (logo, name, offer) takes no more than about a quarter of the running time.
- **Strength:** contested. Sharing studies penalise early, heavy branding; purchase-intent studies reward an early brand and message. Showing the product early as part of the story, with little brand talk, satisfies both.
- **Sources:** Valuable Virality (see above); [Why, When, and How Much to Entertain Consumers in Advertisements? A Web-Based Facial Tracking Field Study](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2014.0854) (peer-reviewed); Tellis et al. 2019 (see above); Meta x Toluna study (see above); Instagram creator FAQ (see above)
- **For LumeBook:** the personalised book is the visible product from the first seconds, but "לומבוק" and the offer appear briefly (caption, last beat, Collab tag); giveaway Reels will mostly reach existing followers only.
- **Overlaps with:** H3, S1, C7
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K6. Speak to a real parenting concern or make parents feel "not alone"

- **Rule:** The Reel connects to something parents of 1 to 8 year olds actually deal with (bedtime, routines, emotions, a new sibling, starting kindergarten, reading) and leaves the viewer feeling understood or helped, without hype.
- **Strength:** moderate. Several surveys agree that parents seek information and reassurance online, but most are US, several are a decade old, and none isolates Instagram Reels.
- **Sources:** [Parents and Social Media](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2015/07/16/parents-and-social-media/) (industry study); [Parents on social media: Likes and dislikes of sharenting](https://mottpoll.org/reports-surveys/parents-social-media-likes-and-dislikes-sharenting) (industry study); [Parents' attitudes and experiences related to digital technology](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2020/07/28/parents-attitudes-and-experiences-related-to-digital-technology/) (industry study); [The Use of Social Media as a Mechanism of Social Support in Parents](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10826-017-0716-6) (peer-reviewed); [Consumed by influence: the role of social media in shaping maternal identity and product choices during complementary feeding](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/communication/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2025.1730534/full) (peer-reviewed); [Forums for Parents of Young Children: Parents' Online Conversations in Israel and France](https://doi.org/10.54195/ijpe.18228) (peer-reviewed); [Digital 2026: Israel](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-israel) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** frame Reels around social-emotional moments that Israeli parents talk about (a new sibling, first day at the gan, confidence, bedtime) and show the book as a small, honest help; the viewer is most likely an Israeli woman on Instagram.
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K7. Tie the Reel to a buying moment and to the person who buys

- **Rule:** The Reel names or shows a gifting or life moment (birthday, new sibling, holiday, first day of kindergarten, grandparent visit) and makes clear who would buy it (parent or grandparent).
- **Strength:** weak to moderate. The life-change mechanism is peer-reviewed but old; gifting numbers are industry surveys outside Israel; no study on birthdays or milestones as triggers for children's books was found.
- **Sources:** [Life Status Changes and Changes in Consumer Preferences and Satisfaction](https://doi.org/10.1086/209014) (peer-reviewed); [International Kids Gifting Behaviours (Giraffe Insights)](https://toyworldmag.co.uk/?p=192007) (industry study); [Grandparents Embrace Changing Attitudes and Technology (2018 AARP Grandparenting Study)](https://www.aarp.org/research/topics/life/info-2019/aarp-grandparenting-study.html) (industry study); [How Instagram Boosts Brands and Drives Sales](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/how-instagram-boosts-brands-and-drives-sales) (platform)
- **For LumeBook:** build calendar Reels around birthdays, a new sibling, Rosh Hashana, Hanukkah and the start of the school year, and make some Reels for grandparents (the gift moment, a grandchild far away).
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

### K8. Put a real face, ideally the child's reaction, on screen

- **Rule:** A real person's face is visible for most of the Reel, with the child's reaction as the emotional centre, and a parent speaking to camera when there is talking.
- **Strength:** contested. Faces help on Instagram photos in one large study and not in another; for video the support is indirect.
- **Sources:** [Faces engage us: Photos with faces attract more likes and comments on Instagram](https://doi.org/10.1145/2556288.2557403) (peer-reviewed); Li and Xie 2019 (see above); Tellis et al. 2019 (see above); Meta x Toluna study (see above); [Slapping Cats, Bopping Heads, and Oreo Shakes: Understanding Indicators of Virality in TikTok Short Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.02452) (peer-reviewed)
- **For LumeBook:** frame close-ups or medium shots of the child's face at the reveal; when someone speaks, they look at the lens.
- **Overlaps with:** H4, S4, F6
- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/edit-topic.md`

## 8. Text on screen and the caption (T)

### T1. Make the Reel work with sound off: burned-in Hebrew captions or key text

- **Rule:** Anything said that carries the message also appears as burned-in Hebrew text, so a muted viewer gets the point.
- **Strength:** Moderate. Captions aiding comprehension is strong in lab work, and the sound-off share for Reels is lower than often claimed.
- **Sources:** [Video captions benefit everyone](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5214590/) (peer-reviewed); [80% of video caption users aren't hearing impaired, finds Verizon](https://www.streamingmedia.com/Articles/News/Online-Video-News/80-of-Video-Caption-Users-Arent-Hearing-Impaired-Finds-Verizon-131860.aspx) (industry study); [Capture attention with updated features for video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/updated-features-for-video-ads) (platform); [Unlock the Power of Reels Ads](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/) (platform); [Instagram is rolling out auto-generated captions](https://techcrunch.com/2022/03/01/instagram-is-rolling-out-auto-generated-captions-for-videos/amp/)

- **Overlaps with:** H6, E4, T2

### T2. On-screen text shows key words, not a full transcript, and does not compete with a busy picture

- **Rule:** Captions show short key phrases synced to speech, not a transcript, and no full sentence sits on screen while the product is being demonstrated.

- **Full evidence:** `smm-research/notes/cta-text.md`

### T5. Hebrew text stays long enough to read, in big type with strong contrast
