# LumeBook product facts used in the concepts

Read on 15 Oct 2026 from https://lumebook.com/IL/he (public homepage and its FAQ, fetched with curl). Re-check before posting: prices and delivery times change.

- How it works (site steps): 1. choose a book, 2. upload a photo, 3. see a free preview, 4. liked it? pay and get the full book.
- Site wording: creating the book and the preview are free, you pay only if you liked it ("יצירת הספר והתצוגה המקדימה בחינם ... משלמים רק אם אהבתם").
- Printed personalised book: ₪119 (shown next to a struck-through ₪149), includes a free digital copy sent by email within minutes.
- Home delivery in Israel: up to 7 business days, plus ₪30, paid once for same-day orders. Free self-pickup at Shtaner 7, Jerusalem; Rishon LeZion pickup point plus ₪20.
- Format: 22 x 22 cm, softcover, 24 colour pages. The child's picture on the cover and 10 more pictures inside the story.
- Dedication: printed dedication, or a blank space for a handwritten one, chosen on the site.
- Last pages: recommendations for more books and offers.
- Ages on the FAQ: 2 to 8.

Not used in audience copy: the site's own description of how the pictures are made (project rule: never mention AI in audience copy).
