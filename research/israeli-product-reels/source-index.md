# Source index: Israeli creator product Reels

Accessed 2026-10-15. 173 verified examples from 108 creators, 0 partly inspected leads, 24 excluded. Every row is also in `sources.csv` (one row per Reel, all fields).

Method: every Reel was downloaded without signing in (yt-dlp), sampled at one frame per second into contact sheets, and transcribed locally with Whisper (Hebrew, first 90 seconds; the first 18 transcripts with beam search, the rest with greedy decoding to save time). Sonnet agents coded each Reel from all of its frames, transcript, caption and counts with one brief (`src/CODING_BRIEF.md`). I then checked every included Reel myself against its frames: all frames for the eight deep dives, a 10-frame strip plus the coding for the rest (`inspected` says which). Round 2 (evening of 15 Oct) added 150 Reels from the Reels grids of 12 children's brands (creator collaborations only), 9 parent creators and the 14 Oct study's remaining leads; agents coded them one Reel at a time with added yes/no fields, and I checked each against a 10-frame strip. Followers were not read for round-2 creators (shown as unavailable). The `approach` column adds one label for round 2, Use (children using the product), alongside the seven approaches in `playbook.md`.

**How to read the numbers.** Plays, likes and comments are public counts read on 15 Oct 2026 from Instagram (media info in the logged-in Claude browser pane, read only). They do not show reach, watch time, saves, shares, sales or whether a Reel was boosted as an ad. Shares and saves are not public: unavailable for every row. "Likes hidden by creator" means the creator turned like counts off. Followers come from the source named in `followers_source`.

**Relationship.** "Collaboration post" means Instagram lists a brand as co-author of the Reel. "Disclosure" quotes what the caption or video says about payment or gifting. Instagram's paid-partnership label was off on every Reel here. Where nothing is disclosed, the commercial relationship is marked unknown.
