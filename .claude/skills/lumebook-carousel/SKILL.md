Named Hook Studio on 10 Oct 2026 and renamed Carousel Machine on 22 Oct 2026 (Yarin: "change hook studio name to carousel machine"). Hook Studio started when Yarin asked for it to "focus heavily on click baity hooks, content and slide design". The folder, the skill id, the scheduled task, the cloud routine, the login item (`hook_studio_login.py`, `com.yarinbruner.hook-studio`), the `/hook-studio/` path and `hook-studio.html` keep their old names.

## What comes first

Spend the effort in this order. When two rules pull against each other, the earlier one wins, as long as the three fixed lines hold (the carousel answers what the cover opens, nothing untrue about children, the parent is never shamed or scared).

1. **The hook.** The cover is the loudest true thing the topic allows: a big gap, a big face, very large type. Write three wordings of every candidate from calm to loud and keep the loudest that is still true. Reach first for the "Loud formulas" in `carousel/carousel-pipeline/hook-bank.md`
2. **The content.** Every slide is worth the swipe on its own: a headline that would work as a cover, one idea, something concrete (a sentence to say in quotation marks, a ten second action, a number). Cut every line that only connects, explains twice or softens
3. **The slide design.** The bold look (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/pillars.md`): the headline is set very large, so lines are short, and each slide marks the one phrase that gets the colour block (`highlight`). Copy that does not fit the large type gets cut, never shrunk

Sources, rotation and the rest of the checks are guardrails. They keep the carousel true and fresh; they are never a reason to pick a quieter hook.

One pipeline, two modes. Everything lives in `carousel/carousel-pipeline/` (scripts, schema, topic bank, history, drafts, approved runs) and the README there is the operator manual.

All Hebrew a parent will read follows the **lumebook-hebrew-voice** skill. Read its `SKILL.md` before writing any copy, in both modes.

### The six options

- Direction `kid` (ids kid-1, kid-2, kid-3): the child tells the parents what the moment feels like from the inside and what may sit behind the behaviour. Three genuinely different angles, not three rewrites
- Direction `expert` (ids expert-1, expert-2, expert-3): a clear, useful, evidence based take on the same topic. Use `expert_frame: {"type": "evidence_based_take"}` unless a real, verifiable person said exactly what you quote (then `named_expert` with their name and the source id)
- Vary across the six: hook family (at most 2 options per family), target parent, child gender and awareness stage (up to 24 Oct 2026 the code word never varied: always לומי; since then there is none)
- Since 24 Oct 2026 both groups of three are `kid` options on two different topics (see the section at the top); the expert direction is for older packs

The two directions differ in copy, photo direction and their colour on the review page (`carousel/carousel-pipeline/pillars.md`). On the slides both use the clean look (Yarin, 14 Oct 2026, from his two reference slides: "from now on design the text of the carousels like these except the cta slide"): centred Heebo Black in solid dark plum on the picture's plain wall, the body a step down in a lighter plum, no highlight block, no speech bubbles, no veil, a cream round arrow. The last slide keeps the bold look (dark slide, highlighted "בסיפור אישי", the comment line as a yellow button). Write camera choices that leave a plain wall or sky in the text zone.

### Who every carousel is about

One named Israeli child per option: a girl or a boy with a name from `carousel/carousel-pipeline/names.md` (`child_name`, matching `child_gender`, a different name for each option). The copy talks to mom (feminine singular: את, תגידי, כתבי) or to both parents (plural: אתם, תגידו, כתבו), set in `addressee`. Never to dad alone, and no dad without mom on the slides or in the image anchor (Yarin's rule, 7 Oct 2026). Talking to both parents may open with "אמא ואבא" (Yarin's own hook, 8 Oct 2026); the validator allows that pair only when `addressee` is parents. Use the name where a family would say it, usually in mom's quoted sentence ("״נועם אחרי המנוחה אני כאן״"). The validator blocks אבא, אתה and "בוא נ", names outside the list, a name that never appears before the last slide, and an image anchor that does not match the addressee.

### The ten slides

**10 slides, numbered examples, one picture (Yarin, 14 Oct 2026: "every slide from the second one should show one-two examples, numbering them", then "one picture that depicts the idea for the entire carousel, also make them 10 slides, 2nd slide explaining, second to last recap").** Slides 3 to 8 each show 1 or 2 examples, numbered 1, 2, 3... across the carousel, the digit written right before the example ("1 חיבוק של 3 שניות"), listed in the slide's `examples`. The total is the option's draw (`examples_target`, 6 to 9, Yarin 14 Oct 2026) and the cover's number should match it. Each option has ONE picture (`image` on the option, not on the slides) that depicts the idea; it is also the cover, so it keeps the slide 1 thumbnail rules, and it sits behind all ten slides. The validator enforces this for packs generated from 2026-10-14 10:00; older packs keep 7 slides with a picture per slide.

Yarin's brief (7 Oct 2026): give the reader real value, hook them again on every slide, touch a problem or a feeling, talk about a solution, and only at the very end suggest helping the child with a personalised story where they are the hero, then the CTA. The slides before the last one never prepare a sale, and the brand is never named.

| # | role | job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook | the thumbnail (Yarin, 10 Oct 2026): clickbaity words that say what the viewer gets (a number of things, a sentence, a result; value first, Yarin 11 Oct 2026) with the biggest honest gap, over the one picture: the child facing the viewer with an exaggerated expression (hook-bank.md "Slide 1 is the thumbnail", image-briefs.md "Slide 1 is the cover"). Built from a formula in `carousel/carousel-pipeline/hook-bank.md` and scored with its hook formula (9+ of 12, no zero). Main line up to 8 words, slide up to 12, headline in up to 3 lines of 18 characters |
| 2 | explain | explains the idea: what sits behind the feeling or behaviour and why the examples work. Kid: in the child's words. Expert: one sourced idea in plain words. Written so it still stops someone who lands here first (the slate's slide 2) |
| 3 to 8 | example | 1 or 2 numbered examples each, continuing the count, each real value on its own (a sentence to say in quotation marks, a ten second action, a number). Kid: in the child's words; expert: concrete and sourced where it states a fact |
| 9 | recap | the recap: the examples in a few words and the idea they share, something to screenshot. No new example. No book, story offer, link or comments talk (the validator blocks ספר שבו, סיפור אישי, גיבור, קישור, תגובות on slides 1 to 9) |
| 10 | story_cta | one short line that suggests helping the child with a personalised story (must say סיפור and אישי), then the CTA. Never LumeBook (לומבוק) or a book title. Up to 18 words in total |

**Hook bank and hook slate.** Before writing, read `carousel/carousel-pipeline/hook-bank.md` (formula cards, evidence labels, rotation rules). Then build the private hook slate first (`hook_slate` in pack.json, schema_version 3): 6 kid and 6 expert candidates for tonight's topic, six different formulas per direction across at least 3 families and 3 mechanisms, each with slide 1 text, a slide 2 that works as a first slide on its own, the open question, the payoff slide and how it closes, `factual_claim`, a 0 to 2 score on the ten slate criteria and the four fail checks. Never use a candidate that fails; never use a formula listed in `blocked_expired_formulas`. Build the six options from passing candidates: take the loudest first, then swap only as far as the difference rules need (4+ mechanisms, 4+ sentence shapes, 3+ slide 2 moves, 2+ awareness stages), and never rank or recommend a winner. Each option records `hook: {formula, gap, payoff_slide, score, slate_id, mechanism, slide2_move}`; `gap` is the question the hook opens and `payoff_slide` (2 to 9; 3 to 6 in 7 slide packs) is where the carousel answers it. Prefer formulas not in `recent_hook_formulas_rotate_away_from`, but never force a weaker hook just to rotate. A hook that states a fact about children needs a sourced_fact claim on slide 1 or 2. Be honest in both scores; a weak hook gets rewritten, not re-scored. Everything in the bank is a LumeBook hypothesis until `hook-results.json` says otherwise; never describe a hook as proven.

**Pull on every slide.** Each slide before the last (1 to 9) ends with a reason to swipe, recorded as `pull` (`question`, `open_loop`, `next_step`, `contrast` or `numbered`, plus a short note). Good pulls: a question answered on the next slide, a line that sets up the next headline, the next number of the list, "ועוד משהו קטן לפני שאת הולכת". Never a sentence cut in the middle.

**The headlines are the story (Yarin, 10 Oct 2026: "the headlines of each slide [must] tell a story even if the viewer doesnt read the rest").** Many people swipe through reading only the big line. Read just the ten headlines in order: they must narrate the whole carousel (the cover's promise, the explanation, the numbered examples one by one, the recap, the offer). Every headline names its own point in a full thought of at least 3 words: an example headline names the thing itself after its number ("בלי מסך חצי שעה עד שעה", "קערת דגנים וחלב"), never "דבר ראשון" or "ועוד משהו" alone, and a number or a time goes in the headline, not only in the body. The body adds detail, the source's wording, and the sentence to say in quotation marks, never the thing itself. The validator rejects an order-word-only or under 3 word headline in packs generated from 2026-10-10 22:00, and the review page prints each option's headlines in one line ("הכותרות בלבד") so you can check the story at a glance.

Headlines alone must tell the story, and each one is written like a cover: it stops someone who lands on that slide first. Limits for the bold look (packs from 10 Oct 2026 18:30; `bold_look_rules` in the start JSON): cover headline up to 3 lines of 18 characters; other headlines up to 2 lines of 22 characters; body up to 3 lines of 34 characters; up to 20 words on every slide between the cover and the last one, and 18 on the last slide. The validator rejects longer copy, so cut to the lines that earn the swipe.

**Examples: 3 to 7 (Yarin, 10 Oct 2026: "when you give examples i want them to be between 3-7").** Since 14 Oct 2026 the 10 slide layout replaces this: 6 to 9 numbered examples on slides 3 to 8 (Yarin, 14 Oct 2026: "lets do 6-9"; six slides of 1 or 2, so most slides show one). The rule below applies only to older 7 slide packs. Wherever a slide of a kid or expert option lists examples (a story or music or a bath), record the items in that slide's `examples` list: 3 to 7 distinct items, each one visible on the slide. `nightly.py start` draws a number from 3 to 7 for every option (`examples_per_option`, saved in `drafts/DATE/example-slots.json`); copy the option's number into `examples_target` and make every example list in that option exactly that long. An option whose slides list nothing needs no list, only the target. Packs generated from 2026-10-10 23:00 are checked; the games and books carousels already have their own 3 to 7 suggestions.

**Highlight.** Only the last slide's highlight is painted since the clean look (14 Oct 2026); in 10 slide packs `highlight` is optional before the last slide. Older packs: every slide but the last has `highlight`: the 1 to 3 whole words of its headline (on one line, with their prefix letter) that get the colour block. Choose the punch of the line: the number, the contradiction, the feeling ("2 דברים", "הר געש", "ברגע שאתם הולכים"). The last slide may have one too ("בסיפור אישי").

**Sentences to say.** When an example is a sentence, write the sentence itself in quotation marks in the body. It is the line people screenshot (the clean look sets it as plain text, no bubble).

### CTA and caption

- Since 24 Oct 2026 the CTA asks to save the post and send it to a mom who will relate (section at the top); the comment CTA below is for older packs
- CTA idea, fixed by the brief, in the option's addressee: to both parents `כתבו "X" בתגובות וקבלו קישור ליצירת ספר שבו הילד שלכם הוא הגיבור`, to mom `כתבי "X" בתגובות וקבלי קישור ליצירת ספר שבו הילד שלך הוא הגיבור` (girl: הילדה ... היא הגיבורה). X is always `לומי` (Yarin, 10 Oct 2026: "i want the cta word to always be לומי"): the same word in all six options and every night, no topic code words and no 14 day rotation. The validator rejects any other word in packs generated from 2026-10-10 22:00
- The code word always sits inside straight quotation marks right after the verb: כתבו "לומי" בתגובות (Yarin, 8 Oct 2026). The validator rejects a bare code word
- The CTA text appears on the last slide (10) and, word for word, as the caption's last line. No second CTA anywhere (no link in bio, save, share, follow, tag)
- Never say or hint that anything is made with AI: no label on slides, nothing in the caption, alt text or comments (Yarin, 8 Oct 2026: it ruins trust and engagement). The validator rejects AI, בינה מלאכותית and "נוצר בעזרת"
- No brand and no book title anywhere a parent reads (slides, caption, CTA). The comment link does the selling; the validator rejects לומבוק, LumeBook and catalogue titles
- Caption: line 1 restates the hook in other words, then 2 to 4 short value lines, then one bridge line, then the CTA. 5 to 8 lines, no em dash, period or comma

### Claims and sources

Every factual statement goes into `claims` with its slide, the Hebrew wording, its kind and what the source supports:

- `sourced_fact`: cites ids from this option's `sources` (title, publisher, url, date read, verbatim quote, what it supports)
- `product_fact`: cites `site-...` ids from `product_facts` (checked on the live site in this run)
- `lumebook_wording`: our own phrasing, such as an imagined child line; say what it rests on

If a source is about a related situation (an illness absence, a return after lockdown), say so in `supports`. Do not stretch a source to cover more than it says.

### Image briefs

Each option has ONE English `image` brief (Yarin, 14 Oct 2026): the picture that depicts the carousel's idea, shown behind all ten slides. It is also the cover, so the child faces the viewer with an exaggerated expression (image-briefs.md "Slide 1 is the cover"). Fields: beat, action, setting, camera, lighting, wardrobe and objects, and a text zone (edge top or bottom, 30 to 50 percent, and what the empty surface is; every slide's text sits there). Each option also has one `character_anchor` (child with age, parent, wardrobe). `carousel_lib.compose_image_prompt()` adds the anchor, the feature-film 3D animated style (never photo-real, Yarin 8 Oct 2026), the cream plum violet and muted gold palette, the cover framing, the reserved typography zone and the no-text rule, so the review page shows the exact prompt the model would get. Slides carry no image of their own. Read `references/image-briefs.md` before writing it.
