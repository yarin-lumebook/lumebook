## Brand personality

Proposed personality: a warm, observant storyteller who speaks to an adult as a peer. Values to express: care, attention to the individual child, practical clarity, and honesty about what a book can offer. This is a recommendation derived from the existing voice rules, not customer research.

## Messaging framework

Proposed primary value proposition: a personalised storybook starring the child, chosen for their occasion or story. The product brief describes name and photo personalisation and a catalogue of occasions; verify current features before using them in audience copy. [Product brief](/Users/yarinbruner/Claude/Projects/Lumebook/docs/lumebook-product-brief.md)

Recommended pillars: child-centred wonder and possibility, personal recognition, shared reading, and purchase confidence. Position birthdays as the first gift test route and a new sibling as the proposed first parent workshop scenario. Keep group gifting as a separate route. Do not promise developmental outcomes.

Competitive positioning is pending buyer-alternative research. Do not assert superiority over another provider or claim unique features without checking.
