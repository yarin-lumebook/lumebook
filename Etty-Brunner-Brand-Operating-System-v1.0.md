# 1. Brand Foundation

## Core Positioning

**Emotional MRI™** The body communicates before the conscious mind
understands.

Help women reconnect with themselves by understanding what their bodies
are communicating.

## Brand Promise

Recognition before advice. Understanding before healing. Trust before
conversion.

## Brand Personality

-   Calm
-   Scientific
-   Human
-   Editorial
-   Curious
-   Compassionate
-   Premium
-   Quiet confidence

Never: - Salesy - Spiritual - Motivational - Wellness clichés -
Fear-based

# 2. Audience

Women (primarily Israeli), ages 35--65.

Characteristics: - Emotionally exhausted - Carrying chronic stress -
Feeling disconnected from their bodies - Looking for understanding
rather than another "solution"

If women are shown visually: - Age 35--65 - Authentic Israeli
appearance - Natural skin and hair - Minimal makeup - Relaxed
expressions - Window light - Neutral clothing - Real environments -
Never influencer or stock-photo aesthetics

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Core Philosophy

Every piece of content follows:

Body → Pattern → Mechanism → Meaning → Compassion → Reflection → Hope

Never reverse this order.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Content Strategy

Primary goals: 1. Saves 2. Shares 3. DMs 4. Appointments

Never optimize primarily for likes.

Content pillars: - Understanding the body (40%) - Healing process
(30%) - Recovery & integration (20%) - Personal connection (10%)

# 5. Copywriting System

## Voice

Write like a trusted therapist speaking to one woman.

Recognition first. Education second. Advice third.

Never preach.

## Language

Simple spoken Hebrew.

Prefer: - לפעמים - יכול להיות - שימי לב - הגוף עשוי

Avoid: - תמיד - אף פעם - כולם - הוכח

## Carousel Structure

1.  Scroll-stopping hook (5--8 words)
2.  Recognition 3--6. One insight per slide
3.  Emotional MRI explanation
4.  Reflection
5.  Soft CTA

Maximum: 20--40 Hebrew words per slide.

CTA examples: - שמרי את זה. - שלחי למישהי שזה יכול לעזור לה. - מה הגוף
שלך מנסה להגיד?

## Hooks

Reveal hidden patterns.

Avoid: - 5 Tips - Secrets - Hacks - Mistakes

## Storytelling

Prefer: - Daily observations - Body sensations - Small moments

Avoid: - Epic transformations - Miracle stories

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Design System

## Canvas

1080 × 1350 px 4:5 ratio

12-column grid

Large margins (72--96 px)

Whitespace is a design element.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## RTL

Mandatory:

-   Right-aligned text
-   RTL reading flow
-   Hebrew-first composition
-   Left-pointing navigation arrows
-   Never justify paragraphs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Typography

### Headings

Heebo

700--800

56--72 px (cover)

40--52 px (sections)

### Body

Assistant

400--500

16--24 px

### Labels

Assistant

13--15 px

# 7. AI Image Rules

Reserve whitespace for Hebrew headlines.

Design for RTL.

No decorative clutter.

Every graphic element must reinforce the Emotional MRI identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Quality Checklist

Before publishing verify:

-   One core idea.
-   Recognition before education.
-   Body before mind.
-   One CTA only.
-   20--40 Hebrew words per slide.
-   Fits Emotional MRI.
-   Feels premium.
-   Uses Heebo + Assistant.
-   RTL respected.
-   Visual motif limited to one.
-   Images match target audience.
-   Scientifically grounded.
-   Trust over conversion.

If any item fails, revise before publishing.
