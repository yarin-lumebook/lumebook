We have no sales data from social at all, so October is for measuring a baseline. (Source: our product brief, public view of both pages.)

The competitor

MeBook (מיבוק) has a near-identical name and, per the SMM lead's own check of their site, the same core features: free preview, instant digital copy, social-stories angle and a religious-observant line. Their homepage shows a ₪139 hardcover with 10–14 business day delivery and a "65,000+ customers" claim (mebook.ai, 1 Oct 2026). Our delivered price is ₪149 (₪119 + ₪30 shipping) for a softcover, up to 7 business days. We can't win on price or on a feature list.

The plan in three moves

1. Own the developmental angleEvery post ties the book to a real moment: new sibling, giving up the pacifier, starting gan. 40% of content. One professional reads a story on camera.
2. Creators paid in books40 outreach DMs a week to parent creators. A free book for a reveal reel, labelled as an ad as Israeli rules require. Commission only when a sale happens.
3. Customer revealsEvery buyer is asked to film the child's first look. Free proof and free content.

Constraints I'm working within: ₪0 cash budget, one person, no baseline. Hanukkah is 4–12 Dec 2026 (hebcal.com). I propose we advertise an order cutoff of Sun 22 Nov, which still needs ops to confirm.

How we'll measure it

  • North star: orders traced to social (creator codes, tracked links, a "how did you hear about us" question at checkout).
  • October: measure the baseline. December target: 3× October's traced orders and previews. This target is my assumption and gets replaced with real numbers at the 31 Oct review.
  • Kill rule: if milestone posts don't beat our other content on shares and saves by December, we rethink the angle.
