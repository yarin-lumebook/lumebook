## What it is
Online store for personalized children's books. Parent picks a story from a catalog, uploads a photo of the child + name, AI generates an illustrated character who looks like the child and stars on every page. Free preview before paying; "pay only if you love it". Digital copy arrives by email within minutes; printed book ships.

Tagline (HE hero): "כשהילדים גיבורים בסיפור הם גיבורים גם בחיים" — books that help kids face challenges and build confidence. Positioning is *developmental/emotional* (social-stories approach), not just "cute gift".

Origin story (/about): started at home — the founders made a story for a child named Ariel, saw the confidence effect, built the company on social-stories principles + AI.

## Wizard (/wizard) — 4 steps
1) Child name (first name only), age, gender (boy/girl), book language (Hebrew/English/Arabic/Russian) → 2) story → 3) photo → 4) preview/pay. Steps 2–4 not walked through.

## B2B — kindergartens / schools / orgs (/kindergarten)
Flow: order qty → get a link to send parents → each parent uploads a photo in ~1 minute → Lumebook does the rest; org tracks progress in a dashboard. One story per group; every child appears with own name/photo. Optional org logo + dedication. Org discount-code field exists.
Per-book price tiers (read from the live slider; boundaries between sampled points not verified): 5 → ₪99 · 10 & 20 → ₪80 · 30 → ₪75 · 50/100/150 → ₪70. Slider range 5–150. Prices include 18% VAT.
