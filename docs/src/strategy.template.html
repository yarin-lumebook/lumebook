Lumebook · Social & content strategy · Q4 2026

Make the child's face the ad.

Right now our feed reads like a parenting magazine. It gets almost no engagement and sells nothing. For the next 90 days, three things change. The child's reaction becomes the content. Parent creators become the distribution, paid in books. "See your free preview" becomes the call to action on every post.

v1 · 1 Oct 2026 · Owner: SMM lead · Constraints: ₪0 cash budget (barter only), one-person team, no baseline data yet.

The competitor problem

MeBook (מיבוק) sells the same product: a photo-based AI book with milestone stories. Their homepage lists a ₪139 hardcover, 10–14 business days, and "over 65,000 satisfied customers", and they post as @mebook.ai on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok fact (mebook.ai, read 1 Oct 2026; the same page also mentions free shipping over ₪299, so check their real delivered price before comparing publicly). Press coverage from Aug 2025 put them at 25,000+ books sold (mako.co.il).

Two consequences. First, we are not the cheaper option once shipping is added, so we can't compete on price. Second, the names sound almost the same (מיבוק and לומיבוק). If our content doesn't carry clearly recognisable brand cues, part of our reach may go to them assumption.

Three engines

Creators

Parent creators get a free book and post the moment their child sees it. Their audience, our product. This is where reach comes from.

Customer reveals

Every buyer gets one ask: film the first look. This is proof, plus an endless supply of reels at zero cost.

Content that sells

Milestone tips that end with the book as the tool. Keep the parenting value and add a clear bridge to the product.

03 · Guidelines

Marketing guidelines

These rules apply to every post, story, reply, creator brief and caption. If a piece breaks one, it doesn't go out.

Voice

At the child's eye level, warm and practical. Keep the #הורותבגובההעיניים spirit. Spoken Hebrew, short sentences. We talk to the parent about their child, never at the parent.

Do

  • ספר שעוזר לדבר על האח החדש
  • דרך עדינה להיפרד מהמוצץ
  • One idea per post, with a concrete moment ("the first night without the pacifier")
  • Say "helps" and "a gentle way to"

Don't

  • ספר שפותר קנאת אחים
  • Clinical or outcome promises ("cures fears", "proven to build confidence")
  • Guilt ("still giving them screens?")
  • Naming or mocking competitors

Visual rules

  • Show a real face in the first 2 seconds. Use the child's reaction or the book opening to their face. Don't open on a logo.
  • Show the real book in real hands. Use AI mock-ups only to show the preview step, and label AI scenes the way we already do.
  • Burned-in captions on every video. Most people scroll with sound off.
  • A recognisable signature, because of the name clash. Use the same 1-second end card (לומיבוק logo + תצוגה מקדימה בחינם), the same page-flip transition and the same short sound on every reel. Write the name in full every time, never just "the book".

Children's safety and consent (non-negotiable)

  • We only repost a child with the parent's written consent in DM, and we save a screenshot of it.
  • First names only. No gan or school names, no location tags, no uniforms with logos, no house numbers in frame.
  • A customer's child never appears in an ad or a boosted post without separate explicit consent for that use.
  • We take content down within 24 hours of any request, with no questions asked.
  • No bath, bedtime-undressed or medical-moment footage, ever. We hide or delete inappropriate comments on child content immediately.

Claims and disclosure

  • Creators must label sponsored posts. Israel's Consumer Protection Authority treats free products as compensation, so a free book means the post is advertising and must say so clearly fact (ynet coverage of the guidelines). Put this in every brief.
  • We only use the site's "30,000+ children" and "4.9 on Google" numbers after a founder confirms the source needs founder.
  • Use one age range everywhere. The site currently gives four (1–10, 2–8, 2–10, 3–8) needs founder.
  • Check prices against the live site on the day you post. Never post a price from memory.

Cadence a one-person team can sustain

  • 4 reels a week on Instagram, cross-posted to TikTok and Facebook. Reuse the best creator video as our own reel, with their permission.
  • Stories 5 days a week (3–5 frames): a reveal repost, a poll, and a link sticker to the free preview with UTM.
  • 1 carousel a week: a milestone guide that ends on the matching book.
  • Pin 3 posts: the best reveal, how the free preview works, and the best review. Rename the highlights to how it works / reveals / Hanukkah / reviews.

Developmental wedge

  • One milestone per month, all channels. Oct: new sibling. Nov: starting gan / grade 1 adjustment. Dec: Hanukkah gifts framed as "a story for the moment they're in". Every reel, carousel and story that month points to the matching book. Our site already has the sibling and first-day-of-gan guides, so reuse them as source material (product brief, blog section).
  • Explain the method in 20 seconds, once, and pin it. "Why a story about them helps": a child rehearses the situation safely before it happens. Use plain words and the founders' own description of social stories, not clinical language.
  • Get one professional on the record. An OT, speech therapist, child psychologist or gan teacher who reads the story for a milestone and appears in a reel. Offer a book and a credit. This is also our second creator tier.
  • Parent proof is about the moment, not the book. Ask buyers: "What was hard before? What changed after the story?" Use only with consent, and never promise an outcome.
  • Language limits. "Helps", "supports", "a gentle way to". Never "treats", "fixes" or "proven". Any statistic or study named in a post needs a link in the caption.

CTA rule

Every post ends with exactly one CTA, either "לתצוגה מקדימה בחינם, קישור בביו" or "כתבו 'ספר' בתגובות ואשלח לכם קישור". With the comment version, answer every comment by DM with the UTM link. Comments also tell the algorithm the post is worth showing.

Don't throw away the parenting tips. Turn them into Milestone → book posts. A tip with no bridge to the product is a free service to parents, not marketing.

06 · Proof

Customer reveal loop

  • Message after purchase (WhatsApp or email, whichever ops can send): "צלמו את הרגע שהם רואים את הספר בפעם הראשונה ותייגו @lumebookai". Reward: we feature them, plus a monthly draw for a free book needs founder.
  • A card in the box with the same ask and a QR code to our Instagram, if Bookpod can add inserts needs founder.
  • Past customers. Send one message to previous buyers asking for reveal videos and Google reviews. This is the biggest free stock of proof we have.
  • Refresh the existing "לקוחות משתפים" highlight every week. Get consent before every repost (see the guidelines).
07 · Community

Community

  • Answer every comment and DM the same working day. DMs are where hesitant parents ask "is it really free to preview?"
  • Parent Facebook groups: contribute real answers on milestone threads (pacifier, siblings, gan adjustment). Never drop links unprompted. Offer group admins a free-book giveaway in exchange for a sanctioned post.
  • One collab giveaway a month with a creator: a free book, posted as a joint Instagram collab post so it reaches both audiences.
  • Answer common questions in public. When the same DM question comes up 3 or more times, it becomes a reel.

09 · Measurement

11 · Dependencies

What I need from the founders

  1. The developmental evidence: who wrote and reviewed the stories, whether a professional looked at them, and which sources the "social stories" claim rests on. Everything in the wedge depends on this needs founder.
  2. One real difference from MeBook, in a sentence, that we can prove on camera. Free preview, instant digital copy, social stories and a religious line are all matched.
  3. A book allowance for barter: e.g. 30 free books for the quarter, plus our real cost per book.
  4. A personal link per creator with the automatic audience discount, and how checkout tracks each link (the 25% per sale, commission only, is already set).
  5. A "how did you hear about us?" question at checkout, plus a weekly export of orders and previews by source.
  6. Confirm the "30,000+ children" and "4.9 Google" numbers, and pick one age range for the whole site.
  7. A Hanukkah offer. Our ₪30 shipping makes us more expensive than MeBook when delivered. Free shipping on 2+ books, for example, would give creators a reason to say "now". This isn't my call, but it is the biggest conversion lever I can see.
  8. A post-purchase reveal message, a box insert if Bookpod allows it, and one message to past customers.
  9. Confirm the Hanukkah cutoff date with ops. Also fix the blank shipping prices in the US FAQ.
