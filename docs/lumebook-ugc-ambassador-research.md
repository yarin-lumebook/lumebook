# UGC / ambassador / creator programs: what to borrow for Lumebook's signup form

Researched 2026-10-03. I read each claim below on the URL cited next to it. JS-rendered forms (Aspire, Social Snowball, Loyoly, Globo) were opened in a browser and read **without submitting anything**. Google Form and GRIN questions were read from the form definitions in the page source. Anything I didn't read on the brand's own page is marked **(secondhand)**. Anything I couldn't open is in the "Couldn't verify" list at the end. This is not legal advice; have the consent wording checked before you rely on it.

## 1. Patterns worth stealing for Lumebook's form

### 1a. The recurring field set (what the real forms actually ask)

| Field | Seen in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| First and last name, email | every form | always required |
| Phone | Mini Mioche (optional), Noodle & Boo, Agam (required) | Israeli forms treat phone as required (Agam). |
| Instagram handle | Little Sleepies (required), Mini Mioche (required), Noodle & Boo, Lovevery (optional connect) | IG first. TikTok is usually optional. |
| TikTok handle | Little Sleepies, Mini Mioche, Lovevery (all optional) | |
| Follower count / channel size | KiwiCo (required), Noodle & Boo | Little Sleepies validates real numbers through linked IG Insights instead. |
| Children's ages / birthdates | Lovevery (**required**: child's birthday), KiwiCo (optional: kids' ages, months if under 3), Noodle & Boo ("I am a mom to… names & birth dates"), Little Sleepies (required: "Age of your little one(s)?") | Kids' ages are the one niche-specific field nearly everyone asks. |
| Shipping address | Little Sleepies (required), Noodle & Boo, Agam | Asked up front because the gift ships right after approval. |
| "Are you familiar with us?" / "Why do you want to join?" | Little Sleepies (required), Noodle & Boo | Filters for real fans. |
| **"Are you open to product instead of payment?"** | KiwiCo (required, Yes/No) | Best single question for a ₪0 budget. |
| **"This is an unpaid collaboration" acknowledgement** | Little Sleepies (required checkbox) | Sets expectations before you ever talk to them. |
| **Content-rights checkbox inside the form** | Mini Mioche (rights grant shown above "Create Account"), Agam (accept the program terms) | Get consent at signup so you don't have to chase it later. |
| Payout details (PayPal) | Little Sleepies | Not relevant if Lumebook pays only in books or credit. |

Length: real forms run **6 to 13 fields**. KiwiCo's YouTube form has 9 questions. Mini Mioche has 6 fields plus a rights clause. Little Sleepies' gifted form has 13 fields plus IG validation.

### 1b. Recommended question list for Lumebook (RECOMMENDATION, not a finding)

Aim for one screen and roughly 3 minutes. **\*** means required.

**About you**
1. Full name\*
2. WhatsApp number\* *(IL adaptation: WhatsApp is the main contact channel here. Lilibloom has a WhatsApp button on its ambassador page and even pays points for a WhatsApp family-group recommendation.)*
3. Email\*
4. City\* *(collect the full address only after approval. Less friction and less personal data held.)*
5. Instagram handle\* / TikTok handle (optional)
6. Rough audience size\*: dropdown with "private account / up to 1K / 1K–5K / 5K–20K / 20K+". *(The bands are a recommendation. Brands ask for follower count (KiwiCo, Noodle & Boo), but Hooray says follower size doesn't matter.)*
7. Link to one video you've made that you're proud of (optional). Doubles as a UGC portfolio check.
8. I am a… (multi-select)\*: Lumebook customer / parent who hasn't ordered yet / content creator / kindergarten teacher (gannenet) / child therapist (speech, OT, emotional) / other. *(MeBook runs expert "creator" pages, e.g. a speech therapist and content creator; Lovevery runs a separate education/therapy track.)*

**Your kids**
9. Children's ages\* (multi-select buckets: 1–2, 3–4, 5–6, 7–8, 9+). *(Don't ask for the child's name or photo at signup. Lumebook collects those in the wizard at order time.)*
10. What's coming up for your child in the next 3 months?\* Options: starting gan / first grade, new sibling, leaving diapers, giving up the pacifier, a fear, birthday, holiday, nothing specific. *(Matches Lumebook's catalogue, so the free book fits a real moment and content timing follows it.)*

**Content**
11. What would you film?\* (multi-select): unboxing / my child's first reaction to seeing themselves / bedtime reading / before-and-after of the milestone / a short review.
12. Formats you're happy to make\* (multi-select): Reel, TikTok, Story, Google review, a recommendation in a parents' WhatsApp group. *(Lilibloom gives each of these a points value.)*
13. How will your child appear on camera?\*: full face / from behind or hands only / not at all (book only). *(Lumebook-specific. It filters for creators who will show the reaction moment and tells you upfront what you can reuse.)*
14. Are you open to collaborating for a free book instead of payment?\* Yes/No. *(Copied from KiwiCo.)*

**Consent (separate checkboxes, not one blanket box)**
15. I'm 18+ and the parent or legal guardian of every child who will appear.\* *(Modelled on Hooray's "Release Form for a Minor".)*
16. I understand this is an unpaid collaboration: the book is the full compensation.\* *(Modelled on Little Sleepies.)*
17. I'll mark posts as an ad or collaboration (e.g. "קיבלתי במתנה" / #שיתוף_פעולה) and tag @lumebook.\* *(Israel's Consumer Protection Authority says a free product still counts as a commercial relationship that must be disclosed. The exact wording is my recommendation; the authority didn't mandate specific words.)*
18. Lumebook may repost my content on its own social accounts, with credit.\*
19. *(optional)* Lumebook may also use my content in paid ads (e.g. a TikTok Spark code or an IG partnership ad) and on the website.
20. *(optional)* Lumebook may show the AI-illustrated pages featuring my child in its marketing. *(Hooray's own T&C promise not to use a customer's uploaded photo or likeness in marketing without separate permission. Lumebook should ask for this separately too.)*
21. How did you hear about us? / Anything else? (optional)

**Hebrew adaptation notes (recommendation):** Israeli examples write in the feminine for the main audience (Lilibloom's ambassador copy is all feminine; Hadas says its terms use the feminine "for convenience" but apply to everyone). For Lumebook, write gender-neutral slash forms ("הורה", "צלמ/י") or feminine with a one-line note. Give the form a Hebrew title that reads like a club, not a job, e.g. "מועדון ההורים-יוצרים של Lumebook".

### 1c. Perks that fit a ₪0 / barter budget (recommendation, based on what the programs below do)

- **Free personalised book** as full compensation. This is Hooray's core model: an endorsed collab is paid with a 100%-off coupon, and Hooray ran it at scale with 1,615 nano-influencers (secondhand case study).
- **Points-for-missions ladder** (Lilibloom on Loyoly, IL): reel 300 pts, review video 300, post 200, Facebook review 250, Google review 150, story 100, follow / WhatsApp-group recommendation 50; 500 pts = ₪100 gift card. Lumebook version: a reel or TikTok earns a second book or a sibling edition.
- **Commission paid as store credit.** In Israel, cash commission needs a tax invoice: Agam pays from ₪200 against an invoice and otherwise gives site credit; Hadas converts commission to credit from ₪100 and pays cash only from ₪500 with an invoice. Default to credit, so parents without an עוסק פטור can still join.
- **Personal discount code for followers** (BRAVE Books: 10% commission on its code; Hooray requires the code in every post). It costs margin, not cash.
- **Repost / feature on the brand account** (Hooray, The Book Drop, Noodle & Boo). Free to give, and micro-creators value it.
- **Early access / sneak peeks** (BRAVE Books, Noodle & Boo). For Lumebook: first look at new holiday books, e.g. the Hanukkah titles.

### 1d. Child-consent and rights clauses to include (recommendation)

From what the programs actually use:
1. **Guardian certification plus minor release.** Hooray's release has the signer certify they're the parent or guardian with authority. It permits use of the child's appearance, name, likeness and biographical data, and waives approval of edits. Lumebook should **narrow** this: name only if separately ticked, and no biographical data.
2. **Licence scope, tiered.** Hooray and Wonderbly take worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, editable rights. For parents of young kids in Israel, I'd offer (a) organic repost with credit (required) and (b) ads and website (optional), plus an easy takedown: "email or WhatsApp us and we'll remove it from our channels." Wonderbly already says it can remove content at any time and gives a report email.
3. **Disclosure duty.** Hooray requires #Ad / #Advertisement / #SponsoredByHooray in the first three hashtags. Israel's CPA requires disclosure whenever there's any benefit, including a free product.
4. **Separate consent for the AI artwork of the child** (based on Hooray's T&C). Lumebook-specific, because the product itself is a likeness of the child.
5. **Spark code / partnership-ad permission** (Hooray's TikTok program requires the Spark ads code), so you can boost the best UGC later without re-asking.
6. **Light-touch consent for UGC found in the wild:** a "reply #YESLumebook" mechanism (Wonderbly's "#YESWonderbly"; Hooray also accepts any affirmative DM reply). Useful for customers who post without ever filling in the form.
7. **Data minimisation.** Israel's Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 took effect 14 Aug 2025 (Pearl Cohen). Collecting only what's needed (no child photo or name at signup) is the safer default.

## 3. Per-brand details

### Hooray Heroes (personalised books, direct competitor model)
- **Trio ambassador (posted 22 Jan 2020).** Wants an active Instagram account, at least 3 kids, and a "passion for taking beautiful, high-quality photos (must!)". Follower count doesn't matter: "tens of thousands of followers or just a small following". Perk: a free book featuring the three kids, plus an IG/FB repost with a tag. Obligation: one IG post plus photos to the brand. Only selected applicants are emailed. https://hoorayheroes.com/stories/calling-all-influencers/
- **TikTok program (posted 8 Jan 2022).** 18+ TikTok creators. Free book in exchange for one video "your style", **a TikTok Spark ads code**, and rights to use it in social posts and compilations. Hooray reviews existing content and contacts suitable candidates only. https://hoorayheroes.com/blogs/stories/go-viral-on-tiktok-with-hooray-heroes
- **Official TikTok rules (12 Jan 2022).** 18+ US residents (not Rhode Island). Selection based on "public image, social media profile and lifestyle". Benefit: one book plus shipping via a 100% coupon valid 14 days. Obligations: at least one high-quality video within **1 month of receipt**, disclosure (#Ad / #Advertisement / #Advertising / #SponsoredByHooray) in the first three hashtags, and a tag of @hoorayheroes. Licence: worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, sublicensable, including ads, TV and radio, with the promoter's name, likeness and voice; no approval right. When a child appears, the minor release applies. https://hoorayheroes.com/stories/official-collaboration-rules/
- **General collabs page.** A form reviewed by the "Collaboration Department"; unsuccessful applicants may not get a reply. https://hoorayheroes.com/stories/influencer-collaborations/
- **Form status:** all three pages currently show a raw `[contact-form-7 …]` shortcode instead of a form (WordPress leftovers on a Shopify site), so the forms look broken. I couldn't read their questions.
- **Influencer terms.**
  - Two tiers: endorsed (book plus shipping via coupon is the full consideration; coupon must be used within 14 days) and paid (fee plus approval step).
  - Each post needs the @hoorayheroes tag, the discount code, #hoorayheroes, a website link, and disclosure in the first three hashtags. The book cover and illustrations must be clearly visible.
  - The influencer must obtain consent from third persons, and the minor release applies.
  - Content may be used for paid amplification unless agreed otherwise.
  - Either side can terminate on 10 days' notice.
  - https://hoorayheroes.com/stories/influencer-terms/
- **UGC terms + "Release Form for a Minor".**
  - Consent is triggered by brand hashtags or mentions, by posting a gifted product, by site reviews, or by any affirmative reply to a DM or email request.
  - The release: the signer certifies they're the parent or guardian, and irrevocably permits use of the child's appearance, name, likeness and biographical data for advertising, waiving review of edits.
  - https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/user-generated-content-terms-and-conditions
- **Customer T&C (relevant to an AI-photo product):**
  - Uploaded photos are licensed only to make and fulfil the order.
  - Hooray will **not** use an uploaded photo or identifiable likeness in public marketing "unless separate permission has been obtained".
  - Its image-processing providers aren't authorised to train general AI models on the photos.
  - Uploaders warrant they're the child's parent or guardian.
  - https://hoorayheroes.com/pages/terms-and-conditions
- **Scale (secondhand, platform case study dated 18 Apr 2023):** 1,615 nano-influencers in France and Spain, each given a free book for UGC with commercial rights; 1,394 IG posts and 6,147 stories. Hooray is quoted saying over 90% of its advertising is UGC. https://www.epidemic.co/how-hooray-heroes-used-nano-influencer-marketing-to-create-authentic-user-generated-content
