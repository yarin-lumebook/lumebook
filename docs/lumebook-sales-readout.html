LumeBook: what the sales data says and what social does about it

Written 12 Oct 2026, updated 15 Oct with the 14 Oct findings on link tracking and grandparents. Internal, do not share outside the project: it holds sales figures.

Source for every number: 17 screenshots of the store's admin dashboard taken on 12 Oct 2026 at 09:34, from the Analytics tab (pdf.lumebook.com/api/admin/modern/analytics) and the Marketing tab (pdf.lumebook.com/api/admin/modern/marketing). The situation in plain words

  1. People like making the book, but most don't pay.

    Of 6,130 people who started a book, 1,329 paid within a week, about 1 in 5. In the last 30 days, 202 orders reached the payment step and 85 were bought, so about half leave there.

  2. Ads are expensive.

    A sale costs ₪61 if ads get credit for every sale and ₪113 on Meta's own count. The company's goal is ₪30 and its ceiling is ₪50, on a ₪119 book. Ads cannot be what grows LumeBook right now.

  3. Sales behave like a tap.

    Strong months had ≈300 to 490 orders, weak months ≈15 to 20, and it is about 115 a month now. No steady stream of customers arrives on its own. Building that stream is what social is for.

  4. Sales to people who already know the product cost almost nothing.

    A purchase that followed a WhatsApp reminder or a post-purchase message cost $0.16 to $2.34 in message fees. The dashboard says those purchases followed the message, not that the message caused them.

  5. People buy for a moment in the child's life.

    Birthday is the number one book. Birthday, new sibling, pacifier, potty and first grade together are 603 of 1,972 orders, almost a third.

  6. The buyers are not only young moms.

    In the ads, women 55 to 64 and 35 to 44 have the most purchases, and women 25 to 34 cost the most per purchase among the large groups. My reading is that the older group is grandmothers buying a gift. The data does not prove it.

  7. The admin can't show where a sale came from yet.

    Its Marketing tab says the website does not record which link brought an order, so a sale from a post or from a creator looks like any other sale. A 14 Oct read of the store's code found that it does send link data (UTMs, first and last touch) when a book is created, so the gap may be in the admin's reporting rather than in the tracking (research/creator-content-conversion/report.md).

Social decisions

These need no further information and sit inside the social role.

  1. Choose topics by buying moment.

    Something on birthdays every week. One milestone theme a month, in order of sales: new sibling, then pacifier, then potty.

  2. One gift-giver post a week.

    Aimed at a grandmother or aunt giving the book. It sits outside the carousel rule of mom or both parents, so it runs as a separate test for about a month. A 14 Oct read of 2,982 comments on creator posts found only 5 people who said they were grandparents (research/creator-content-conversion/report.md), so the grandmother buyer is still a hypothesis and this test is how to find out.

  3. Pick creators by moment and place, not by size.

    First in line: creators living a moment now (second child on the way, a toddler giving up the pacifier, a birthday coming), two or three creators of grandmother age, and creators from the top cities. Each creator gets the book that matches their moment.

  4. Show the finished book and the price openly once a week.

    The printed book in a child's hands, what it costs, how fast it arrives. My guess is that people leave at payment because they are unsure what they will get or surprised by the total.

  5. Measure with what social controls.

    A one-line daily log of what went out (post, creator video, group post). Every Monday, compare it to orders per day in the admin and mark the days that jumped. Count comments and DMs per post. Thu 8 Oct had ≈13 orders in one day and nothing on the dashboard explains it. A log would.

  6. Start parent groups with the top cities.

    Rishon LeZion, Tel Aviv, Ashkelon, Be'er Sheva, Petah Tikva, Holon, Netanya, then outward. (My first draft left Tel Aviv out without a good reason. It is second by orders.)

  7. Publish the week's strongest post on Wednesday evening or Thursday.

    Ad sales were cheapest on Thursday and Friday. The dashboard itself says this is not a valid test yet, but it costs nothing to try.

  8. One request to Yossi: show sales per link and per creator in the admin.

    Before creator videos go live for Hanukkah (creator books are due 10 Nov). The store already runs an ambassador program with personal links and sends link data with each new book (research/creator-content-conversion/report.md), so what's missing is a view of which orders each link brought. Without it nobody can show what social sold or check creator commissions against real sales.

Not doing: asking for money to boost posts, since ads already cost too much per sale. Views and followers stay in the KPI table as supporting numbers. Orders and books started are the ones that matter.

Numbers not to use

  • "Conversion rate 61%" and the by-source cards (gallery 50%, custom 64%, scratch 73%). The monthly chart shows exactly 100% for six straight months, so unpaid orders from that period are missing. Use 21.7% per person, or 16% per order for the last 30 days.
  • Site visitors. 129 visitors against 161 add-to-carts in the same week, while Meta reports 4,214 clicks in 30 days. Visitor tracking sees a fraction of the traffic.
  • Child age. See above.
  • The weekday table as proof. The dashboard says 4 of the 8 full weeks it needs are missing.

Things to check with whoever built the dashboard

  • The Analytics tab says there is no random control group for WhatsApp, while the Marketing tab reports extra sales against one.
  • "+5.4 per 100" for the first coupon is higher than the 5.3% who bought, so the left-alone group is probably tiny.
  • The second coupon has fewer sends (269) than the third (390), so step two may be skipped for some people.
  • Budget of ₪700 a day shown against ≈₪296 a day actually spent.

Open questions nobody has answered

  1. What ran in Nov 2025 and Feb to Mar 2026, and what stopped in Dec and in May?
  2. Was there any ad spend in August 2026? August had ≈118 orders before the Meta data begins on 8 Sep. If no ads ran then, sales without ads were about 3.8 a day, the same as the 30-day ad window, and the ads may be adding very little.
  3. What happened on Thu 8 Oct?
  4. What do print and shipping cost per book? That sets the real ceiling for cost per sale.
