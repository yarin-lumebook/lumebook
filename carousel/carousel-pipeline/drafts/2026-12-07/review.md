- [Potty Training Regression](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/toddler/toilet-training/Pages/Regression.aspx) · HealthyChildren.org, American Academy of Pediatrics (last updated 25 May 2022; adapted from the AAP Guide to Toilet Training, 2nd Edition): Check with the pediatrician first; a change like a new child-care routine is a common cause; ask the child why and listen; hugs, praise and stickers on a chart; it usually does not last very long, a few days or weeks in many cases. An American Academy of Pediatrics family page.
- [Emotional Issues and Potty Training Problems](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/toddler/toilet-training/Pages/Emotional-Issues-and-Bathroom-Problems.aspx) · HealthyChildren.org, American Academy of Pediatrics (last updated 25 May 2022; adapted from the AAP Guide to Toilet Training, 2nd Edition): From about age 3 children can feel shame after an accident and may hide it; do not criticize or punish; say you know, that it is okay and that next time will go better; ask for help cleaning up; constant asking can make a child hold back until it is too late; avoid returning to diapers, use training pants, the potty in the child's room, going with the child to the preschool bathroom. An American Academy of Pediatrics family page.

A Nemours page for parents.

The page is about infants and toddlers; used as general advice for older children and as support for reading stories about feelings.
