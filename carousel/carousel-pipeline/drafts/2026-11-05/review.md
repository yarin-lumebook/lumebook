**מקורות**

- [Tips, Tricks, and Books for Helping Kids Understand Calendars](https://www.readbrightly.com/books-help-kids-understand-calendars) · Brightly (Penguin Random House), written by a kindergarten and preschool teacher: Kids make sense of calendar words through their own lives; a calendar kids add cues to (Xs for days gone by); counting the days or weeks to an event; songs and chants with movement; a greeting that names what we do each day. A teacher's practical article, not a clinical guideline.
- [Teaching the Concept of Time to Preschoolers](https://empoweredparents.co/teaching-concept-of-time-to-preschoolers) · Empowered Parents (early years educators' parenting site): Time is abstract and kids need repeated experiences of it; saying time words in context (a birthday in 2 weeks); feeling how long five minutes is; a visual timer for 3 to 5 minutes; a predictable routine and a routine poster; baking as a feel for passing time. An educators' site, used as general advice.
