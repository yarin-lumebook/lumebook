**מקורות**

- [Helping Young Children With Sharing](https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/helping-young-children-with-sharing/) · ZERO TO THREE, 11 Aug 2017: Young children cannot yet fully see another child's side (skills develop around 3.5 to 4), a long turn is fine, a timer works as a neutral rule-keeper, children can learn to ask Can I have a turn, feelings of both children are acknowledged, the waiting child is offered another activity, and practice continues.
- [Helping Kids Learn to Take Turns and Practice Waiting](https://www.pbs.org/parents/thrive/helping-kids-learn-to-take-turns-and-practice-waiting) · PBS KIDS for Parents (Samantha Gratton), 4 Aug 2023: Waiting is hard, a visual countdown timer helps, something else to do while waiting helps, and talking about what comes next comforts a child and says who has the next turn.
- [Social Development in Preschoolers: Learning How to Share & Cooperate](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/preschool/Pages/Social-Development-in-Preschoolers.aspx) · HealthyChildren.org (American Academy of Pediatrics), last updated 5 Dec 2024: Preschoolers can settle a dispute by taking turns or trading toys, and a reminder that each child gets an equal turn helps.
