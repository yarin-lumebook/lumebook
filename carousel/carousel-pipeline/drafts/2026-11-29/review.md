Lewis, PhD, August 2023): Sitting still is not required: toddlers may stand, look at a page or two, want the same favorite again and again, attention spans grow soon; if it does not work, try again later and keep the book out.

Lewis, PhD, August 2023): Regular reading times, lap, choosing from three or four books, turning pages, voices, pointing and naming pictures, the child's name as a character, flaps and textures, few words per page, books about daily routines, toys in hand, books kept within reach.
