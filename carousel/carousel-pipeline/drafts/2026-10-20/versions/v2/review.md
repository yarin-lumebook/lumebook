**מקורות**

- [Potty Training Regression](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/toddler/toilet-training/Pages/Regression.aspx) · HealthyChildren.org, American Academy of Pediatrics (last updated 25 May 2022; adapted from the AAP Guide to Toilet Training, 2nd Edition): Toilet training regression: see the pediatrician first to rule out physical causes such as constipation or a urinary tract infection; otherwise it is usually a response to a change or stress (new child care, new sibling, a move); it usually lasts days or weeks.
