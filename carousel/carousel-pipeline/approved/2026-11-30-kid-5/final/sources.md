# Sources · kid-5 · 2026-11-30

- [Helping Young Children With Sharing](https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/helping-young-children-with-sharing/) · ZERO TO THREE, 11 Aug 2017 · read 2026-10-29
  - supports: Young children cannot yet fully see another child's side (skills develop around 3.5 to 4), a long turn is fine, a timer works as a neutral rule-keeper, children can learn to ask Can I have a turn, feelings of both children are acknowledged, the waiting child is offered another activity, and practice continues.
  - quote: "children this age haven't developed the ability to put themselves in another's shoes yet. ... these skills develop between 3.5 to 4 years old. ... It is okay to let children have a "long turn" ... a timer or clock can be a helpful rule-keeper and visual aid. ... Can I have a turn? ... It's OK to be upset when you have to wait. Waiting is hard! ... When struggles happen, it's important to acknowledge how both children feel. ... Keep practicing"
- [Helping Kids Learn to Take Turns and Practice Waiting](https://www.pbs.org/parents/thrive/helping-kids-learn-to-take-turns-and-practice-waiting) · PBS KIDS for Parents (Samantha Gratton), 4 Aug 2023 · read 2026-10-29
  - supports: Waiting is hard, a visual countdown timer helps, something else to do while waiting helps, and talking about what comes next comforts a child and says who has the next turn.
  - quote: "Waiting is hard (even for grown-ups)! ... You can also use a timer that shows a countdown for taking turns! ... gave each person something else to do while they waited! ... Talking about what comes next can comfort your child and remind them who gets the next turn."
- [Social Development in Preschoolers: Learning How to Share & Cooperate](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/preschool/Pages/Social-Development-in-Preschoolers.aspx) · HealthyChildren.org (American Academy of Pediatrics), last updated 5 Dec 2024 · read 2026-10-29
  - supports: Preschoolers can settle a dispute by taking turns or trading toys, and a reminder that each child gets an equal turn helps.
  - quote: "Three-year-olds are able to work out solutions to disputes by taking turns or trading toys. ... Remind them that when two children are sharing a toy, each gets an equal turn."
