  - supports: Hear and support the child's feeling instead of dismissing it, do not judge the reaction, plan the day together for a sense of control, and point out the skills the child already has. A general page about disappointment, applied to a birthday that does not go to plan.
  - quote: "Instead, emphasize that you hear your child and express your support for how they're handling this. ... Let them know that even though this situation is different, they can use some of the same skills to get through it."
