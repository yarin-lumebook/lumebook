- [Restraint collapse: Why kids fall apart after school](https://understood.org/en/articles/why-some-kids-fall-when-they-get-home-from-school) · Understood.org (published 9 Feb 2026, last modified 5 Aug 2026) · read 2026-10-29
  - supports: A snack first, do not open with negative questions, and connect about the day before demands such as homework or chores. General advice for school age children, not specific to first grade.
  - quote: "A snack can help your child get back on an even keel. ... Don't ask about negatives first. ... the need to share or connect about their day"
- [How to Cope With After-School Meltdowns](https://www.pbs.org/parents/thrive/how-to-cope-with-after-school-meltdowns) · PBS Parents (Sep 14, 2016) · read 2026-10-29
  - supports: School is tiring, hunger and thirst after school, questions later, time to decompress, a warm greeting, time to play and run, and listening without fixing. Page about school age children in general.
  - quote: "Going to school can be completely exhausting for many kids. ... Many children come home starving and dehydrated, even if they communicate otherwise. ... Get ahead of the hunger crash by planning the after school snacks in advance. ... Leave the questions for later. ... most kids need time to decompress and zone out before discussing the daily play-by-play. ... A simple greeting and a hug or high-five is a great way to connect and provide emotional space from the school day. ... Many of them lack sufficient time to play, run and regroup throughout the day. ... Let your child speak without attempting to fix any identified problems."
- [How to Help Your Child Develop Empathy](https://www.zerotothree.org/resource/how-to-help-your-child-develop-empathy/) · ZERO TO THREE (page dated 1 Feb 2016) · read 2026-10-29
  - supports: Reading stories about feelings is one way Zero To Three suggests to help a child with feelings. The page is about empathy, not about school; used only as support for reading stories about a hard moment.
  - quote: "Read stories about feelings."
