## 18 months
- Says "no" to almost everything: a new grasp of choice and of being their own person; patience, humour, steady limits (H-18c; Z-def: from about 18 months they see themselves as separate).
- Runs off, then clings: checks back with you as a safe base (H-18c; C18 lists moving away while checking you are close).
- Tantrums: expected at this age, should get shorter and rarer; distraction, or let them calm down (C18; Z-tant: self-stopping lags behind understanding).
- Separation anxiety starting now: many start at 15 to 18 months; worse when hungry, tired, sick; short goodbye ritual, come back when you said (H-sep).
- Grabs toys, won't share: sharing has no real meaning yet; put treasured toys away (H-soc1, 2009).
- Picky eating: independence through food is normal, appetite drops as growth slows, a new food can take 10+ tastes (H-18c, H-picky).
- Red flags: missed CDC milestones or lost skills (by 18 months: trying 3+ words besides mama/dada, walking without holding on, pointing to show) (C18); not pointing, copying, pretending, or looking at you when something new happens (H-18c).

## 2 years
- Tantrums and fast mood swings: little control over emotional impulses; choices, redirection, praise work better than punishment; tantrums peak at 1 to 3 and ease after 3 (H-emo2, H-24c, H-tant).
- Says "no" to everything: peaks at 2 to 3; name the feeling, give real choices, don't end a request with "okay?" (Z-def).
- Plays next to other kids, won't share: they don't yet know how to share or solve conflicts; show turns and words (C2).
- Hits, bites, kicks when stopped: little impulse control, few words for anger; short calm line, attention to the child who was hurt, never label "a biter" (H-emo2, H-agg, Z-bite).
- Better with the sitter or the gan teacher than at home: feels safe enough with you to test limits (H-emo2).
- Big protest at goodbyes: reassure you will come back, praise patience on return; easier by 3 (H-emo2).
- Eats a lot one day, almost nothing the next: the parent offers, the child decides how much (C2, H-24c).
- Afraid of loud sounds, animals, surprises: stay calm, reassure, show how you cope (H-24c).
- Not toilet trained at 2: most can't until 2 to 3, starting too early causes setbacks (C2).
- Red flags: missed milestones or lost skills (by 2: two words together, pointing to 2 body parts, noticing when someone is hurt) (C2); very passive or withdrawn, sad all the time (H-emo2); unusual aggression for more than a few weeks, injuries (H-agg).

## 30 months
- Lines up toys, wants the same story again and again: likes routine and order (H-30c).
- Plays alone or beside others: a milestone at this age (C30).
- "Look at me" for every new skill: CDC milestone (C30).

## 3 years
- Endless who, what, where, why questions: a milestone by 3 (C3).
- Mixes up pretend and real: often can't tell them apart at 3 to 4; avoid threats they may believe (H-m34).
- Sees monsters in shapes: emotional milestone at 3 to 4; reassure, never tease (H-m34, H-emoP).
- Imaginary friend: usually normal, helps practise social skills (H-imag).
- "All by myself", picks clothes and food: growing independence; let them try with you nearby, offer choices (H-3c).
- Shares sometimes, grabs other times: starts taking turns and swapping, adults still nudge (H-socP).
- Cries at gan drop-off: most calm within about 10 minutes (C3).
- Suddenly eats less: growth slows; small meals, no special substitute meals (H-3c).
- Rule repeated but not followed: self-control lags until about 3½ to 4 (Z-tant).
- Red flags: missed milestones (by 3: 2 back-and-forth turns of conversation, says first name, understood most of the time, calms within ~10 minutes) (C3); keeps clinging or crying every time parents leave, ignores other children, no fantasy play, no self-control at all when angry (H-m34).

## 4 years
- Nonstop why, what, when, how: the age of questions; keep answers short and simple (H-4c).
- Bumpy speech, repeats whole words: normal while language grows, most outgrow it; don't rush or say slow down (H-4c, H-stut).
- Tall tales: normal at 4 to 5; under about 6 fantasy and reality blur (H-m45, H-lie).
- Burst of energy, testing limits: offer choices between acceptable options (H-m45, H-4c, H-disc).
- Nightmares: common at this age; go quickly, reassure, a nightlight can help (H-4c, H-night).
- Eats very little: growth slows; pushing food adds calories they don't need (H-4c).
- Big feelings, easily hurt by words: model apologies and respect (H-4c).
- Red flags: missed milestones (by 4: sentences of 4+ words, tells about the day, pretend play, comforts others) (C4); stuttering lasting more than 6 months, repeating sounds ("b-b-baby"), tension or blinking while speaking (H-4c, H-stut); can't calm down when upset, frequent nightmares (H-4c).

## 5 years
- Makes up stories: much better language; a story with 2 events is a CDC milestone; under about 6 truth and fantasy blur; stay calm, model honesty (H-5c, C5, H-lie).
- Tests limits, adventurous: set limits together (H-5c).
- Friends suddenly matter more: growing independence, they learn from peers (H-5c, H-m45).
- Big feelings about starting school: prepare through stories and role play (H-5c, C5).
- Still wets the bed: about 15% of 5 and 7 year olds; not the child's fault, don't blame, most outgrow it (H-bed).
- Focuses 5 to 10 minutes: a CDC 5-year milestone (screens don't count) (C5).
- Red flags: missed milestones (by 5: conversation of 3+ turns, counts to 10, hops on one foot) (C5); extremely fearful or aggressive, can't separate without a major protest, can't stay with an activity 5 minutes, sad much of the time (H-m45); bedwetting that starts after 6+ months dry, or with pain (H-bed).

## Caveats
- CDC milestones are a floor: missing one is a reason to talk to the doctor, not a diagnosis. Write "worth checking with the doctor", never "it's fine".
- Short crying at drop-off that settles is on track at 3 (C3), but crying or clinging every time at 3 to 4 is on the AAP watch list (H-m34). Say it that way.
- No age-specific source for fear of the dark; nothing gives the "why" phase an exact start age.
- Pages from 2009 (H-m2, H-soc1, H-emoP) are old; prefer CDC where they differ.
